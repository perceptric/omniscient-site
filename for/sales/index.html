Questions, answered

Still curious? Book a demo and we'll walk you through it.

Do reps still need to log calls?

No. Calls on Zoom, Google Meet or Teams get notes on their own, and Otto suggests the deal updates.

Can managers see what changed?

Yes. Every change shows who made it, or that Otto suggested it and who approved it, with the quote behind it.

Does it replace our email?

Your email stays where it is. Emails sync from Gmail or Outlook, and drafts open in your inbox to send.

Can we keep our own stages?

Yes. Set your own stages and fields in a few clicks.
