# 14 Best AI CRMs in 2027

Most CRMs now have AI somewhere in the product. Many still wait for your team to type in what happened before the AI can help, so the pipeline stays as current as the last rep who logged a call.

An AI CRM does the work itself. It reads your email, calendar and calls, fills in records and runs agents that find leads, prep meetings and update deals. Founders, SDRs, account executives and sales managers use one to spend less time on admin.

The payoff is more time with buyers and a pipeline you can trust. 🤖

## What Is an AI CRM?

**An AI CRM is a CRM that fills in records, answers questions and does sales work for you by reading your email, calendar and calls with AI.**

Under the hood, it syncs each conversation to the right company, person and deal, then uses language models and agents to pull out facts, draft messages and suggest updates. Most run as a web app connected to Gmail or Outlook and your meeting tools, often with a Slack or Chrome add-on.

Most tools include:

- ✅ Automatic record updates
- ✅ Meeting notes and summaries
- ✅ AI email drafts
- ✅ Agents for routine sales work

The result is a CRM that keeps up with your deals while you sell.

## Why Do You Need an AI CRM for Sales?

A classic CRM works when reps have time to log every call. As deals and meetings pile up, records fall behind and managers forecast from memory.

An AI CRM solves this.

- **Less admin:** Notes, contacts and deal fields fill in from each conversation.
- **Faster prep:** Meeting briefs and account research arrive before each call.
- **Better follow-up:** AI drafts the next email from the thread for you to send.
- **Answers you can check:** Every answer and suggested update comes from one shared memory, with the quote behind it.
