# Attio Review (2027): Is It a Good CRM?

Attio is a flexible CRM that builds your records from email and calendar and lets you shape the data model around how your business works. Startups, investors and fast-moving B2B teams use it when a standard CRM feels too rigid.

We build Omniscient, an agentic CRM, so read this knowing which side we sit on. We also built our own Attio importer, so we've worked with Attio's records and objects up close. This review covers what Attio does well, how its pricing works in 2027 and who it fits.

## Is Attio a good CRM?

Yes. Attio is a great CRM for teams that like to design their own system, with custom objects, relationships and workflows that match how they sell. It fits startups and investors best, and its free plan covers a team of up to 3.

If you want a CRM that runs the whole sales motion for you on day one, from lead lists to closed deals, look at an agentic CRM like Omniscient too.

## What Attio is best at

Attio is best at bending to fit your process. You start with companies, people and deals, then add objects of your own, like investors, partners or products, and link them together. Records fill in from your inbox and calendar, so the CRM is useful from the first day.

![Attio homepage](tool:attio.com)

Attio has also moved toward agents. It now calls itself the CRM for agentic revenue, with [custom agents](https://attio.com/platform/custom-agents) you build for your own tasks and a [web agent](https://attio.com/platform/web-agent) that researches companies for you.

Here is what stands out when you use it:

- **The data model is yours.** Objects, attributes and relationships follow your business, so a fund can track deal flow and a SaaS team can track accounts in one tool.
- **Records fill in for you.** Email, calendar and enrichment add people, companies and details as you work.
- **Agents work on your data.** Custom agents and Ask Attio read the same records your team uses.
- **It is open to builders.** Every plan gets the API, webhooks, an MCP server and an App SDK, and Attio has iOS and Android apps.

## Attio key features

Here are the parts of Attio most teams use, with links to Attio's own pages for each one.

### Flexible data model

Attio's [data model](https://attio.com/platform/data) lets you sync and structure your data your way. Objects grow with the plan, from 3 on Free and 5 on Plus to 12 on Pro and any number on Ultra. Custom objects and relationships start on Pro, and extra objects past 12 cost $400 a month each on yearly billing.

Records grow with the plan too. Free holds 50,000 records, Plus 250,000, Pro 1,000,000 and Ultra 3,000,000.

### Email and calendar sync with enrichment

Every plan syncs email and calendar. Each user connects 1 account on Free and Plus, 2 on Pro and 3 or more on Ultra. [Enrichment](https://attio.com/platform/enrichment) fills in company and person details on every record, so you spend less time typing.

From Plus up, Attio writes summaries and labels for your emails. This helps a busy rep see what a thread is about before opening it.

### Ask Attio and agents

[Ask Attio](https://attio.com/platform/ask) answers questions about your data and helps you create lists and reports with AI. Custom agents take on repeat jobs, like routing new leads or researching a company before a call.

Ask Attio runs on credits. Each user gets a monthly allowance, and the workspace shares a pool for agents and workflows. The pricing section below has the numbers.

### Call Intelligence

[Call Intelligence](https://attio.com/platform/call-intelligence) records and analyzes your meetings. It turns calls into summaries linked to the right people, companies and deals. Call Intelligence starts on Pro.

### Sequences and email sending

Attio sends email from inside the CRM. [Sequences](https://attio.com/platform/sequences) send personal outreach in steps, and they start on Pro. Here is how much each plan sends:

| Attio plan | Emails a month per user | Bulk send at once | Sequences |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | 200 | 10 | No |
| Plus | 1,000 | 50 | No |
| Pro | Unlimited | 50 | Yes |
| Ultra | Unlimited | 50 | Yes |

### Workflows and reporting

[Workflows](https://attio.com/platform/workflows) run on every plan and use the workspace credit pool. They move records, send alerts and connect other tools when something changes.

[Reporting and forecasts](https://attio.com/platform/reporting) show your pipeline in real time. You get 3 reports on Free, 15 on Plus, 100 on Pro and as many as you like on Ultra. Plus adds funnels and segments, and Pro adds targets and trends.

## Attio pricing

Attio moved to a new pricing model. Each paid plan now has a monthly price for the workspace, which includes the first user, plus a price for each added user. Here is how [Attio's plans](https://attio.com/pricing) work on yearly billing:

| Plan | Workspace with first user | Each added user | Users | Monthly billing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | $0 | Up to 3 | Free |
| Plus | $40 | $35 | Up to 10 | $50, then $44 per user |
| Pro | $100 | $79 | Any number | $125, then $99 per user |
| Ultra | $1,000, with 5 users | $189 | Any number | Yearly billing |

![Attio's plans a month on yearly billing: Free $0 for up to 3 users; Plus $40 with the first user, then $35 per added user, up to 10 users; Pro $100 with the first user, then $79 per added user, adding sequences and Call Intelligence; Ultra $1,000 with 5 users, then $189 per added user, billed yearly](pricing.svg "Attio's plans on yearly billing")

Every new workspace starts with a 14-day free trial of Pro. When it ends, you can pick any plan, Free included.

Ultra is Attio's top plan. It adds SQL access through the API and MCP, a data connector, any number of objects and reports, and Governance, which other plans can add for $30 per user a month, billed yearly.

### What a team of 5 pays

The workspace price makes small teams easy to work out. A team of 5 on Plus pays $40 for the first user and $35 for each of the other 4, so $180 a month on yearly billing. On Pro, the same team pays $416 a month.

![What a team of 5 users pays a month on yearly billing: Attio Plus $180, Attio Pro $416 and Attio Ultra $1,000; Omniscient Starter $95, Pro $245 and Business $495](team-cost.svg "A team of 5 users, billed yearly")

Most teams that want sequences, Call Intelligence or custom objects price Attio at Pro, since all three start there.

### AI credits

Attio prices its AI with credits. Each user gets Ask Attio credits every month, and the workspace shares a second pool for agents and workflows.

| Plan | Ask Attio credits per user a month | Workspace credits a month |
|---|---|---|
| Free | 100 | 250 |
| Plus | 500 | 1,500 |
| Pro | 1,000 | 10,000 |
| Ultra | 2,500 | 20,000 |

![Attio workspace credits a month by plan: Free 250, Plus 1,500, Pro 10,000 and Ultra 20,000, plus 100 to 2,500 Ask Attio credits per user by plan](credits.svg "Attio's shared workspace credits by plan")

Paid plans can add more workspace credits. Packs start at 5,000 credits for $70 a month billed yearly, or $85 billed monthly, and go up to 50,000 credits for $475 a month billed yearly.

Credits make AI costs easy to see as usage grows. Teams that run many agents or workflows should check their usage in the trial, so they pick the right plan and pack.

### Attio for startups

Attio's [startup program](https://attio.com/startups) gives venture-backed startups 80% off the yearly Pro plan and credits for the first year, then 30% off the second year. It covers workspaces of up to 10 seats. You apply within 90 days of creating your workspace, and the startup must be under 5 years old. If you qualify, it is one of the best deals in the CRM market.

## Who Attio is for

Attio fits teams that want to own the shape of their CRM. Here is who gets the most from it:

- **Startups with a custom process.** Founders who want objects for partners, investors or products, and the free plan to start.
- **Investors and funds.** Deal flow, portfolio companies and relationships fit well in Attio's objects and lists.
- **Teams with builders.** Ops people or engineers who enjoy designing workflows and using the API, MCP and App SDK.
- **Teams on the go.** Reps who want iOS and Android apps for days away from the desk.

## When teams look beyond Attio

Teams usually look beyond Attio when they want something new from their CRM. Here are the most common goals we hear:

- **One CRM that runs the whole sales motion.** A CRM where the agent finds leads, preps meetings, recaps calls and keeps deals current from day one.
- **AI in the seat price.** One number to plan around, with no credit pools to watch.
- **Free seats for light users.** Teammates who only look things up or log notes at no cost.
- **Sequences and call notes on a smaller budget.** Both start on Attio Pro, at $100 a month with the first user and $79 for each added user, billed yearly.

Here are the tools we'd look at for each goal:

- **Pick Omniscient** if you want one agentic CRM for your whole sales motion, from finding leads to closing deals, with Otto doing the busy work.
- **Pick folk** if your deals start from your network and LinkedIn conversations.
- **Pick Twenty** if you love Attio's flexible objects and want open source software you can host yourself.
- **Pick HubSpot** if you send marketing email in bulk and want sales and marketing in one place.
- **Pick Affinity** if you run deal flow at a fund and want to see the warm path to each founder.
