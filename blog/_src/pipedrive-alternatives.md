## What Pipedrive does well

Before you switch, list what Pipedrive does for you today, since you'd be giving it up. These are the [Pipedrive](https://www.pipedrive.com/en) features worth weighing before you go:

- **Nova on every plan:** Its meeting assistant writes a brief before each call, records and transcribes it, then drafts CRM updates you approve in one click.
- **One inbox for leads:** Leads from web forms, chat and imports land in one place, labeled hot, warm or cold.
- **Reports from a prompt:** AI builds a report from a plain sentence, next to forecasting and goal tracking.
- **A big marketplace:** More than 500 integrations, plus an MCP server that lets ChatGPT and Claude work with your live account.

Honestly, if your team plans its week around a pipeline board and sells mostly by email, Pipedrive is a calm, simple place to work.

## Why teams look for a Pipedrive alternative

Pipedrive now sells four plans, Lite, Growth, Premium and Ultimate, and [every plan draws a line](https://support.pipedrive.com/en/article/new-pipedrive-plans) that a growing team runs into:

- Lite allows 2,500 leads and deals per user, and Growth allows 5,000.
- Sequences and the forecast view start on Growth, so a Lite team has neither.
- [LeadBooster](https://support.pipedrive.com/en/article/leadbooster-add-on), with a chatbot, live chat, Prospector and web forms, comes with Premium and Ultimate and is an add on below that.
- Premium allows up to 25 sequences, and Ultimate caps custom fields at 500, where the old Enterprise plan had no cap.
- Changing plans moves your account onto Pipedrive's latest list pricing, which matters if you signed up at an older price.

To bring LinkedIn into Pipedrive, you add a Marketplace app such as [LinkMatch](https://www.pipedrive.com/en/marketplace/app/link-match-for-pipedrive/6ceb1e3f05f1745a), a Chrome extension from Logical Pure Minds that imports LinkedIn profiles and syncs LinkedIn messages into Pipedrive.

## How to move from Pipedrive to another CRM

We'd run both CRMs side by side for the length of a trial, about two weeks, in this order:

```steps
1. Connect your inbox first: Add Gmail or Outlook and your calendar to the new CRM while Pipedrive keeps running.
2. Add missing email addresses: Our importer skips people without one and matches everyone else by email, so give an address to each person you plan to keep.
3. Export what won't come over: Save anything the new importer skips as spreadsheets. Ours leaves activities, products, Leads Inbox items and custom fields behind.
4. Import, then open a few deals: Move organizations, people, deals and notes, and confirm a handful of deals kept their stage, amount and owner.
5. Rebuild automations last: Recreate workflows and sequences once the data settles, so nothing fires on a half imported deal.
```

In Omniscient, each imported pipeline keeps its stages and their win probabilities, and Won and Lost stages are added where they're missing. A few more rules decide how your records land:

- Won and lost deals keep their status, and each owner is matched to a teammate by email.
- People already in your workspace are matched by email, and running the import again matches records by their Pipedrive ID, so nothing doubles up.
- Pipedrive organizations come over without a website, so each company takes its domain from its people's work emails, skipping personal addresses like Gmail.

Omniscient has no task list, so write each open deal's next activity into its Next step field once the import finishes. From then on, Otto suggests updates to your deals from meetings and emails. The hardest call we made was deciding when he may change a deal without asking you, and we settled on these rules:

- You set every field to off, suggest or auto.
- A value with no quote behind it, or one Otto is less than 60% sure of, is dropped before you see it.
- An auto field fills itself only when it's empty and Otto is at least 85% sure.
- A value someone on your team typed is never changed.
- Everything else waits for you to accept or skip, like an amount that moved on a call.
