# Attio Pricing: Plans and Costs Explained (2027)

Attio's paid plans start at $40 a month on yearly billing. That covers the workspace and its first user on Plus, and each added user costs $35. Most teams pick Pro, Attio's most popular plan, at $100 a month with the first user and $79 for each added user, billed yearly.

Attio is a flexible CRM that lets you shape objects, records and workflows around your business. This guide explains each plan, what a real team pays, what costs extra and how to pay less.

## Attio Free

Free costs $0 for up to 3 users. It is a real CRM for a small team, with:

- Email and calendar sync, with 1 account per user.
- 3 objects and up to 50,000 records.
- 200 emails a month per user, and bulk sends of up to 10 at once.
- 3 reports.
- 100 Ask Attio credits per user and 250 workspace credits a month.
- The API, webhooks, the MCP server, the App SDK and mobile apps.

Free is a great place to start a CRM for a founder or a two-person team.

## Attio Plus

Plus costs $40 a month for the workspace and first user, and $35 for each added user, billed yearly. On monthly billing it costs $50, then $44 per user. Plus fits teams of up to 10 users, and it adds:

- 5 objects and up to 250,000 records.
- 1,000 emails a month per user, and bulk sends of up to 50.
- 15 reports, with funnels and segments.
- Email summaries and labels.
- Private lists.
- 500 Ask Attio credits per user and 1,500 workspace credits a month.

Plus suits a small team that wants more room than Free and doesn't need sequences or call recording yet.

## Attio Pro

Pro costs $100 a month for the workspace and first user, and $79 for each added user, billed yearly. On monthly billing it costs $125, then $99 per user. Pro has no user limit, and it is where most of Attio's sales features live:

- **Custom objects and relationships:** up to 12 objects, then $400 a month for each extra object on yearly billing.
- **Sequences:** personal outreach in steps, with unlimited emails per user.
- **Call Intelligence:** records and analyzes your meetings.
- **More reporting:** 100 reports, with targets and trends.
- **Rules and teams:** 8 rules per object and up to 10 teams.
- **More accounts:** 2 email and calendar accounts per user.
- **More AI:** 1,000 Ask Attio credits per user and 10,000 workspace credits a month.
- **Records:** up to 1,000,000, then $240 a month per extra million on yearly billing.

If you need sequences, call recording or custom objects, price Attio at Pro, since all three start here.

## Attio Ultra

Ultra costs $1,000 a month with 5 users included, and $189 for each added user. It is billed yearly. Ultra is built for larger teams with more data and stricter controls:

- Any number of objects and reports.
- Up to 3,000,000 records.
- SQL access through the API and MCP.
- A data connector.
- Governance, which adds single sign-on, user provisioning, audit logs and data controls.
- 2,500 Ask Attio credits per user and 20,000 workspace credits a month.
- 3 or more email and calendar accounts per user.

## What a team of 5 pays

The workspace price makes the math easy. Take a team of 5 on yearly billing:

1. **Plus:** $40 for the first user, plus 4 users at $35, comes to $180 a month.
2. **Pro:** $100 for the first user, plus 4 users at $79, comes to $416 a month.
3. **Ultra:** $1,000 a month, since 5 users are included.

On monthly billing, the same team pays $226 a month on Plus and $521 on Pro.

## What costs extra

Most teams pay the plan price and nothing more. These are the extras to know about:

- **Workspace credits:** Paid plans can add credit packs. Packs cost $70 a month for 5,000 credits, $120 for 10,000, $260 for 25,000 and $475 for 50,000, on yearly billing. On monthly billing they cost $85, $150, $330 and $595.
- **Extra objects:** On Pro, each object past 12 costs $400 a month on yearly billing, or $500 monthly.
- **Extra records:** On Pro and Ultra, each extra million records costs $240 a month on yearly billing, or $300 monthly.
- **Governance:** Pro teams can add it for $30 per user a month, billed yearly. Ultra includes it.

### How Attio's AI credits work

Attio prices its AI with two kinds of credits. Seat credits belong to each user and pay for Ask Attio. Workspace credits are shared and pay for workflows, AI attributes and agents.

| Plan | Ask Attio credits per user a month | Workspace credits a month |
|---|---|---|
| Free | 100 | 250 |
| Plus | 500 | 1,500 |
| Pro | 1,000 | 10,000 |
| Ultra | 2,500 | 20,000 |

![Attio workspace credits a month by plan: Free 250, Plus 1,500, Pro 10,000 and Ultra 20,000, plus 100 to 2,500 Ask Attio credits per user by plan](credits.svg "Attio's shared workspace credits by plan")

A few things help you plan:

- Ask Attio uses seat credits first, then workspace credits, unless an admin sets a limit.
- Record summaries are included on paid plans and use no credits.
- Emails sent straight from a sequence use no credits.
- Admins can see usage under Workspace settings, then Billing, then Usage.

The [credits guide](https://attio.com/help/reference/workspace-settings-billing/manage-workspace-and-seat-credits) lists what each workflow step and AI action costs.

## How to pay less for Attio

Here are the easiest ways to lower your Attio bill:

1. **Pay yearly.** Yearly billing saves 20% on Plus and Pro.
2. **Apply to the startup program.** Venture-backed startups with up to 10 users get 80% off the yearly Pro plan and credits for the first year, then 30% off the second year. Apply within 90 days of creating your workspace on the [startup program page](https://attio.com/startups).
3. **Start on Free or Plus.** Move to Pro when you need sequences, call recording or custom objects.
4. **Watch credit usage in the trial.** Run your real workflows for 14 days, then buy a credit pack only if you need one.

Upgrades apply right away and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at your next renewal, so time a move down for the end of a billing period.

### How Omniscient compares on price

Omniscient is an agentic CRM that runs a B2B team's whole sales motion, from finding the next customer to closing the deal and keeping the account. Otto, its built-in agent, does the busy work at every step, and all of it runs on [Omniscience](/product/omniscience/), a shared memory of every customer conversation.

Here is how [Omniscient's plans](/pricing/) work, billed yearly:

- **Free:** $0 for teammates who only view or log data.
- **Starter:** $19 per seat a month.
- **Pro:** $49 per seat a month, with free viewer seats.

Paid seats include the CRM's AI, so there are no credit pools to watch. Monthly billing costs 20% more, call recording with a bot is a $25 per seat add-on, and teams of 21 seats or more get 10% off. Omniscient has no bulk marketing email, and the best parts start on Pro.

Attio is worth it for teams that want to shape the CRM around their own process. If you qualify for the startup program, Pro becomes one of the best deals in the CRM market.
