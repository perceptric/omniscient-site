# folk CRM Review (2027): Is It a Good CRM?

Many deals start in a LinkedIn message, a WhatsApp chat or an intro from a friend. That history sits across apps and inboxes, and copying it into a CRM by hand rarely happens.

folk is a CRM built for relationship-driven sales, with a Chrome extension that captures contacts from LinkedIn and the web, plus AI assistants that research leads and draft follow-ups. Agencies, founders, partnership teams and small sales teams use it to keep their network and their pipeline in one place. This review covers what folk is best at, its key features, its plans and limits, and who it fits.

By the end, you'll know whether folk fits your team and which plan to price. 🤝

## Is folk a good CRM?

**folk is a good CRM for teams whose deals start from their network, LinkedIn and messaging apps, and who want a simple tool to capture those contacts and follow up.** It fits agencies, founders, partnership teams and small sales teams that sell through relationships.

## What folk is best at

folk is best at turning your network into a pipeline. Its Chrome extension, folkX, saves people from LinkedIn and the web in one click, and email, calendar and WhatsApp sync fill in the history.

![folk homepage](tool:folk.app)

Here is what stands out when you use it:

- **Capture from anywhere.** folkX adds contacts from LinkedIn, Sales Navigator and other sites without copy and paste.
- **WhatsApp on every plan.** folk logs your direct WhatsApp chats from the last 12 months on each contact.
- **AI assistants included.** Research, Follow-up and Recap assistants come with every plan.
- **Simple to pick up.** Contacts are unlimited on every plan, and pipelines feel like a shared spreadsheet.
- **Trusted by many teams.** folk says more than 5,000 companies use it.

## folk key features

Here are the parts of folk that relationship-driven teams use most, with links to folk's own pages.

### folkX Chrome extension

The [folkX extension](https://www.folk.app/) works on LinkedIn, Sales Navigator, Instagram, X and other sites. It saves a person to folk with their profile details, so you can add them to a pipeline or a campaign from the page you are on. It comes with every plan.

![The folkX side panel open on a LinkedIn profile, showing the saved contact with Enrich, Open in folk, details, interactions and notes](folk-folkx.webp "folkX on a LinkedIn profile. Image: folk help center")

### Email, calendar and WhatsApp sync

folk syncs Google and Outlook email and calendars, plus WhatsApp. Each member gets 1 synced account on Standard and 5 on Premium, and WhatsApp counts as one synced account. Standard keeps the last 30 interactions per contact, while Premium and Enterprise keep the full history.

![A folk contact list with a Past interactions panel showing a synced WhatsApp conversation, message by message with times](folk-whatsapp.webp "WhatsApp chats logged on a contact. Image: folk help center")

### Pipelines and deals

Every plan includes pipeline management, so you can track people and companies by stage. Premium adds custom objects and deals, which helps teams that track more than one deal per company.

![A folk sales pipeline board with Demo booked and In discussion columns, contact cards with company, tags and owner, and Add people, Email all and Enrich all buttons](folk-pipeline.webp "A folk pipeline board. Image: folk help center")

### AI assistants

folk includes AI assistants on every plan:

1. **Research Assistant:** looks up details about a contact or company.
2. **Follow-up Assistant:** suggests who to follow up with and drafts the message.
3. **Recap Assistant:** sums up your history with a contact.
4. **Workflow Assistant:** runs automated steps, with 1,500 runs a month on Standard and 10,000 on Premium.

AI fields fill in columns for you, with 2,000 a month on Standard and 5,000 on Premium.

![The folk assistants page with the Follow-up assistant, marked Active, and the Research assistant, each with a short description](folk-assistants.webp "folk assistants. Image: folk help center")

![The Recap assistant settings in folk, with a toggle and custom instructions for how recaps are written](folk-recap.webp "Recap assistant settings. Image: folk help center")

### Email campaigns and sequences

Every plan sends email campaigns with templates, LinkedIn and Gmail templates and campaign analytics. Premium adds multi-step sequences with waits and conditions. Members send up to 2,000 messages a month on Standard and 5,000 on Premium.

![A folk email sequence step with a Wait 3 days delay, a From sender, 3 recipients sent one by one, templates and a Stop if replies toggle](folk-sequence.webp "Building an email sequence. Image: folk help center")

### Enrichment, calls and mobile

folk finds work emails for your contacts, with 500 a month on Standard and 1,000 on Premium. Every plan also includes phone call recording with transcripts and summaries, a mobile app that scans business cards and records voice notes, and an MCP server for AI assistants.

![The folk Calls view listing recorded phone calls with contact, summary, duration and date](folk-calls.webp "Recorded calls in folk. Image: folk help center")

## Getting started with folk

folk is quick to set up, since most of your contacts already live in your inbox and on LinkedIn. Here is the usual path for a new team:

```steps
1. Connect your inbox: Sync Gmail or Outlook and your calendar, so past emails and meetings land on each contact.
2. Install folkX: Add the Chrome extension and save people from LinkedIn as you browse.
3. Sync WhatsApp: Bring in your direct chats from the last 12 months.
4. Build a pipeline: Name your stages and add the people and companies you're working with.
5. Invite your team: Add members to the workspace. Premium adds workspace roles for owners, admins and members.
```

Most small teams can work in folk on day one, then add campaigns and assistants as they grow.
