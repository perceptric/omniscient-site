# 15 Best LinkedIn Outreach Tools With a CRM in 2026

Your team sends LinkedIn invitations from one tool and keeps deals in another, so every reply that matters gets copied into the CRM by hand, or never gets there at all. We build Omniscient, which sends LinkedIn outreach and runs the CRM it lands in. We've spent a long time on the join between sending a message and remembering what came of it, and that join is what we judge every tool on.

If you're short on time, here's where we'd start:

- Pick Omniscient if you want LinkedIn outreach you approve and a CRM that fills itself in from email and calendar.

- Pick Breakcold, folk or Salesflare if your team writes its LinkedIn messages by hand and wants the CRM to keep up.
- Pick HubSpot Sales Hub if HubSpot is already your CRM and LinkedIn steps as reminders are enough.
- Pick La Growth Machine or lemlist if you keep HubSpot or Pipedrive and want automated LinkedIn steps synced into it.

## Four kinds of LinkedIn outreach tool with a CRM

Every tool here says CRM somewhere, and the word means something different in each. We sort them into four kinds.

![Four kinds of LinkedIn outreach tool with a CRM: outreach and CRM in one product (Omniscient, NetHunt CRM), automation with a light CRM of tags, notes and stages (Kanbox, Meet Alfred, Linked Helper, Dripify, Waalaxy), a CRM that holds LinkedIn contacts with the record first (Breakcold, folk, Salesflare, HubSpot Sales Hub), and outreach synced into the CRM you keep (La Growth Machine, lemlist, HeyReach, Closely)](/assets/blog/linkedin-outreach-tools-with-crm/three-kinds.svg)

- Outreach and CRM in one product. The tool sends your invitations and messages, and the same record holds deals, email and meetings. Omniscient and NetHunt CRM work this way.
- LinkedIn automation with a light CRM. You get tags, notes or pipeline stages on each LinkedIn contact, while a deal pipeline with email history usually lives in another tool. Kanbox, Meet Alfred, Linked Helper, Dripify and Waalaxy sit here.
- A CRM that holds LinkedIn contacts. The record comes first, LinkedIn contacts and conversations flow into it, and your team does most of the LinkedIn sending itself. Breakcold, folk, Salesflare and HubSpot Sales Hub work like this.
- Outreach synced into the CRM you keep. The tool sends on LinkedIn and pushes contacts and messages into HubSpot, Salesforce or Pipedrive. La Growth Machine, lemlist, HeyReach and Closely take this route.

Which kind you need depends on where your deals close. When most deals close in LinkedIn messages and one call, a light CRM can be enough. When buyers move on to email and meetings, you'll want the first kind, or a full CRM with an outreach tool synced into it.

## How we chose these LinkedIn outreach tools

A LinkedIn outreach tool sends invitations and messages from your account and follows up when people accept. For this list we kept tools that also store contacts, notes or deals, plus the outreach tools that sync every message into the CRM you already run.

We judged the CRM side on the questions we had to answer when we joined our own sending engine to our CRM:

- Does a LinkedIn lead become one record, or a second copy of someone you already know?
- Does a reply stop that person's outreach in every campaign?
- Do email and meetings land on the same record as the LinkedIn thread?
- Is there a pipeline with deals and stages?
- How is sending paced, and who approves what goes out?

Honestly, every tool here that sends for you carries some risk on LinkedIn, ours included. Section 8.2 of the [LinkedIn User Agreement](https://www.linkedin.com/legal/user-agreement) bans "bots or other unauthorized automated methods," and members who use them "risk having their accounts restricted or shut down" ([LinkedIn Help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1341387)).

## How a LinkedIn lead becomes one CRM record

Surprisingly, much of our work on joining outreach to the CRM went into deciding when two records are the same person. A campaign reaches people who may already be in your CRM from a demo request or last year's import.

If the tool makes a fresh contact for every lead, your reps end up with two records and half the history on each. Our matching rules exist to prevent that.

![Five steps for how a LinkedIn lead becomes one CRM record in Omniscient: saved to a list or a campaign, matched by profile ID and then LinkedIn address, company matched by web domain, typed fields left alone, and every touch on one timeline, with emails and meetings joining once the record has the person's email address](/assets/blog/linkedin-outreach-tools-with-crm/one-record.svg)

These are the rules we settled on, and the same questions are worth putting to any vendor:

- Search results stay search results until you keep one, so a Scout list of 500 leads adds only the people you choose.
- We match a person first by the ID our data source gives their profile, then by LinkedIn address, because two people can share a name.
- We create a company only once its web domain is known, so firms with similar names stay apart.
- New data fills empty fields and leaves anything a teammate typed exactly as it was.

    A CRM in the tool: Which kind?

      A simple LinkedIn pipeline: Kanbox
      LinkedIn, email and X in one tool: Meet Alfred
    The lowest price per account: Linked Helper

```steps
1. Add a lead you already have: Put someone from your CRM in a test campaign and see whether the tool finds their existing record.
2. Reply from a second account: Answer the first message and confirm the sequence stops for that person in every campaign.
3. Look for email and meetings: Email the same person and book a call, then see whether both appear next to the LinkedIn thread.
4. Set your pacing: Use a daily range, working hours in your time zone and a weekly cap, and confirm nothing goes out overnight.
5. Decide who approves messages: Choose whether someone reviews each message or approves the whole campaign at launch.
6. Turn a reply into a deal: Create an opportunity from a reply and confirm its stage and amount land in your pipeline.
```
