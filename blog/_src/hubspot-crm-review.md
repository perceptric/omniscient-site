# HubSpot CRM Review (2027): Is It a Good CRM?

Many growing teams run sales in one tool, marketing in another and support in a third. Each tool shows part of the customer, and the full story sits somewhere in between.

HubSpot is a CRM platform that puts sales, marketing, service and website tools on one shared customer database. Founders start on the free CRM, and sales reps, marketers and service teams move up to Sales Hub and the other hubs as they grow. This review covers what HubSpot is best at, its key features, Sales Hub pricing, HubSpot Credits and who it fits.

By the end, you'll know which HubSpot plan fits your team and what to try if you want more. 🎯

## Is HubSpot a good CRM?

**HubSpot is a good CRM for teams that want sales and marketing on one database, with a free CRM to start and a clear path to grow.** It fits small businesses best when marketing email, landing pages and sales all run in one place.

## What HubSpot is best at

HubSpot is best at putting every team that talks to customers on one database. Sales Hub is the part built for sales teams, and it sits next to Marketing Hub, Service Hub and HubSpot's website tools.

![HubSpot homepage](tool:hubspot.com)

Here is what stands out when you use it:

- **A real free CRM.** The [free CRM](https://www.hubspot.com/products/crm) has no end date and needs no credit card.
- **Sales and marketing together.** Leads from forms, emails and ads land on the same records your reps work.
- **Room to grow.** You can start free and add seats, hubs and features as the team grows.
- **A huge app marketplace.** HubSpot connects to more than 2,000 other tools.
- **Agents across the platform.** HubSpot's Agent Hub runs AI agents for prospecting, data and customer support.

## HubSpot key features

Here are the parts of HubSpot that sales teams use most, with links to HubSpot's own pages.

### The free CRM

The free CRM covers up to 2 users. It includes:

- Contact, company and deal management.
- Deal pipelines.
- Email tracking, templates and scheduling.
- Meeting scheduling, with 1 personal meeting link.
- Live chat and document sharing.
- Reporting dashboards, up to 10.
- Breeze Assistant, HubSpot's AI helper.

### Pipelines and deal management

[Sales Hub](https://www.hubspot.com/products/sales) helps reps move deals from first touch to close. It adds deal scoring, deal pipelines, AI guided selling and deal plans. The number of pipelines grows with the plan: up to 15 on Starter, 100 on Professional and 350 on Enterprise.

### Sequences and sales automation

Sequences send personal emails in steps and start on Professional. Professional covers 5,000 sequences per account and up to 500 sends per user a day. Enterprise raises that to 1,000 sends per user a day and lets workflows start a sequence.

Sales workflows grow with each plan:

1. **Starter:** up to 50 workflows, with some limits on triggers and actions.
2. **Professional:** up to 300 workflows.
3. **Enterprise:** up to 1,000 workflows, plus quote-based workflows and workflow health checks.

### Calling, notes and conversation intelligence

HubSpot has calling built in, with a power dialer, voicemail drops and calls logged to the CRM. Calling minutes grow from 500 a month on Starter to 3,000 on Professional and 12,000 on Enterprise.

HubSpot Notetaker records meetings, and call transcription and coaching come with Professional, at 750 hours per account a month, and Enterprise, at 1,500 hours. Conversation intelligence, with tracked terms, comes with Enterprise.

### Forecasting and reporting

Professional adds default and custom forecasting, plus up to 100 custom reports. Enterprise adds forecasting across team levels, up to 500 custom reports and deal journey analytics. Dashboards grow from 10 on Free to 30 on Starter, 75 on Professional and 100 on Enterprise.

### AI agents and Breeze

HubSpot's [Agent Hub](https://www.hubspot.com/products/artificial-intelligence) is one home for its AI agents, and it comes with every Starter, Professional and Enterprise edition. For sales teams, the main agents are:

- **Prospecting Agent:** watches buying signals and starts personal outreach.
- **Data Agent:** pulls insights from your CRM, calls and documents.
- **Agent Builder:** lets you build your own agents on your customer data.

Many agents run on HubSpot Credits. Breeze Assistant, the AI helper, comes with the free CRM too.

### HubSpot Credits

HubSpot Credits pay for power features and AI agents. Each paid plan includes a monthly amount: 500 on Starter, 3,000 on Professional and 5,000 on Enterprise. Custom agents you build with Agent Builder use credits too.

Teams that plan to run many agents should check their credit use during setup, so the bill matches the work.

### What to budget on Professional

Most sales teams that want sequences and forecasting land on Professional. Here is what to plan for:

1. **The seat price:** $90 to $100 per seat a month, by how you pay.
2. **Onboarding:** a one-time fee of $1,500.
3. **Credits:** 3,000 a month are included, and heavy agent use may need more.
4. **Quotes:** HubSpot's CPQ lives in Revenue Hub, which is sold on its own.

### HubSpot for Startups

[HubSpot for Startups](https://www.hubspot.com/startups) gives Seed and Series A founders 90% off the first year, plus discounts on AI credits as they grow. Seed means under $2 million raised, and Series A means over $2 million raised. If you qualify, apply before you buy.

## Who HubSpot is for

HubSpot fits teams that want one platform for every customer-facing team. Here is who gets the most from it:

- **Small businesses that run marketing and sales together.** Newsletters, landing pages, forms and the pipeline all share one database.
- **Teams that want to start free.** The free CRM covers 2 users with no end date.
- **Growing sales teams.** Sequences, forecasting and playbooks on Professional give managers more control.
- **Teams that sell by phone.** Built-in calling, a power dialer and call logging come with paid plans.
- **Companies with many tools.** More than 2,000 apps connect to HubSpot.
