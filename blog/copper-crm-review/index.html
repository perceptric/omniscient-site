Many service businesses live in Gmail and Google Calendar all day. Their client history sits in threads and calendar invites, and a separate CRM often feels like one more place to type.

Copper is a CRM built to work inside Google Workspace, made for relationship-focused service businesses. Agencies, consultants, financial advisors and small sales teams use it to track clients, deals and projects without leaving Gmail. This review covers what Copper is best at, its key features, its plans and limits, and who it fits.

By the end, you'll know whether Copper fits your team and which plan to price. 📬

Is Copper a good CRM?

Copper is a good CRM for service businesses that run their day in Google Workspace and want client relationships, deals and projects in one simple tool. It fits agencies, consultancies and small teams that want a CRM with no admin to set it up.

If you want a CRM that runs the whole sales motion for you on day one, from lead lists to closed deals, look at an agentic CRM like Omniscient too.

What Copper is best at

Copper is best at fitting into Google Workspace. It connects to your Google account and adds a Chrome extension, so contacts, emails, files and meetings flow into the CRM as you work.

Here is what stands out when you use it:

  • Built for Google Workspace. Gmail, Google Calendar, Contacts, Drive and Sheets sync with Copper, and the Chrome extension works right inside Gmail.
  • Easy to set up. Copper needs no scripting language and no dedicated admin.
  • Deals and projects together. Pipelines track the sale, and project management tracks the work after it closes.
  • Built for repeat business. An activity feed, rekindle emails and feedback follow-ups help you stay close to past clients.
  • Trusted by many teams. More than 30,000 companies use Copper.

Copper key features

Here are the parts of Copper that service teams use most, with links to Copper's own pages.

Google Workspace integration

Copper syncs Gmail email, contacts and files, Google Calendar and Google Contacts, and works with Google Drive, Google Sheets and Looker Studio. The Chrome extension shows client details next to each email, so you can add a contact or update a deal without switching tabs.

Pipelines and deal management

Drag-and-drop pipelines track each deal by stage, with flags and alerts that point out deals that need attention. Copper also tracks revenue and helps with contracts and proposals.

Projects and tasks

Copper includes project management on every plan, so the team can track client work after the deal closes. Tasks, reminders and task automation keep each step moving.

Email and automation

Copper includes email templates and open and click tracking, and Basic includes task automation. Professional adds workflow automation and bulk email, and Business adds email series, Copper's way to send a set of follow-up emails over time. Copper also includes an AI email template generator and an AI email rewriter.

Reporting

Professional adds reporting, with activity and pipeline reports. Business adds custom reports and multi-currency.

Integrations and mobile

Copper connects to tools such as Slack, Zendesk, DocuSign, QuickBooks, PandaDoc, Mailchimp, RingCentral and JustCall, and to more than 5,000 apps through Zapier. It also has an API and mobile apps for iOS and Android.

Getting started with Copper

Copper is quick to set up, since it reads from the Google account you already use. Here is the usual path for a new team:

  1. 01
    Sign in with Google

    Connect your Google Workspace account, so Gmail, Calendar and Contacts start syncing.

  2. 02
    Add the Chrome extension

    Install it to see client details and update deals right inside Gmail.

  3. 03
    Bring in your data

    Import contacts and deals from a spreadsheet, and export them any time.

  4. 04
    Set up your pipeline

    Name your deal stages and add custom fields, labels and owners for your process.

  5. 05
    Invite your team

    Add teammates and set their permissions. Copper also supports single sign-on with Okta.

Most small teams can work in Copper on day one, then add automations and reports as they grow.

Copper pricing

Here is how Copper's plans work:

Prices are from Copper's site in November 2026.

  • Basic: $23 per seat a month billed yearly, or $29 billed monthly, with up to 2,500 contacts.
  • Professional: $59 per seat a month billed yearly, or $69 billed monthly, with up to 15,000 contacts.
  • Business: $99 per seat a month billed yearly, or $134 billed monthly, with any number of contacts.

Copper has no free plan. Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial with no credit card, and yearly billing saves up to 26%. Prices don't include taxes and fees.

What a team of 5 pays

A team of 5 on yearly billing pays $115 a month on Basic, $295 on Professional and $495 on Business.

Most growing teams price Copper at Professional, since workflow automation, bulk email, reporting and integrations start there. Personal onboarding and premium support are offered to eligible Professional and Business accounts on yearly plans.

Who Copper is for

Copper fits service businesses that keep their client relationships in Google Workspace. Here is who gets the most from it:

  • Agencies and consultancies. Deals and client projects live in one place.
  • Financial services and professional firms. Long client relationships stay visible over time.
  • Small teams in Google Workspace. Copper works inside Gmail with no admin to set it up.
  • Teams that want repeat business. Rekindle emails and feedback follow-ups keep past clients close.

FAQs

How much does Copper CRM cost?

Copper CRM costs $23 per seat a month on Basic, billed yearly, or $29 billed monthly. Professional is $59 per seat a month billed yearly, or $69 monthly, and Business is $99 billed yearly, or $134 monthly. Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial with no credit card.

Does Copper CRM have a free plan?

No, Copper CRM has no free plan. Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial, and no credit card is required. After the trial, Basic is the lowest-cost plan at $23 per seat a month on yearly billing, with up to 2,500 contacts.

Does Copper CRM work with Gmail?

Yes, Copper CRM is built to work inside Google Workspace. It syncs Gmail email, contacts and files, Google Calendar and Google Contacts, and works with Google Drive and Sheets. Its Chrome extension shows client details next to each email in Gmail.

What are Copper CRM's contact limits?

Copper CRM's Basic plan holds up to 2,500 contacts, Professional up to 15,000 and Business any number. Teams with large client lists or plans to grow should price Professional or Business, since the contact limit rises with each plan.
