Pipedrive shows your team a clear pipeline board, and many small teams plan their week on it. What the board keeps out of view is everything around it: lead capture, sequences and room for more deals arrive plan by plan, and LinkedIn needs a Marketplace app.

A Pipedrive alternative is a sales CRM that keeps the pipeline view and adds what you want next, from lead lists to call notes. It syncs your inbox and calendar so every deal stays current. Founders, SDRs, account executives and agencies use one to sell with fewer tools.

The result is a pipeline that stays current with less typing. 🚀

What Is a Pipedrive Alternative?

A Pipedrive alternative is a sales CRM that tracks leads and deals through a pipeline by syncing your inbox, calendar and calls to each record.

You import your organizations, people, deals and notes, and the new CRM logs each email and meeting on the right deal as you work. Most run as a web app with mobile apps, and some add a Chrome extension or live inside Gmail.

Most tools include:

  • ✅ A drag-and-drop deal pipeline
  • ✅ Email and calendar sync
  • ✅ Follow-up reminders and workflows
  • ✅ Reports and forecasts

Some add a built-in agent that writes call notes and suggests deal updates for you to accept. The result is a board that reflects what buyers really said, with less time spent on data entry.

Why Do You Need a Pipedrive Alternative?

Pipedrive is a great fit for teams that plan their week around a pipeline board, and Nova, its meeting assistant, comes with every plan. Its four plans add room as a team grows: sequences and the forecast view start on Growth, and LeadBooster lead capture comes with Premium. See Pipedrive's pricing for current prices.

A Pipedrive alternative solves this.

  • Follow-ups from day one: Some tools include email workflows on their entry plan.
  • Lead capture included: Several find new leads or capture web forms without an add-on.
  • LinkedIn in the CRM: A few save LinkedIn profiles straight into the CRM from a browser extension.
  • One shared memory: Every email, meeting and call lands on the right company, person and deal, so your team and AI agents read from one place.

How do Pipedrive alternatives keep a pipeline up to date?
