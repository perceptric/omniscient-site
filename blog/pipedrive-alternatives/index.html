What Pipedrive does well

Before you switch, list what Pipedrive does for you today, since you'd be giving it up. These are the Pipedrive features worth weighing before you go:

  • Nova on every plan: Its meeting assistant writes a brief before each call, records and transcribes it, then drafts CRM updates you approve in one click.
  • One inbox for leads: Leads from web forms, chat and imports land in one place, labeled hot, warm or cold.
  • Reports from a prompt: AI builds a report from a plain sentence, next to forecasting and goal tracking.
  • A big marketplace: More than 500 integrations, plus an MCP server that lets ChatGPT and Claude work with your live account.

Honestly, if your team plans its week around a pipeline board and sells mostly by email, Pipedrive is a calm, simple place to work.

Why teams look for a Pipedrive alternative

Pipedrive now sells four plans, Lite, Growth, Premium and Ultimate, and every plan draws a line that a growing team runs into:

  • Lite allows 2,500 leads and deals per user, and Growth allows 5,000.
  • Sequences and the forecast view start on Growth, so a Lite team has neither.
  • LeadBooster, with a chatbot, live chat, Prospector and web forms, comes with Premium and Ultimate and is an add on below that.
  • Premium allows up to 25 sequences, and Ultimate caps custom fields at 500, where the old Enterprise plan had no cap.
  • Changing plans moves your account onto Pipedrive's latest list pricing, which matters if you signed up at an older price.

To bring LinkedIn into Pipedrive, you add a Marketplace app such as LinkMatch, a Chrome extension from Logical Pure Minds that imports LinkedIn profiles and syncs LinkedIn messages into Pipedrive.

13Less Annoying CRM

4.8/5 on Capterra

Less Annoying CRM is a simple CRM built for small businesses that have outgrown a spreadsheet but don't want a big CRM. It tracks contacts, tasks, pipelines and email for one flat price, and its support is free for everyone, even before you pay.

Key features

  • Daily follow up email: Every morning you get a list of the people you need to contact.
  • Email logging and notes: Synced emails and timestamped notes build a timeline for each contact.
  • Calendar sync: Connect a Google or Outlook calendar, or both, and meetings land on the contact's profile.
  • Unlimited pipelines and custom fields: Both come with the one plan, at no extra cost.
  • User permissions: Share notes with your team and restrict access where you need to.
  • HIPAA compliance: Healthcare businesses can sign a BAA at no extra cost.

Pros

  • One price, nothing to upgrade: Refreshingly, every feature sits on one plan, the opposite of Pipedrive's four tiers with a limit at each step.
  • Free human support: Email and phone support are free for everyone, including people on the trial.
  • Your data stays yours: You can export everything to Excel whenever you like, so you're never locked in.

Cons

  • Built to stay simple: It covers contacts, tasks, pipelines and email logging, so teams that want sequences or AI will outgrow it.
  • Soft contact ceiling: The team suggests staying under about 50,000 contacts and companies.

Price and plans

How to choose a Pipedrive alternative

Start from the reason you're leaving, then put the two CRMs that fit it best through a real week in their free trials. These are the tests we'd run:

  • Price the plan you'll need: Find the plan with the feature you're leaving for, like sequences or a dialer, and compare that price instead of the entry plan.
  • Connect one inbox: Connect your email and calendar on day one and see what the CRM fills in by Friday.
  • Follow one LinkedIn conversation: If you prospect on LinkedIn, send or log one conversation and see where the reply ends up.
  • Ask about migration help: Nutshell moves your data during the trial, Salesflare on Enterprise, monday CRM on Ultimate, and we do it for you at Omniscient.

  4. 04

  5. 05
    Rebuild automations last

    Recreate workflows and sequences once the data settles, so nothing fires on a half imported deal.

In Omniscient, each imported pipeline keeps its stages and their win probabilities, and Won and Lost stages are added where they're missing. A few more rules decide how your records land:

  • Won and lost deals keep their status, and each owner is matched to a teammate by email.
  • People already in your workspace are matched by email, and running the import again matches records by their Pipedrive ID, so nothing doubles up.
  • Pipedrive organizations come over without a website, so each company takes its domain from its people's work emails, skipping personal addresses like Gmail.

Omniscient has no task list, so write each open deal's next activity into its Next step field once the import finishes. From then on, Otto suggests updates to your deals from meetings and emails. The hardest call we made was deciding when he may change a deal without asking you, and we settled on these rules:

  • You set every field to off, suggest or auto.
  • A value with no quote behind it, or one Otto is less than 60% sure of, is dropped before you see it.
  • An auto field fills itself only when it's empty and Otto is at least 85% sure.
  • A value someone on your team typed is never changed.
  • Everything else waits for you to accept or skip, like an amount that moved on a call.

FAQs

What is the best alternative to Pipedrive?

Pick by your reason for leaving. Omniscient fits small teams that prospect on LinkedIn, Close fits teams that sell by phone, and HubSpot fits teams that want a free CRM with room for marketing.

Is there a free alternative to Pipedrive?

Yes. HubSpot's free CRM covers up to 2 users, Zoho CRM's free edition covers 3 users, and Attio's free plan covers up to 3 seats.

Is there a cheaper alternative to Pipedrive?

On yearly billing, Freshsales Growth costs $9 per user a month, Nutshell Foundation $13 and Zoho CRM Standard $14. Less Annoying CRM costs $15 per user a month for everything. Compare each with Pipedrive on the plan your team would really need.

What is a simpler alternative to Pipedrive?

Less Annoying CRM has one plan with unlimited pipelines and free phone support. If your team lives in Gmail, Streak and Copper keep the pipeline inside your inbox.

Does Pipedrive have LinkedIn automation?

Pipedrive connects to LinkedIn through Marketplace apps such as LinkMatch, which imports LinkedIn profiles and syncs LinkedIn messages. If you want LinkedIn outreach inside the CRM itself, Omniscient sends it, with every message approved before it goes out.

Can I move my data from Pipedrive to Omniscient?

Yes. Our importer brings over organizations, people, pipelines, deals and notes, or we migrate them for you from an export. Save activities, leads and custom fields as spreadsheets first.
