We build the LinkedIn sending engine inside Omniscient, and plenty of Expandi's safety choices match ours, like sending only inside your working hours and warming up a new profile slowly. We'll point out where the two differ.

What Expandi does well

Expandi built its Business plan around account safety, and the rest of the plan holds up too:

  • Business costs $99 a month per seat, or $79 a month billed yearly, with a dedicated IP based in your country and an automatic profile warmup.
  • Sequences mix connection requests, messages and email follow ups, with If/Action conditions that branch on what each person does.
  • Native integrations cover HubSpot, Salesforce and Pipedrive, plus Smartlead, webhooks, Zapier and API credentials.
  • Duplicate prevention, a blacklist, campaign A/B tests and a Global Smart Inbox with labels, tags and notes come with every seat.
  • The Agency plan starts at 10 seats, is priced on a call and adds white label, client reports, and roles and permissions.

Honestly, that's a strong list, and the safety side reads a lot like the engine we built. If a dedicated IP and an automatic warmup matter most to you and the price works, staying with Expandi is a fair call.

Why teams look for an Expandi alternative

Most of the reasons come down to how Expandi is priced and where your replies end up:

  • Each seat runs one LinkedIn account at a time, so every rep needs a Business seat until you reach the Agency plan's 10 seats.
  • The free trial lasts 7 days, while nine tools on this list give you 14 days or more.
  • Personalized images and GIFs cost extra on top of the plan, and video through Sendspark is a paid add on.
  • Replies land in Expandi's inbox and reach your CRM through a sync, so the CRM stays a second tool with its own bill.

These hit hardest for a small sales team, or for anyone who wants LinkedIn replies and deals in the same place.

The biggest difference is who does the varying. Expandi's guide to active times recommends "varying your hours each day," which leaves that chore with you. We had our engine move the hours on its own, because account safety shouldn't depend on someone remembering a daily task. It also drops to half pace for 3 days when under 20% of a week's invitations are accepted, once 20 have gone out.

LinkedIn keeps its exact invitation limit private. Hitting it can restrict your invitations, and LinkedIn says that restriction "typically lasts one week." Every weekly number on a vendor's site, ours included, is a ceiling that vendor picked for itself. Our guide to the LinkedIn connection request limit explains where the usual numbers come from.

The 13 best Expandi alternatives in 2026

01Omniscient

Omniscient is a CRM with LinkedIn outreach built for small B2B sales teams that want their prospecting and their deals in one place. Otto, our agent, drafts a message for each lead, and nothing goes out until your team approves it. Where Expandi syncs replies to a CRM you pay for separately, Omniscient is the CRM, and it fills itself in from your email and calendar.

Try Omniscient free →

Key features

  • Approval before sending: Otto drafts each invitation and message from the lead's role, posts and company news, and only approved text goes out.
  • A preview before go live: Before a campaign starts, you see how many people it reaches, who's left out and the reason for each one.
  • Automatic warmup: A new account starts at 5 invitations a day and adds 2 a day, and the warmup starts again after a reconnect or a restriction.
  • A profile view first: A profile view goes out 1 to 4 hours before each invitation, unless you turn that setting off.
  • Days that vary on their own: Each day's total lands between 80% and 100% of your limit, inside your working hours, with a lunch break.
  • Automatic slowdown: When acceptance drops under 20% or LinkedIn rate limits the account, sending falls to half pace for 3 days.
  • Scout lead search: Describe your buyers in a sentence, and Scout finds them and scores each lead out of 100 for fit and timing.
  • Inbox on the CRM record: Replies from every teammate's sender land in one Inbox, right next to the person's CRM record.

Pros

  • Outreach and CRM together: Replies and deals share one record, so your team never copies a LinkedIn thread into a second tool.
  • Safety that runs itself: Warmup, varied hours and slowdowns happen automatically, with no daily schedule for anyone to keep.
  • No double outreach: A person sits in one live campaign at a time, and a reply stops their outreach in every campaign.
  • Nothing sent twice: The engine confirms the relationship on LinkedIn before each invitation, and a send that times out is never retried on its own.

Cons

  • LinkedIn only: Campaigns have no email steps, so cold email still needs a tool of its own.
  • No dedicated IP: Omniscient doesn't give each sender its own IP address, which Expandi includes with every seat.
  • One sender per campaign: Each campaign sends from one LinkedIn account and there's no white label, so agencies rotating dozens of client senders will do better with HeyReach.
  • Newer product: Our LinkedIn outreach is newer than most tools here and has a shorter track record with large sales teams.

Price and plans

You can try Omniscient free for 14 days with no credit card. Prices are per seat, per month, billed yearly:

  • Free: $0 for teammates who only view or log data.
  • Starter: $19 per seat, per month.
  • Pro: $49 per seat, per month.
  • Business: $99 per seat, per month.

Monthly billing costs 20% more. The LinkedIn Outbound page and the CRM page show the rest.

02HeyReach

HeyReach is a LinkedIn automation tool built for agencies and sales teams that run outreach from many LinkedIn accounts at once. It rotates sending between your senders and puts every reply in one unified inbox. Interestingly, it publishes the flat agency price that Expandi only shares on a call, which makes budgeting for 25 client accounts much simpler.

Key features

  • Sender rotation: Connect many LinkedIn accounts and let HeyReach spread the sending between them automatically.
  • Unified Inbox: Answer every sender's LinkedIn messages from one place, send voice notes from your desktop and reply for a colleague.
  • LinkedIn steps with logic: Connection requests, messages, voice notes and profile views run in sequences with "If Connected" branches.
  • Email through partners: Native Instantly, Smartlead and EmailBison integrations add email, and HeyReach finds verified addresses for you.
  • HubSpot sync: Every connection, message and reply shows up on your HubSpot contacts with no manual logging.
  • API, webhooks and MCP: Build your own workflows, or let Claude or ChatGPT run campaigns through the MCP server.
  • Proxies on Growth: Each sender on the Growth plan gets a dedicated residential proxy.

Pros

  • Flat agency pricing: $999 a month covers 25 senders, with white label and a dedicated Slack channel included.
  • Free extra users: Teammates, clients and VAs join at no cost, because HeyReach bills only for senders.
  • Generous trial: 14 days with no card and up to 3 LinkedIn accounts, or 30 days on request for Agency and Unlimited.

Cons

  • No email of its own: Email runs through Instantly, Smartlead or EmailBison, which means a second tool and a second bill.
  • Your own proxies on agency plans: Agency and Unlimited ask you to supply proxies, while Growth includes them.
  • Narrow CRM sync: HubSpot, Attio and Breakcold connect directly, and Salesforce or Pipedrive go through Zapier, Make, n8n or the API.

Price and plans

You can try HeyReach free for 14 days with no credit card and up to 3 LinkedIn accounts.

  • Growth: $79 per sender, per month, or $64 billed yearly.
  • Agency: $999 a month for 25 senders, with a 50 sender option.
  • Unlimited: $2,999 a month for unlimited senders.

Our guide to LinkedIn automation tools for agencies compares the agency plans side by side, and HeyReach vs Expandi goes deeper on these two.

03Skylead

4.8/5 on Capterra

Skylead is a LinkedIn automation and cold email tool built for sales teams and agencies that want both channels inside one seat. Each seat connects unlimited email accounts with warmup and sends up to 100,000 emails a month, for about the price of an Expandi seat. That makes it the closest swap for a team where cold email matters as much as LinkedIn.

Key features

  • Smart sequences: If/else conditions move each lead between LinkedIn and email steps based on what that person does.
  • LinkedIn actions: Connection requests, messages and InMails sit next to view and follow steps that warm a lead up again.
  • Unlimited email accounts: Connect as many mailboxes as you like, warm them all up and send up to 100,000 emails a month.
  • Email finder and verifier: Find and verify addresses inside Skylead before an email step ever goes out.
  • Real time CRM sync: HubSpot, Pipedrive and Salesforce stay up to date while campaigns run, alongside webhooks and an API.
  • Image and GIF personalization: Unlimited personalized images and GIFs come with the All-in-one plan.

Pros

  • Email without a second tool: The finder, warmup and sending all live in the same seat as your LinkedIn steps.
  • Extras Expandi charges for: Image and GIF personalization is included, where Expandi bills it on top of the plan.
  • Unlimited team members: Every plan lets you add as many teammates as you need.

Cons

  • Promotional agency price: Both Agency prices are a first year promotion, so ask what year two costs before you move clients over.
  • AI costs extra: The AI SDR and AI data enrichment are paid add ons on every plan.
  • Short trial: The free trial lasts 7 days, the same as Expandi's.

Price and plans

You can try Skylead free for 7 days.

  • All-in-one: $100 per seat, per month.
  • Agency: $999 a month for 25 seats, or $1,999 a month for unlimited seats, as a first year promotion.
  • Annual: priced on a call with their sales team, with 2 months free and a white label option.

04GetSales.io

4.7/5 on Capterra

GetSales.io is a LinkedIn and email outreach platform built for agencies that run campaigns on each client's own accounts. Every LinkedIn account runs on a managed proxy with a gradual ramp, which covers much of what Expandi's dedicated IP and warmup do. Personally, we'd keep its AI SDR on approval while you learn how it writes.

Key features

  • A workspace per client: Each client keeps its own LinkedIn and email accounts and its own inbox, walled off from your other clients.
  • Sign in links: Clients connect their own LinkedIn through a link, so their password and 2FA code never pass through you.
  • Managed ISP proxies: Every LinkedIn account runs on a managed proxy, with a gradual ramp and pacing built in.
  • Unibox: Replies from every account in a workspace land in one team inbox, each with its label and its next move.
  • AI SDR: Each reply gets one of 15 labels, and the drafted answer waits for your OK or sends on its own, scenario by scenario.
  • Email Outreach: A module with 100 inboxes, adaptive warmup and a dedicated server and IP for each client.
  • Free Playtables: Build and score lead tables for free with your own API keys before you pay for any sending.

Pros

  • One bill for both channels: An agency can drop its separate cold email tool and run LinkedIn and email from one app.
  • No proxy shopping: Every LinkedIn account comes with a managed proxy, so you never buy or set one up yourself.
  • Cheaper at scale: The price per LinkedIn account falls from $69 to $39 once you connect 5 accounts.

Cons

  • CRM sync still on the way: HubSpot, Pipedrive, Salesforce, folk and GoHighLevel are marked as coming, so replies reach a CRM through webhooks, the API or MCP for now.
  • White label needs volume: White label comes with the Business setup, which starts at $500 a month in resources.
  • Short trial: LinkedIn and email sending get a free trial of 7 days.

Price and plans

Playtables are free with your own API keys, and LinkedIn and email sending come with a free trial of 7 days.

  • LinkedIn accounts: $69 a month for 1 account, or $39 per account from 5 accounts.
  • Email Outreach: $59 a month for 100 inboxes.
  • Business: included from $500 a month in resources, with white label and a dedicated team chat.
  • Enterprise: included from $1,500 a month in resources, with migration and a monthly growth review.

05La Growth Machine

4.9/5 on Capterra

La Growth Machine is a multichannel outreach tool built for sales teams where each rep runs LinkedIn, email and calls from one identity. You pay per identity, meaning one person's set of accounts, and the channels you get depend on the plan. Frankly, we'd pick it over Expandi for reps who call and email as much as they message on LinkedIn.

Key features

  • One sequence across channels: LinkedIn invitations, messages and voice notes run next to emails, calls and X, depending on your plan.
  • AI voice messages: Turn text into LinkedIn voice notes, so reps don't have to record each one by hand.
  • Waterfall enrichment: Nine email providers and two verification tools work through each lead until a verified email turns up.
  • Multichannel inbox: From Pro up, LinkedIn and email replies land in one inbox.
  • Rotating inbox: Pro sends from up to 5 mailboxes per identity and Ultimate from up to 10, which spreads your email volume.
  • HubSpot and Pipedrive sync: Ultimate writes sent and replied messages to your CRM and can trigger your CRM workflows.
  • Mobile proxies: Campaigns run in the cloud behind dedicated 5G mobile proxies.

Pros

  • Email in every identity: Even Basic covers LinkedIn and email, with one sending mailbox per identity.
  • Free team members: Pro includes 25 team members at no extra cost, and Ultimate includes unlimited team members.
  • Smaller agency minimum: The Agency plan starts at 6 identities, against Expandi's 10 seats.
  • A full trial: 14 days with no card, covering every feature apart from custom sequences.

Cons

  • Channels gated by plan: Calls start on Pro, and X and CRM sync need Ultimate.
  • Basic caps identities: Basic allows up to 3 identities, so a growing team moves up to Pro.
  • Trial covers one identity: You can test only 1 identity until you upgrade.

Price and plans

You can try La Growth Machine free for 14 days with no credit card. Prices per identity, per month:

  • Basic: €60, or €50 billed yearly, for LinkedIn and email.
  • Pro: €120, or €100 billed yearly, adding calls and the multichannel inbox.

  • Agency: priced on a call with their sales team, with a minimum of 6 identities.

lemlist is a sales engagement platform built for teams whose outreach starts with email and adds LinkedIn, calls and SMS around it. It's our pick when email is your main channel and LinkedIn is the second touch. Budget for the Multichannel plan from day one, because the Email plan has no LinkedIn automation.

Key features

  • Lead database: Search 650M+ leads and find emails and phone numbers without leaving lemlist.
  • Deliverability tools: lemwarm, inbox rotation and deliverability alerts come with every plan.

  • lemAgent and MCP: AI agents research leads and write messages, and the MCP server connects your own AI assistant.

Pros

  • Deep email tooling: Warmup, inbox rotation, domain purchase and deliverability testing all live in one place.

  • Useful trial: 14 days of Multichannel features, apart from in app calls, with 200 credits included.

Cons

  • LinkedIn costs the higher tier: LinkedIn steps come only with Multichannel, which is priced per user.
  • Email first by design: If most of your replies come from LinkedIn, a tool built around LinkedIn will fit your day better.
  • Data costs extra: Enrichment credits and buying signals are paid add ons on top of your plan.

Price and plans

You can try lemlist free for 14 days on the Multichannel plan, with 200 credits included.

07SalesRobot

SalesRobot is a LinkedIn and cold email automation tool built for B2B sales teams and agencies that want cloud campaigns with voice notes, video and AI on the replies. Advanced is where most teams leaving Expandi would land, with unlimited campaigns and native CRM sync for less than an Expandi seat.

Key features

  • LinkedIn and email sequences: Campaigns mix LinkedIn steps and emails, with smart delays and conditions between them.
  • Voice and video messages: Send AI personalized voice notes and videos inside your LinkedIn sequences.
  • AI Appointment Setter: An AI agent handles the replies and works each conversation toward a booked meeting.
  • Native CRM sync: Advanced and Professional connect directly to HubSpot, Pipedrive and Salesforce.
  • Cloud sending: Campaigns run in the cloud, with dedicated local IP rotation and behavior that mimics a person.
  • Unified inbox: From Advanced up, LinkedIn and email replies share one inbox, with notes and tags on each lead.

Pros

  • Advanced covers most teams: Unlimited campaigns, full daily quotas, A/B tests and CRM sync for $79 a month per account.
  • No card for the trial: The 14 day trial takes no credit card, so nothing charges when it ends.
  • Professional for teams: At Expandi's price, Professional adds team management and stops two teammates from contacting the same lead.

Cons

  • Basic is for testing: One active campaign and 20 connection requests a day won't carry a real outbound program.
  • Email costs extra: Email automation adds $15 per email account a month on top of the LinkedIn plan.
  • Bold safety claims: SalesRobot puts the risk of a ban at 0.00001%, a number LinkedIn's private limits make impossible to verify, so we'd read it as marketing.
  • High daily ceiling: Advanced allows 75 connection requests a day, nearly twice the 40 our own engine allows, so set yours well below it.

Price and plans

You can try SalesRobot free for 14 days with no credit card. Prices per LinkedIn account:

  • Basic: $59 a month, or $39 billed yearly.
  • Advanced: $79 a month, or $59 billed yearly.
  • Professional: $99 a month, or $79 billed yearly.
  • Enterprise: priced on a call with their sales team.

Price and plans

10Dux-Soup

4.0/5 on Capterra

Dux-Soup is a LinkedIn automation tool built for sellers and agencies that want to choose where their campaigns run. Its STACKED app runs the same drip campaigns from your own computer or from Dux-Soup's cloud. STACKED cloud costs the same as Expandi's monthly Business price, and the local version costs much less as long as your machine stays on.

Key features

  • Local or cloud: STACKED local runs as a desktop app on Mac or Windows, and STACKED cloud runs around the clock on managed infrastructure.
  • Drip campaigns: Mix up to 12 actions, such as invitations, messages, InMails, visits, endorsements and post likes, with custom delays.
  • Reply detection: STACKED detects responses and pauses the campaign when someone replies.
  • Unified response inbox: Replies from LinkedIn, Sales Navigator and Recruiter land in one place.
  • Native integrations: Two way connections to Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Freshsales, HighLevel and Slack, plus Zapier and Make.
  • Five safety controls: Natural pauses, daily limits that ramp up, speed throttling, an auto snooze and a daily planner keep activity measured.

Pros

  • Choice of deployment: Start local to save money, then move to the cloud when campaigns need to run all day.
  • Wide CRM coverage: Salesforce, HubSpot and Pipedrive connect natively, the same three Expandi covers, plus Freshsales and HighLevel.
  • Agency friendly billing: Agencies get unlimited free agents and add or remove accounts with billing by the day.

Cons

  • LinkedIn only: Campaigns have no email steps, so cold email stays in a separate tool.
  • Local needs your computer: The cheaper local version stops whenever your computer sleeps or shuts down.
  • Safety details stay private: Dux-Soup keeps the specifics of its safety engineering confidential, so you can't line its limits up against Expandi's.

Price and plans

You can try STACKED free for 14 days. Prices per seat:

  • STACKED local: $55 a month, or $41.25 billed yearly.
  • STACKED cloud: $99 a month, or $74.17 billed yearly.

Teams of 10 or more seats get a discount.

Price and plans

How to choose an Expandi alternative

Start with whatever pushed you away from Expandi, then trial the two or three tools that fix it. These questions settle most choices:

  • Count your senders first: Per seat pricing suits a team of three, while 25 or more client accounts make flat plans like HeyReach Agency or Skylead Agency cheaper.
  • Decide where email lives: If cold email matters as much as LinkedIn, keep both in one sequence with Skylead, La Growth Machine, lemlist or Dripify.
  • Follow a reply: Ask where a LinkedIn reply lands and who copies it into your CRM, because native sync or a CRM like Omniscient removes that step.
  • Read the safety defaults: Look for a warmup that runs on its own, daily invitations under 40 and a pace that changes from day to day.
  • Use the trial on a real list: A 7 day trial leaves little time to see who accepts, so launch your test campaign on day one.

How to switch from Expandi without risking your account

A LinkedIn account brings its pending invitations and recent pace into the new tool, so the first week after a switch needs the most care. Here's the order we'd follow:

  1. 01
    Stop Expandi's campaigns first

    A removed seat stays active until your billing cycle ends, so pause every campaign yourself.

  2. 02
    Save who you've contacted

    Export everyone in your Expandi campaigns, so the new tool can skip people who already heard from you.

  3. 03
    Bring your blacklist

    Load it as your do not contact list before the first campaign, so it holds across every import.

  4. 04
    Remove the seat as well as the account

    Disconnecting a seat from a LinkedIn account doesn't cancel the seat, so take it off your subscription too.

  5. 05
    Restart slower than you ended

    Treat the first week like a warmup, even on an old account, and raise limits once acceptance holds above 20%.

  6. 06
    Test the reply path

    Have a colleague answer a test message and confirm it lands on the right CRM record.

FAQs

What is the best alternative to Expandi?

Pick by the reason you're leaving. Omniscient fits teams that want outreach and a CRM together, HeyReach, Skylead and GetSales.io fit agencies, La Growth Machine fits reps who also call, and Linked Helper costs the least per account.

Is there a cheaper alternative to Expandi?

Yes. Expandi's Business plan costs $99 a month per seat, or $79 billed yearly. Linked Helper starts at $15 per LinkedIn account a month, Waalaxy at $19 per user, Omniscient at $19 per seat billed yearly and Kanbox at $20 a month on Starter.

Which Expandi alternatives include a dedicated IP?

Dripify gives each account a dedicated IP in your location, and SalesRobot sends with dedicated local IP rotation. HeyReach gives each Growth sender a dedicated residential proxy, GetSales.io runs every account on a managed ISP proxy, La Growth Machine uses dedicated 5G mobile proxies, and Linked Helper sends from your own computer or server.

What is the best Expandi alternative for agencies?

HeyReach and Skylead publish flat agency prices of $999 a month for 25 senders or seats, where Expandi prices its Agency plan on a call. GetSales.io gives each client its own workspace and inbox, and Meet Alfred's Agency plan adds white label from 3 seats.

Does Expandi have a CRM?

Expandi has a Global Smart Inbox with labels, tags and notes, and native integrations with HubSpot, Salesforce and Pipedrive. Other CRMs connect through Zapier, webhooks or its API.

How many connection requests can Expandi send per week?

Expandi advertises up to 300+ connection requests a week and recommends a weekly number based on its own research. LinkedIn keeps its own limit private.
