Expandi is a LinkedIn automation software that runs outreach sequences from the cloud, with a dedicated IP address in your country for each LinkedIn account. If you're comparing Expandi alternatives, you probably like its careful approach and want something it doesn't sell, like a lower price per account or a CRM that holds every reply.

We built the LinkedIn sending engine inside Omniscient this year, so we read Expandi's safety pages closely for this guide. Many of its choices match ones we made too, like working hours in your own time zone and a warmup for new profiles. Below are the 13 alternatives we'd shortlist, grouped by the reason you're leaving.

Every price below comes from the vendor's own pricing page, read in November 2026. We haven't run campaigns in these tools, so we compare what each vendor publishes with what we learned building our own engine.

What Expandi does well

Here's what Expandi's pricing page and homepage list today, which is what you'd give up:

  • The Business plan costs $99 a month, or $79 a month billed yearly, and includes a dedicated IP address based in your country and an automatic profile warmup.
  • Sequences mix connection requests, messages and email follow ups, with If/Action conditions that branch on what each person does.
  • Its integrations page lists native HubSpot, Salesforce and Pipedrive integrations, plus webhooks, Zapier and API credentials.
  • The homepage lists duplicate checks at the person and company level, a blacklist, A/B tests for messages and a Global Smart Inbox with labels, tags and notes.
  • The Agency plan starts at 10 seats, is priced on request and adds white label use, client reports, and roles and permissions.

That's honestly a strong list, and the safety part reads a lot like the one we built. If a dedicated IP and an automatic warmup are must haves and the price works for you, staying with Expandi is a fair choice.

Why teams look for an Expandi alternative

Each point below starts from Expandi's own pages:

  • Each seat covers one LinkedIn account at a time, says its help center, so every rep needs a Business seat until you reach the Agency plan's 10 seats.
  • The free trial lasts 7 days, while nine tools on this list give you 14 days or more.
  • Personalized images, GIFs and Sendspark video cost extra on top of the plan.
  • Email follow ups go out through the email provider you connect, and the pricing page lists no email finder, so finding addresses takes another tool.
  • Replies land in Expandi's inbox and reach your CRM through a sync, so the CRM stays a second tool with its own bill.

The 13 best Expandi alternatives in 2026

01Omniscient

Omniscient is a CRM and LinkedIn outreach software that drafts a message for each lead and sends only what your team approves. Replies land in one Inbox next to the person's CRM record, and the CRM fills itself in from your email and calendar.

Expandi syncs LinkedIn data to a CRM you pay for separately, while Omniscient is the CRM itself.

Pacing is on for every sender from the first day:

  • A new account starts at 5 invitations a day and adds 2 a day until it reaches your limit, and the warmup starts again after a reconnect or a restriction.
  • A profile view goes out 1 to 4 hours before each invitation, unless you turn that setting off.
  • Each day's real total lands between 80% and 100% of your limit, inside your working hours with a lunch break.
  • A person can be in only one live campaign at a time, and a reply stops their outreach in every campaign.

Before a campaign goes live, Omniscient shows how many people it reaches and who's left out, with the reason. You approve each person's messages, or everyone ready as you go live, and editing a message after its approval clears it.

Scout finds leads from a plain sentence, and Otto, our agent, drafts each message from the person's profile and recent activity.

Admittedly, Omniscient isn't the right fit if you need email steps, a dedicated IP or white label client access, since it sends on LinkedIn only and lists none of those. Each campaign also sends from one LinkedIn account, so an agency rotating dozens of client senders will be better served by HeyReach.

Paid seats start at $19 per seat a month, billed yearly, and the free trial lasts 14 days with no credit card. See the LinkedIn Outbound page and the CRM page for more.

02HeyReach

HeyReach is a LinkedIn automation software that runs outreach from many LinkedIn accounts at once, with every reply in one unified inbox. Growth costs $79 per sender a month, or $64 billed yearly, with the API, webhooks and a one time pool of 100 email enrichment credits per sender.

  • The pricing FAQ says the entry plan gives each sender a dedicated residential proxy, while Agency and Unlimited ask you to bring your own.
  • Agency costs $999 a month for 25 senders and includes one white label brand.
  • Unlimited costs $2,999 a month, with a fair use cap of 300 senders.
  • Cold email runs through native Instantly and Smartlead integrations.
  • The same FAQ offers a free trial of 14 days with no card and up to 3 LinkedIn accounts.

Interestingly, HeyReach publishes the flat agency price that Expandi shares on request. With 25 or more client accounts, that may settle it for you, and our guide to LinkedIn automation tools for agencies compares agency plans side by side.

03Skylead

Skylead is a LinkedIn automation and cold email software that builds sequences with if/else conditions across both channels. The All-in-one plan costs $100 per seat a month and includes unlimited email accounts, 100,000 emails a month and email warmup.

  • Agency is listed as a first year promotion, at $999 a month for 25 seats or $1,999 a month for unlimited seats.
  • Agency adds one inbox for every client conversation and a white label option.
  • The pricing page shows real time sync with HubSpot, Pipedrive and Salesforce, plus an API.
  • The free trial lasts 7 days.

A Skylead seat costs about the same as an Expandi seat and adds email volume and warmup, so it suits teams where cold email matters as much as LinkedIn. Agencies should confirm the price after the first year before moving clients over.

04GetSales.io

GetSales.io is a LinkedIn and email outreach software that gives each agency client a workspace with its own accounts and inbox. LinkedIn costs $69 a month for 1 account, or $195 a month for 5, and the page says the price per account falls to between $39 and $10 at scale.

  • Each LinkedIn account runs in its own cloud session with a managed ISP proxy and a gradual ramp, which covers much of what Expandi's dedicated IP and warmup do.
  • Email Outreach is a separate module at $59 a month for 100 inboxes, with email warmup and a dedicated server and IP.
  • Replies from every account land in one team inbox, and each one gets one of 15 labels, such as Interested or Out of office.
  • Clients connect their own LinkedIn accounts through a sign in link, so your agency never holds their passwords.
  • White label comes with the Business setup, listed from $500 a month in resources.

Its AI SDR drafts an answer to each reply and either waits for your approval or sends on its own. Personally, we'd keep approval on while you learn how it writes.

The integrations page lists HubSpot, Pipedrive, Salesforce, folk and GoHighLevel as coming, so confirm your CRM link is live before you move clients over. The trial lasts 7 days, the same as Expandi's.

05La Growth Machine

La Growth Machine is a sales automation software that runs multichannel sequences on LinkedIn, email, calls and X. Basic costs €60 per identity a month, or €50 billed yearly, for LinkedIn and email.

  • Pro costs €120 a month, or €100 billed yearly, and adds calls and one inbox for LinkedIn and email.
  • Ultimate costs €180 a month, or €150 billed yearly, and adds X plus native HubSpot and Pipedrive sync with sent and replied messages in your CRM.
  • An identity is one person's set of accounts, and each one is billed separately.
  • The free trial lasts 14 days with no card and covers 1 identity.

We'd pick La Growth Machine for reps who call and email as much as they message on LinkedIn. Its Agency plan starts at 6 identities, against Expandi's 10 seats.

06lemlist

lemlist is a sales engagement software that finds leads and runs campaigns across email, LinkedIn and calls. LinkedIn steps need the Multichannel plan at $109 per user a month, or $87 billed yearly, since the $69 Email plan has no LinkedIn automation.

  • LinkedIn steps cover profile visits, post likes, skill endorsements, invitations, text messages, voice notes and InMail.
  • Multichannel adds a built in call dialer and SMS, with WhatsApp as an add on, and includes 5 senders per user.
  • Every seat includes lemwarm, lemlist's email warmup, and the lead database lists more than 600 million B2B contacts.
  • Native HubSpot, Salesforce and Pipedrive integrations come with Multichannel.
  • The FAQ lists a 14 day trial with Multichannel features, apart from in app calls, and 200 credits.

Frankly, lemlist is the pick when email is your main channel and LinkedIn is the second touch. Its LinkedIn page promises "smart limits and human-like delays" without giving numbers, so you'd set your own pace and give up Expandi's dedicated IP.

07SalesRobot

SalesRobot is a LinkedIn and cold email automation software that runs in the cloud with a dedicated IP for each LinkedIn account. Basic costs $59 per LinkedIn account a month, or $39 billed yearly, and the free trial lasts 14 days with no card.

Basic allows one active campaign at 20 connection requests a day, so most teams leaving Expandi would start a plan higher:

  • Advanced costs $79 per account a month, or $59 billed yearly, with unlimited campaigns, A/B tests and native HubSpot, Pipedrive and Salesforce sync.
  • Professional costs $99, or $79 billed yearly, and adds team management, which puts it level with Expandi's Business prices.
  • Email automation costs $15 per email account a month on top of the LinkedIn plan.
  • The site lists an AI Appointment Setter that answers leads for you.

The homepage puts the risk of a LinkedIn ban at 0.00001% and credits LinkedIn's mobile app APIs for it. LinkedIn doesn't publish its limits, so we'd read that number as marketing. Advanced allows up to 75 connection requests a day, and our own engine stops at 40, so we'd set yours well below the ceiling.

08Dripify

Dripify is a LinkedIn automation software that runs drip campaigns of LinkedIn and email steps with a visual sequence builder. Pro costs $79 per user a month, or $59 billed yearly, and Advanced costs $99, or $79 billed yearly.

  • Each seat connects one LinkedIn account, the same way Expandi bills.
  • Its FAQ mentions a dedicated IP in your location and randomized timing inside your working hours.
  • Pro includes 100 email finder credits a month, a HubSpot integration, an open API and an MCP server.
  • Team management and what Dripify calls advanced LinkedIn protection come only with Advanced.
  • The free trial lasts 7 days with Advanced features, needs no card and charges nothing when it ends.

Advanced matches Expandi's Business prices, and Pro is the cheaper way into cloud LinkedIn and email sequences. Dripify lists a daily quota of up to 75 connection requests, and we'd stay well under that, since our own engine allows 40 at most.

09Meet Alfred

Meet Alfred is a LinkedIn automation software that runs sequences across LinkedIn, email and X from the cloud. Its LinkedIn Only plan costs $29 per user a month, billed yearly, with a smart inbox, templates and 1,000 Lead Finder results.

  • Multi-Channel costs $49 per user a month, billed yearly, and adds email and X steps, Sales Navigator and InMail, plus Zapier and webhooks.
  • Agency costs $39 per seat a month, billed yearly, starts at 3 seats, and adds native Salesforce and HubSpot, a team inbox, roles and white label.
  • The homepage offers a 7 day free trial with no credit card.

Happily for small agencies, white label starts at 3 seats here, where Expandi's starts at 10. An Agency seat also costs less than a Multi-Channel seat once you meet that minimum.

The pricing page shows only yearly prices, so ask for the monthly rate before you commit.

10Dux-Soup

Dux-Soup is a LinkedIn automation software that runs drip campaigns through its STACKED app, either on your own computer or in Dux-Soup's cloud. STACKED local costs $55 per seat a month, or $41.25 billed yearly, and STACKED cloud costs $99, or $74.17 billed yearly.

  • Campaigns use up to 12 LinkedIn actions, including visits, invitations, messages, InMail, endorsements, follows and post likes, and pause when someone replies.
  • We found no email steps on its pages, so email stays in another tool.
  • One inbox holds replies from LinkedIn, Sales Navigator and Recruiter.
  • Native integrations include Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Freshsales and HighLevel, plus Zapier and Make.
  • Shared team campaigns need 2 or more seats.

STACKED cloud costs the same as Expandi's monthly Business price, and the local app costs less as long as your computer stays on. Reassuringly, the homepage lists daily limits that ramp up gradually, speed throttling and an auto snooze, though it keeps the exact numbers confidential. Its drip campaigns page offers a free trial of 14 days.

11Waalaxy

Waalaxy is a LinkedIn outreach software that sends invitations and follow ups from simple campaigns with monthly quotas. Pro is listed at €19 per user a month for 300 invitations a month, and Advanced at €49 for 800.

  • Business, at €69 a month, adds cold email sequences, 500 email finder credits a month and multiple sender accounts.
  • A LinkedIn inbox is an optional extra at €20 a month.
  • Every plan includes CRM sync, and Advanced adds the API with Make, Zapier and n8n modules.
  • Each paid plan starts with a free trial of 14 days.

The page opens on yearly billing, with up to 50% off, so confirm the monthly price. Monthly quotas set a modest pace for you, which makes Waalaxy a gentle first tool for a founder who found Expandi's sequence builder more than they needed.

12Kanbox

Kanbox is a LinkedIn prospecting software that puts lead scraping, sequences, a smart inbox and Kanban pipelines in one Chrome extension. Starter costs $20 a month, or $12 billed yearly, and the free trial lasts 15 days with no credit card.

  • Starter runs one campaign at a time with 300 lead imports a month.
  • Essential costs $45 per LinkedIn account a month, or $30 billed yearly, with 3 campaigns at once and team access.
  • Pro costs $80, or $50 billed yearly, with 5 campaigns at once, 75,000 imports a month, webhooks and the API.
  • Agency 10 costs $399 a month, or $279 billed yearly, for 5 to 10 LinkedIn accounts, and you bring your own proxies.

Refreshingly, the Kanban board lets you move leads through stages right next to your LinkedIn messages, which suits a founder who works from the inbox. Teammates can manage an account without its owner sharing the password.

We found no CRM integrations or email steps on its site, only an email finder, so replies stay in Kanbox unless you connect the webhooks.

13Linked Helper

Linked Helper is a LinkedIn automation software that runs as a desktop app on your own computer or on a server you control. Standard with local data storage costs $15 per LinkedIn account a month on a monthly term, and a 12 month term takes up to 45% off.

  • Your LinkedIn session stays on that machine, so it has to be on, with the app running, while you send.
  • Native integrations include HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Zoho CRM and Close.
  • Standard caps about a dozen actions at 20 a day, such as likes, group messages and contacts sent to your CRM, and Pro lifts those caps.
  • The trial gives 14 days of full Pro access with no card, and nothing renews on its own.

Luckily for teams watching every dollar, Linked Helper has the lowest monthly price per account here. In return, you give up Expandi's cloud IP, and email stays in your cold email tool.

How to switch from Expandi without risking your account

A LinkedIn account brings its pending invitations and recent pace into the new tool, so the first week after a switch needs the most care. Here's the order we'd follow:

  1. 01
    Stop Expandi's campaigns first

    A removed seat ends with your billing cycle and the account stays active until then, so pause every campaign yourself.

  2. 02
    Save who you've contacted

    Export everyone in your Expandi campaigns, so the new tool can skip people who already heard from you.

  3. 03
    Bring your blacklist

    Load it as your do not contact list before the first campaign, so it holds across every import.

  4. 04
    Remove the seat as well as the account

    Expandi's help center says disconnecting a seat from a LinkedIn account "doesn't cancel the seat," so take it off your subscription too.

  5. 05
    Restart slower than you ended

    Treat the first week like a warmup, even on an old account, and raise limits once acceptance holds above 20%.

  6. 06
    Test the reply path

    Have a colleague answer a test message and confirm it lands on the right CRM record.

Thankfully, a careful tool handles part of this for you. In Omniscient, every newly connected account warms up from 5 invitations a day, even an old one, and our engine checks the relationship on LinkedIn before every invitation. So someone still holding your old Expandi request isn't sent a second one, and people you're already connected with go straight to the message.

LinkedIn's page on invitation restrictions lists invitations that sit pending or get ignored as one cause. It also says a withdrawn invitation can't be sent again to the same person for up to three weeks, so withdraw only the old requests you've given up on.

FAQs

What is the best alternative to Expandi?

It depends on why you're leaving. Omniscient fits teams that want outreach and a CRM together, HeyReach, Skylead and GetSales.io fit agencies, La Growth Machine fits reps who also call, and Linked Helper costs the least per account.

Is there a cheaper alternative to Expandi?

Yes. Expandi's Business plan costs $99 a month per seat, or $79 billed yearly. Linked Helper starts at $15 per LinkedIn account a month, Kanbox at $20 a month on Starter, Waalaxy lists €19 per user a month, and Omniscient starts at $19 per seat a month billed yearly.

Which Expandi alternatives include a dedicated IP?

SalesRobot lists a dedicated IP for each LinkedIn account, and Dripify's FAQ lists a dedicated IP in your location. HeyReach gives each sender on its entry plan a dedicated residential proxy, GetSales.io runs each account with a managed ISP proxy, and Linked Helper sends from your own computer or server.

What is the best Expandi alternative for agencies?

HeyReach and Skylead publish flat agency prices of $999 a month for 25 senders or seats, where Expandi prices its Agency plan on request. GetSales.io gives each client its own workspace and inbox, and Meet Alfred's Agency plan adds white label from 3 seats.

Does Expandi have a CRM?

Expandi has a Smart Inbox with labels, tags and notes, and native integrations with HubSpot, Salesforce and Pipedrive. Other CRMs connect through Zapier, webhooks or its API.

How many connection requests can Expandi send per week?

Expandi's product menu says "up to 300+ connection requests weekly," and its pricing FAQ says it recommends an optimal weekly number from its own research. LinkedIn doesn't publish its own limit.
