You chose HeyReach to run LinkedIn outreach from many accounts at once, and now part of the setup costs more than it should, whether that's a per sender bill for a small team or a second tool just for email. These are the 13 HeyReach alternatives we'd shortlist in 2026, matched to the reason you're leaving.

HeyReach is a LinkedIn automation tool that connects many LinkedIn accounts, rotates sending between them and puts every reply in one unified inbox. We build the LinkedIn sending engine inside Omniscient, so we know which parts of a tool like this protect your accounts and which parts land back on your desk.

If you're short on time, here's where we'd start:

  • Pick Omniscient if you want LinkedIn outreach inside a CRM that fills itself in.
  • Pick Expandi if you want email follow ups and an automatic warmup in one LinkedIn tool.
  • Pick GetSales.io if you run an agency and want each client's LinkedIn and email in its own workspace.
  • Pick Skylead if you run an agency and want one price for 25 seats with cold email included.
  • Pick La Growth Machine or lemlist if every rep runs LinkedIn and email from one plan.
  • Pick SalesRobot if you want an AI agent on your replies and native CRM sync.
  • Pick Dripify if a small team wants LinkedIn and email steps in simple drip campaigns.
  • Pick Meet Alfred if X belongs in your sequences next to LinkedIn and email.
  • Pick Dux-Soup if you want to choose between a desktop app and the cloud.
  • Pick Waalaxy or Kanbox if you do outreach on your own and want a low monthly price.
  • Pick Linked Helper if you want 30+ LinkedIn action types and a desktop app suits you.

What HeyReach does well

HeyReach's pricing promise is "unlimited senders, one flat monthly fee," and the Agency and Unlimited plans come close to it. You pay only for LinkedIn senders, and clients, teammates and VAs join for free. Here's what the plans include:

  • Growth: $79 per sender a month, or $64 billed yearly, with unlimited campaigns, the unified inbox, the API, webhooks and an MCP server. Each sender gets a dedicated residential proxy.
  • Agency: $999 a month for 25 senders, with a 50 sender option, white label, onboarding done for you and a dedicated Slack channel. You bring your own proxies.
  • Unlimited: $2,999 a month for up to 300 senders under a fair use policy, with migration done for you and priority support.
  • Free trial: 14 days with no card and up to 3 LinkedIn accounts, or 30 days on request for Agency and Unlimited.

Admittedly, with 25 or more client accounts, one flat bill and one inbox across all of them is hard to beat, and HeyReach may still be your best fit. Our guide to LinkedIn automation tools for agencies compares the agency plans side by side.

Why teams look for a HeyReach alternative

Most of the reasons come down to how HeyReach is built:

  • It bills per sender until you reach the Agency plan, so a team of three or four pays the Growth rate for every account.
  • It sends on LinkedIn only. Email runs through native integrations with Instantly, Smartlead and EmailBison, which means a second tool and a second bill.
  • Its native CRM sync covers HubSpot, and it also connects to Attio and Breakcold, so Salesforce, Pipedrive and the rest go through Zapier, Make, n8n or the API.
  • A brand new LinkedIn account has to be warmed up by hand until it reaches at least 100 connections before you connect it.

These matter most for a small sales team, or for anyone whose replies need to land in a CRM without somebody copying them over.

The bigger difference is who acts on those numbers. HeyReach handles pushback with Cooldown Mode and Locks, while the warmup schedule and the acceptance rate stay your job. Our engine ramps a new account on its own and slows down when acceptance drops or LinkedIn rate limits it.

01Omniscient

Omniscient is a CRM with LinkedIn outreach built for small B2B sales teams that want their LinkedIn prospecting and their deals in one place. Otto, our agent, drafts a message for each lead, and nothing goes out until you approve it. Every reply lands next to the person's CRM record, which fills itself in from your email and calendar.

Try Omniscient free →

Key features

  • Approval before sending: Otto drafts each invitation and message from the lead's role and recent activity, and only the approved text goes out.
  • Automatic warmup: A new account starts at 5 invitations a day and adds 2 a day until it reaches your limit.
  • Working days that look human: Actions go out in each sender's hours, with a lunch break and random gaps of 3 to 12 minutes at the Normal pace.
  • Automatic slowdown: When under 20% of a week's invitations are accepted, or LinkedIn rate limits the account, it drops to half pace for 3 days.
  • Scout lead search: Describe your buyers in a sentence, and Scout finds them and scores each lead out of 100 for fit and timing.
  • Inbox on the CRM record: Replies from every teammate's sender land in one Inbox beside the person's record, shared with the team or kept to the sender's owner and admins.
  • One campaign per person: A lead can sit in only one live campaign, which stops two teammates from messaging the same buyer at once.

Pros

  • Outreach and CRM together: Replies and deals sit on the same record, so nobody copies a LinkedIn thread into a second tool.
  • Safety that runs itself: Warmup and slowdowns happen on their own, without anyone keeping a schedule by hand.
  • Nothing sent twice: The engine confirms the relationship on LinkedIn before each invitation, and a send that times out is never retried automatically.

Cons

  • LinkedIn only: Campaigns have no email steps, and cold email still needs a tool of its own.
  • One sender per campaign: Each campaign sends from one LinkedIn account, and there's no white label for agencies running dozens of client senders.
  • Newer product: Omniscient's LinkedIn outreach is newer than most tools on this list and has a shorter track record with large sales teams.

Price and plans

You can try Omniscient free for 14 days with no credit card. Prices are per seat, per month, billed yearly:

  • Free: $0 for teammates who only view or log data.
  • Starter: $19 per seat, per month.
  • Pro: $49 per seat, per month.
  • Business: $99 per seat, per month.

Monthly billing costs 20% more. The LinkedIn Outbound page and the CRM page show the rest.

Pros

Cons

Price and plans

03GetSales.io

GetSales.io is a LinkedIn and email outreach platform built for agencies and GTM teams that run campaigns on each client's own accounts. For agencies, it's the closest match to HeyReach on this list, with email built in from the start and a managed proxy on every LinkedIn account.

Key features

  • A workspace per client: Each client keeps its own LinkedIn accounts, mailboxes and inbox, walled off from your other clients.
  • Sign in links for clients: Clients connect their own LinkedIn through a link, so their password and 2FA code never pass through you.
  • Managed ISP proxies: Every LinkedIn account runs in its own cloud session on a managed proxy, with a gradual ramp toward its limits.
  • Unibox: Replies from every account in a workspace land in one team inbox, each with its label and its next move.
  • AI SDR: Each reply gets one of 15 labels, and the drafted answer either waits for your OK or sends on its own, scenario by scenario.

Pros

  • One bill for both channels: An agency can replace a HeyReach and Instantly pairing with one app and one bill.
  • No proxy shopping: Proxies come managed, while HeyReach's Agency plan asks you to bring your own.
  • Free data workspace: Playtables stay free with your own API keys, which lets you build and score lists before paying for sending.

Cons

  • CRM sync still on the way: Native HubSpot, Pipedrive, Salesforce, folk and GoHighLevel integrations are coming, so for now replies reach a CRM through webhooks, the API or MCP.
  • White label needs volume: White label comes with the Business setup, which starts at $500 a month in resources.
  • Short trial for sending: LinkedIn and email sending get a free trial of 7 days.

Price and plans

Playtables are free with your own API keys, and LinkedIn and email sending come with a free trial of 7 days.

  • LinkedIn accounts: $69 a month for 1 account, or $39 per account from 5 accounts.
  • Email Outreach: $59 a month for 100 inboxes.
  • Business: included from $500 a month in resources, with white label and a dedicated team chat.
  • Enterprise: included from $1,500 a month in resources, with migration and a monthly growth review.

Price and plans

FAQs

What is the best HeyReach alternative?

Pick by the reason you're leaving. Omniscient fits small teams that want outreach and a CRM together, and Expandi or Skylead fit teams that want email in the same sequence. For a low monthly price as a solo seller, look at Waalaxy or Kanbox.

What is the best HeyReach alternative for agencies?

GetSales.io and Skylead come closest. GetSales.io gives each client its own workspace and inbox with email built in, and Skylead's Agency plan costs $999 a month for 25 seats as a first year promotion. For 5 to 10 accounts, Kanbox Agency 10 costs $399 a month.

Is there a cheaper alternative to HeyReach?

For one or two senders, yes. HeyReach's Growth plan costs $79 per sender a month. Linked Helper starts at $15 per LinkedIn account with local data storage, Waalaxy at $19 per user, Omniscient at $19 per seat billed yearly and Kanbox at $20 a month.

Does HeyReach have its own CRM?

No. It syncs natively with HubSpot, connects to Attio and Breakcold, and reaches other CRMs through Zapier, Make, n8n or its API.

How many connection requests can HeyReach send per day?

HeyReach lets a sender go up to 40 connection requests a day, suggests 25 once the account is warmed up, and caps each sender at 200 a week. LinkedIn doesn't publish its own limit.
