lemlist is a sales engagement software that runs outbound campaigns across email, LinkedIn, calls, WhatsApp and SMS from one place. If you're comparing lemlist alternatives for LinkedIn outreach, LinkedIn probably books more of your meetings than email does, so you want a tool and a bill built around it.

We built the LinkedIn sending engine inside Omniscient this year, so we read lemlist's help articles on LinkedIn limits closely while we set our own. Below are the 13 alternatives we'd shortlist, matched to the reason you're leaving.

Prices come from each vendor's own pages, read in November 2026. We haven't run campaigns in these tools, so we compare what each vendor publishes with what we learned building our own engine.

If you're short on time, here's where we'd start:

  • Pick Omniscient for LinkedIn outreach you approve, inside a CRM that fills itself in.
  • Pick La Growth Machine for the closest swap, with LinkedIn and email on the starting plan.
  • Pick Expandi for email steps plus a dedicated IP and an automatic warmup.
  • Pick Dripify for simple LinkedIn and email sequences in a visual builder.
  • Pick Skylead if you send a lot of cold email alongside LinkedIn.
  • Pick SalesRobot for a dedicated IP and AI help with first replies.
  • Pick Closely for CRM sync and white label on the first plan.
  • Pick Meet Alfred if you also reach buyers on X.
  • Pick HeyReach if you run many LinkedIn accounts for clients.
  • Pick GetSales.io if each client needs its own workspace and inbox.
  • Pick Waalaxy if you do outreach alone on a small budget.
  • Pick Dux-Soup if you only need LinkedIn and want to choose local or cloud.
  • Pick Linked Helper for the lowest price per account, if a desktop app is fine.

What lemlist does well for LinkedIn outreach

lemlist's homepage says more than 20,000 sales teams run their outbound in it, and its strength is putting every channel into one sequence. Here's what its pricing page lists for LinkedIn today:

  • LinkedIn automation comes with the Multichannel plan, at $109 per user a month or $87 billed yearly.
  • LinkedIn steps cover profile visits, follows, invitations, text messages, voice messages and InMail.
  • Each Multichannel user gets 5 senders, a built in call dialer and lemlist inside Gmail and LinkedIn.
  • HubSpot, Salesforce and Pipedrive integrations come with every plan, along with lemwarm for email deliverability.

Honestly, if email books most of your meetings and LinkedIn is the second touch, lemlist is hard to replace. Our comparison of Waalaxy vs lemlist goes deeper on that split.

Why teams look for a lemlist alternative for LinkedIn

Each reason below starts from lemlist's own pages:

  • LinkedIn steps need the Multichannel plan, so a seller who only uses LinkedIn still pays for the email and call tools that come with it.
  • Its help article on disconnections says you reconnect LinkedIn through lemlist's Chrome extension, and that logging out or clearing cookies disconnects it.
  • Ramp-Up, lemlist's invitation warmup, is a setting that the help center says "must be turned on" before it raises your daily volume.
  • Only invitations ramp up, so on a new account its limits article tells you to lower messages, profile visits and follows by hand for two to three weeks.
  • After a LinkedIn warning, the help center asks you to pause all LinkedIn steps for at least 48 hours, then restart at about 5 actions a day.

lemlist's LinkedIn limits next to our pacing

lemlist's help center is specific about LinkedIn, and its articles on account limits and connection request limits give the figures below. We've put our engine's settings beside them:

Interestingly, the two sets of numbers sit close together. lemlist treats about 100 a week as LinkedIn's cap, but LinkedIn doesn't publish one, so both columns are each tool's own reading. Our guide to the LinkedIn connection request limit explains where the common figures come from.

In the articles we read, lemlist leaves the warmup switch and the pause after a warning to you. That's where the two tools differ most, because our engine ramps up a new account and slows down on its own.

A few more habits shape each day of sending in our engine:

  • Each day's real total lands between 80% and 100% of your limit, so the days don't all look the same.
  • A profile view goes out 1 to 4 hours before each invitation, unless you turn that setting off.
  • A reconnected or restricted account starts its warmup again from 5 a day.

LinkedIn's page on invitation restrictions names "many invitations within a short amount of time" as one cause. Another is invitations that are "ignored, left pending, or marked as spam." A lower daily pace helps with that one too, because a list people ignore shows up in your acceptance rate before many requests pile up.

Spreading invitations out does cost speed: at the Normal pace of 80 a week, a list of 400 people takes at least five weeks to invite.

Every tool on this list automates LinkedIn, ours included. LinkedIn's prohibited software page says members who use such tools are "in violation of the User Agreement," so even a careful pace carries some risk for your account.

The 13 best lemlist alternatives for LinkedIn outreach

01Omniscient

Omniscient is a CRM and LinkedIn outreach software that drafts a message for each lead and sends only what your team approves. Replies land in one Inbox next to the person's CRM record, and the CRM fills itself in from your email and calendar.

A campaign usually starts in Scout. You describe your buyer in one sentence, and each match gets a score out of 100: up to 60 points for fit and up to 40 for timing signals like hiring or a new role.

Otto, our agent, then writes each person's connection request and messages from your pitch and their signals. You approve each person, or everyone at launch, and editing a message clears its approval, so LinkedIn only receives text your team approved.

Two rules hold in every campaign:

  • A person can be in only one live campaign at a time, and a reply stops their outreach in all of them.
  • A send that times out is never retried on its own, because a doubled message can't be taken back.

Admittedly, Omniscient sends on LinkedIn only, with no email or call steps. If email books most of your meetings, lemlist or La Growth Machine will suit you better, and an agency rotating dozens of client accounts should look at HeyReach.

Paid seats start at $19 per seat a month, billed yearly, and the free trial lasts 14 days with no credit card. See the LinkedIn Outbound and CRM pages for more.

02La Growth Machine

La Growth Machine is a sales automation software that runs multichannel sequences on LinkedIn, email, calls and X. Basic costs €60 per identity a month for LinkedIn and email, and Pro costs €120 per identity and adds calls and a unified inbox.

  • An identity is one person's set of accounts, and each one is billed separately.
  • Basic allows up to 3 identities, with 1 sending email each and 250 enriched leads a month.
  • The plan table lists HubSpot and Pipedrive sync on Ultimate, which also adds X.
  • The free trial lasts 14 days with no card and covers 1 identity.

Personally, we'd look here first if you like lemlist's mix of channels and want LinkedIn and email on the starting plan. The catch is that Basic sends from 1 email address per identity. CRM sync appears only on Ultimate's card, so ask which plan your HubSpot setup needs.

03Expandi

Expandi is a LinkedIn automation software that runs outreach sequences from the cloud for sales teams and agencies. Its Business plan costs $99 a month, or $79 billed yearly, and the free trial lasts 7 days.

  • Business includes a dedicated IP address based in your country and an automatic profile warmup.
  • Sequences mix connection requests and messages with email follow ups in one flow.
  • The Agency plan starts at 10 seats with custom pricing and adds white label use.

Frankly, Expandi suits a team where LinkedIn leads and email follows up, and where you'd rather have the warmup handled for you. Its trial is shorter than lemlist's 14 days, so plan a focused test before you move a live account.

04Dripify

Dripify is a LinkedIn automation software that builds sequences of LinkedIn and email steps in a drag and drop builder. Pro costs $79 per user a month, or $59 billed yearly, and Advanced costs $99, or $79 billed yearly.

  • Both plans include LinkedIn and email sequences, a HubSpot integration, an open API and an MCP server.
  • Team management and a check that prevents duplicate outreach come with Advanced.
  • The free trial lasts 7 days with Advanced features and needs no card.

Dripify suits a small team that wants lemlist's LinkedIn and email steps at a lower monthly price per user. Its FAQ allows up to 75 connection requests a day on every plan, and we'd stay well below that, since lemlist suggests 20 and our engine stops at 40.

05Skylead

Skylead is a LinkedIn automation and cold email software that builds sequences with if/else conditions across both channels. The All-in-one plan costs $100 per seat a month, and the free trial lasts 7 days.

  • Each seat includes unlimited email accounts, up to 100,000 emails a month, email warmup and an email finder and verifier.
  • The Agency plan costs $999 a month for 25 seats, or $1,999 for unlimited seats, listed as a first year promotion.
  • Agency adds an agency dashboard and a Unibox for client conversations.

Skylead fits a team that sends a lot of cold email and wants LinkedIn in the same seat. There's no cheaper LinkedIn only plan, so a seller who never sends email still pays for a seat that includes 100,000 emails a month. Ask what the Agency promotion becomes in year two before you move clients over.

06SalesRobot

SalesRobot is a LinkedIn and cold email automation software that runs sequences from the cloud and bills per LinkedIn account. Basic costs $59 per LinkedIn account a month, or $39 billed yearly, and Advanced costs $79, or $59 billed yearly.

  • Every plan puts LinkedIn and email steps in one sequence, with a dedicated local IP and an AI Appointment Setter Agent that keeps leads talking until they book.
  • Basic allows 1 active campaign and 20 connection requests a day, and Advanced lifts that to unlimited campaigns and 75 a day.
  • Native HubSpot, Pipedrive and Salesforce sync starts on Advanced, since Basic only moves leads through CSV files and Google Sheets.
  • The free trial lasts 14 days, and the pricing FAQ says it doesn't take your credit card.

Basic's 20 requests a day matches the figure lemlist's help center suggests, which makes it a sensible place to start. We'd stay well under 75 even on Advanced.

The AI setter is the part to watch. The pricing page lists it on every plan, while the homepage calls it an add-on, so confirm its price. We made the opposite call in Omniscient: Otto writes a suggested answer, and a person on your team decides whether it goes out. If you turn the setter on, read its first week of answers yourself.

07Closely

Closely is a LinkedIn and email outreach software that runs sequences across both channels and puts DMs, InMails and email replies in one unified inbox. Starter costs $65 a month for 1 LinkedIn account, or $39 billed yearly, and each sender is one LinkedIn account plus one email inbox.

  • Starter includes unlimited email accounts, CRM integrations and white label, and the homepage names Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive and GoHighLevel.
  • AI personalization, the email finder and live LinkedIn scraping start on Growth, at $159 a month for 3 senders, or $95 billed yearly.
  • Sequences pause on their own when a prospect replies, and teammates can assign and tag threads in the inbox.

Helpfully, Starter keeps the CRM sync you had in lemlist, so moving doesn't cost you that link. The AI personalization in Closely's headline starts one plan higher, though. The site doesn't say how long the free trial lasts, so ask before you schedule a test.

Closely suits a small team that wants LinkedIn and email together with its CRM connected from the first plan.

08Meet Alfred

Meet Alfred is a LinkedIn automation software that runs sequences on LinkedIn, email and X from its own cloud, with nothing to install on your machine. On yearly billing, LinkedIn Only costs $29 per user a month. Multi-Channel adds email and X for $49, and Agency costs $39.

  • Every plan has a Smart LinkedIn Inbox and human paced delays, and you can adjust daily limits and activity windows yourself.
  • InMail and the Sales Navigator inbox come with Multi-Channel and Agency.
  • Agency has everything in Multi-Channel plus native HubSpot and Salesforce sync, a team inbox and white label. The page's top price list calls it Teams.
  • The free trial lasts 7 days with no credit card.

LinkedIn Only fits a seller who left lemlist because they use only LinkedIn, since email is the part you stop paying for. The pricing page we read showed only yearly prices, so ask for the monthly rate if you'd rather not commit for a year.

Meet Alfred suits a small team that also reaches buyers on X. La Growth Machine is the only other tool here that covers X, and only on its Ultimate plan.

09HeyReach

HeyReach is a LinkedIn automation software that runs outreach from many LinkedIn accounts at once, with every reply in one unified inbox. Growth costs $79 per sender a month, or $64 billed yearly, and Agency costs $999 a month for 25 senders.

  • HeyReach bills per LinkedIn sender, so adding teammates costs nothing.
  • Email runs through native integrations with Instantly, Smartlead and EmailBison.
  • Agency adds white label and done for you onboarding.
  • The free trial lasts 14 days, with no card and up to 3 LinkedIn accounts.

Happily for agencies, the Agency plan puts 25 senders on one fixed bill. The flip side is that email lives in a second tool, so if your sequences need it, you'll pay for Instantly, Smartlead or EmailBison as well.

10GetSales.io

GetSales.io is a LinkedIn and email outreach software for agencies and GTM teams that gives each client a workspace with its own accounts and inbox. LinkedIn costs $69 a month for 1 account, or $195 a month for 5, and email costs $59 a month for 100 inboxes.

  • Each LinkedIn account runs in its own cloud session with a managed ISP proxy, and new accounts warm up gradually.
  • The pricing page says LinkedIn falls to "$39 to $10 per account at scale," with no fee per user, lead or campaign.
  • Replies land in a team inbox with labels, and an AI SDR drafts answers and hands off the ones that need a person.
  • It sends leads to HubSpot, Pipedrive, Salesforce, folk and GoHighLevel, and the trial lasts 7 days for LinkedIn and email.

Clients sign in to their own LinkedIn, so their password and 2FA code never pass through your agency. That matters when you run accounts you don't own.

The bill comes in separate parts for LinkedIn accounts, email capacity and data credits, so add them up before you compare it with lemlist. GetSales.io suits an agency that wants each client kept apart, with email and a managed proxy in the same app.

11Waalaxy

Waalaxy is a LinkedIn outreach software that finds prospects, sends invitations and messages, and follows up until people reply. Its pricing page lists Pro at €19 per user a month with 300 invitations a month, and Advanced at €49 with 800, both on yearly billing.

  • Business, at €69, adds cold email and 500 email finder credits a month.
  • An Inbox for your LinkedIn conversations is an add on at €20 a month.
  • All three listed plans include CRM sync and a free trial of 14 days.

Its homepage says "75% of our users had never done LinkedIn outreach before," and monthly quotas keep a beginner at a modest pace. Waalaxy's subscriptions article gives Business a standard price of €139 a month, which can vary by country. It also says the trial needs a credit card, so cancel before it ends if you're only testing.

12Dux-Soup

Dux-Soup is a LinkedIn automation software that runs drip campaigns through its STACKED app, on your own computer or in Dux-Soup's cloud. STACKED local costs $55 per seat a month, or $41.25 billed yearly, and STACKED cloud costs $99, or $74.17 billed yearly.

  • Both plans include unlimited campaigns, AI personalized messages and actions like visits, follows, endorsements, InMail, invitations and messages.
  • Integrations cover Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive and HighLevel, plus Zapier and Make.
  • The homepage says new users start with "sensible daily activity limits," and a daily planner keeps sending inside your working hours.
  • Campaigns pause on their own when a person replies.

The pricing page lists no email steps, so Dux-Soup replaces only the LinkedIn half of lemlist. The local plan also stops when your computer sleeps, and the cloud plan is the way around that. The site doesn't give the trial length.

Surprisingly few tools here let you pick where the app runs. Dux-Soup suits a team that only needs LinkedIn and wants to start on a desktop, then move to the cloud later.

13Linked Helper

Linked Helper is a LinkedIn automation software that runs as a desktop app on your own computer. Standard costs $15 per LinkedIn account a month on a monthly term with local data storage, and $8.25 a month on a 12 month term.

  • Standard caps some advanced actions, such as group messages and likes, at 20 a day, and Pro lifts those caps.
  • Native HubSpot, Salesforce and Pipedrive integrations come with both plans.
  • The trial gives 14 days of full Pro access with no card, and nothing renews on its own when it ends.

Luckily, it's the lowest price per account on this list if your budget is tight. In return, the app works only while it's running on a computer or server you keep switched on.

How to switch from lemlist without risking your LinkedIn account

Moving a LinkedIn account between tools is the moment we'd watch most closely, because the account's history comes along with it. Here's the order we'd follow, and our guide to automating LinkedIn outreach without getting restricted covers pacing in depth:

  1. 01
    Pause lemlist first

    Its help center says two tools on one LinkedIn account still share one LinkedIn quota.

  2. 02
    Export who you've contacted

    Save your lead lists and replies, so the new tool skips anyone who already heard from you.

  3. 03
    Bring your opt outs

    Before the new tool's first campaign starts, mark everyone who asked you not to contact them.

  4. 04
    Restart slower than you ended

    Treat the first week like a warmup, even on an old account, and raise limits only while acceptance stays at 30% or more, the level lemlist calls healthy.

  5. 05
    Withdraw only stale requests

    lemlist suggests withdrawing after 7 to 14 days, and LinkedIn blocks a new invitation to the same person for up to three weeks after a withdrawal.

  6. 06
    Test the reply path

    Ask a colleague to answer a test message and check that it lands on the right CRM record.

Thankfully, a careful tool handles part of this for you. Before every invitation, our engine checks the relationship on LinkedIn, so someone still holding a request from your lemlist days doesn't get a second one.

FAQs

What is the best lemlist alternative for LinkedIn outreach?

It depends on why you're leaving. Omniscient fits small teams that want LinkedIn outreach and a CRM together. If you still want email steps, look at La Growth Machine or Expandi, and if you run many client accounts, look at HeyReach.

Is there a cheaper alternative to lemlist for LinkedIn?

Yes. lemlist's LinkedIn steps come with the Multichannel plan at $109 per user a month, or $87 billed yearly. Linked Helper starts at $15 per LinkedIn account on a monthly term. On yearly billing, Waalaxy starts at €19 per user, Omniscient at $19 per seat and Meet Alfred at $29 per user.

Which lemlist alternatives keep email steps next to LinkedIn?

La Growth Machine, Expandi, Dripify, Skylead, SalesRobot, Closely and GetSales.io all run email in the same app as LinkedIn. Meet Alfred adds email on its Multi-Channel and Agency plans, and HeyReach connects to Instantly, Smartlead or EmailBison.

Does lemlist's Email plan include LinkedIn automation?

No. lemlist's pricing page lists LinkedIn automation on the Multichannel and Enterprise plans only.
