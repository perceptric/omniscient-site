HubSpot's free CRM shows you every contact, deal and form fill in one place, and it covers 2 users with no end date. What it keeps out of view is the step up: sequences and forecasting sit on Sales Hub Professional, with a one-time onboarding fee, and AI work draws on monthly credits.

What Is a HubSpot CRM Alternative?

A HubSpot CRM alternative is a sales CRM that keeps your contacts, deals and conversations in one place by syncing your inbox, calendar and pipeline.

You import your HubSpot companies, contacts and deals, and the new CRM logs each email and meeting on the right record as you work. Most run as a web app with mobile apps, and some add a Chrome extension or work inside Gmail.

Most tools include:

  • ✅ Contact and company records
  • ✅ A visual deal pipeline
  • ✅ Email and calendar sync
  • ✅ Reports and forecasts

Some add a built-in agent that drafts follow-ups and suggests deal updates from your calls. The result is one clear view of every deal and every conversation, without paying for marketing tools your team doesn't use.

Why Do You Need a HubSpot CRM Alternative?

HubSpot is a great fit for teams that start free and want marketing and sales on one record. A small team feels the limit as it grows: the free CRM covers 2 users, and Sales Hub Professional, where sequences and forecasting start, costs $90 to $100 per seat a month plus a $1,500 onboarding fee.

A HubSpot CRM alternative solves this.

  • Room for more sellers: Most tools here price a seat well below HubSpot Professional.
  • Sequences on a smaller seat: Several include email sequences or workflows for under $50 a seat.
  • A steady AI budget: Some plans include AI in the seat price, so a busy month needs no extra credits.
  • One shared memory: Every email, meeting and call lands on the right company, person and deal, so your team and AI agents read from one place.
