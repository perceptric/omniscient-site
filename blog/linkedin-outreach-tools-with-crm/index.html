Here are the 15 best LinkedIn outreach tools with a CRM for 2026, with the features, pros, cons and price of each, so you can pick one where the conversation and the deal share a record.

We build Omniscient, which sends LinkedIn outreach and runs the CRM it lands in. We've spent a long time on the join between sending a message and remembering what came of it, and that join is what we judge every tool on.

If you're short on time, here's where we'd start:

Four kinds of LinkedIn outreach tool with a CRM

Every tool here says CRM somewhere, and the word means something different in each. We sort them into four kinds.

  • Outreach and CRM in one product. The tool sends your invitations and messages, and the same record holds deals, email and meetings. Omniscient and NetHunt CRM work this way.
  • LinkedIn automation with a light CRM. You get tags, notes or pipeline stages on each LinkedIn contact, while a deal pipeline with email history usually lives in another tool. Kanbox, Meet Alfred, Linked Helper, Dripify and Waalaxy sit here.
  • A CRM that holds LinkedIn contacts. The record comes first, LinkedIn contacts and conversations flow into it, and your team does most of the LinkedIn sending itself. Breakcold, folk, Salesflare and HubSpot Sales Hub work like this.
  • Outreach synced into the CRM you keep. The tool sends on LinkedIn and pushes contacts and messages into HubSpot, Salesforce or Pipedrive. La Growth Machine, lemlist, HeyReach and Closely take this route.

Which kind you need depends on where your deals close. When most deals close in LinkedIn messages and one call, a light CRM can be enough. When buyers move on to email and meetings, you'll want the first kind, or a full CRM with an outreach tool synced into it.

How we chose these LinkedIn outreach tools

A LinkedIn outreach tool sends invitations and messages from your account and follows up when people accept. For this list we kept tools that also store contacts, notes or deals, plus the outreach tools that sync every message into the CRM you already run.

We judged the CRM side on the questions we had to answer when we joined our own sending engine to our CRM:

  • Does a LinkedIn lead become one record, or a second copy of someone you already know?
  • Does a reply stop that person's outreach in every campaign?
  • Do email and meetings land on the same record as the LinkedIn thread?
  • Is there a pipeline with deals and stages?
  • How is sending paced, and who approves what goes out?

Honestly, every tool here that sends for you carries some risk on LinkedIn, ours included. Section 8.2 of the LinkedIn User Agreement bans "bots or other unauthorized automated methods," and members who use them "risk having their accounts restricted or shut down" (LinkedIn Help).

How a LinkedIn lead becomes one CRM record

Surprisingly, much of our work on joining outreach to the CRM went into deciding when two records are the same person. A campaign reaches people who may already be in your CRM from a demo request or last year's import.

If the tool makes a fresh contact for every lead, your reps end up with two records and half the history on each. Our matching rules exist to prevent that.

These are the rules we settled on, and the same questions are worth putting to any vendor:

  • Search results stay search results until you keep one, so a Scout list of 500 leads adds only the people you choose.
  • We match a person first by the ID our data source gives their profile, then by LinkedIn address, because two people can share a name.
  • We create a company only once its web domain is known, so firms with similar names stay apart.
  • New data fills empty fields and leaves anything a teammate typed exactly as it was.

When to keep your CRM and add a LinkedIn outreach tool

If your team already runs HubSpot, Salesforce or Pipedrive well, you may not need a new CRM at all. An outreach tool that syncs into it keeps each deal where your reps already work. La Growth Machine, lemlist, HeyReach and Closely are built that way, and Linked Helper, Waalaxy, Dripify and Meet Alfred from Multi-Channel up also pass contacts to a CRM.

Realistically, a sync is the faster route when your team already lives in its CRM. FAQs

What is the best LinkedIn outreach tool with a CRM?

Omniscient fits teams that want approved LinkedIn outreach and a full CRM that fills itself in from email and calendar. NetHunt CRM suits Gmail teams that want email and LinkedIn steps in one workflow, Kanbox and Meet Alfred suit smaller teams that want a simple pipeline, and La Growth Machine or lemlist suit teams that keep HubSpot or Pipedrive.

Do LinkedIn automation tools include a CRM?

Some do, at different depths. Kanbox has Kanban pipelines, Meet Alfred has tags, notes and pipeline stages, Linked Helper has notes and tags, and Dripify has a dashboard and notes on each lead. Omniscient and NetHunt CRM go further and keep deals and email history on the same record.

Can I use a LinkedIn outreach tool with HubSpot or Salesforce instead?

Yes. La Growth Machine syncs natively with HubSpot and Pipedrive on Ultimate, lemlist and Closely connect to HubSpot, Salesforce and Pipedrive, and HeyReach syncs to HubSpot. Test that replies land on the right existing contact before you launch.

Can HubSpot send LinkedIn messages automatically?

HubSpot sequences handle LinkedIn as tasks. A connection request or InMail task reminds a rep to send it by hand in Sales Navigator, and sequences need a Sales Hub Professional or Enterprise seat.

Is LinkedIn automation allowed?

Section 8.2 of LinkedIn's User Agreement bans bots and other unauthorized automated methods, and LinkedIn says members who use them risk having their accounts restricted. Careful pacing lowers that risk, and no tool can remove it.

How much does a LinkedIn outreach tool with a CRM cost?

Kanbox Starter costs $12 a month billed yearly and Linked Helper Standard costs $15 a license a month, while HubSpot's sequences need Sales Hub Professional at $90 a seat a month billed yearly. Omniscient starts at $19 per seat a month, billed yearly.
