You run outreach on LinkedIn, and someone on your team still copies every reply that matters into the CRM by hand. A LinkedIn outreach tool with a CRM inside promises to end that, so the LinkedIn conversation and the deal sit on one record.

We build Omniscient, which does both, so we've spent a lot of time on the join between sending a message and remembering what came of it. Below are the 15 tools we'd shortlist in 2026, with today's prices and a plain note on how much CRM each one really includes.

Prices come from each vendor's own pricing page in November 2026. We didn't run campaigns in every tool, so we compare what each vendor says against what we learned building ours.

If you're short on time, here's where we'd start:

  • Pick Omniscient if you want LinkedIn outreach you approve and a CRM that fills itself in from email and calendar.
  • Pick NetHunt CRM if your team works in Gmail and wants email and LinkedIn steps in one sequence.
  • Pick Kanbox or Meet Alfred if you want a simple LinkedIn pipeline next to your campaigns.
  • Pick Linked Helper if you want the lowest price per LinkedIn account and don't mind a desktop app.
  • Pick Breakcold, folk or Salesflare if your team writes its LinkedIn messages by hand and wants the CRM to keep up.
  • Pick HubSpot Sales Hub if HubSpot is already your CRM and LinkedIn steps as reminders are enough.
  • Pick La Growth Machine or lemlist if you keep HubSpot or Pipedrive and want automated LinkedIn steps synced into it.
  • Pick HeyReach if you run outreach from many LinkedIn accounts at once.

Four kinds of LinkedIn outreach tool with a CRM

Every tool here mentions a CRM, and the word covers very different things. Reading the vendors' pages side by side, we sorted them into four kinds.

  • Outreach and CRM in one product. The tool sends your invitations and messages, and the same record holds deals, email and meetings. Omniscient and NetHunt CRM work this way.
  • LinkedIn automation with a light CRM. You get tags, notes or pipeline stages on each LinkedIn contact, and a full deal pipeline with email history usually lives in another tool. Kanbox, Meet Alfred, Linked Helper, Dripify and Waalaxy sit here.
  • A CRM that holds LinkedIn contacts. The record comes first, and LinkedIn contacts or conversations flow into it. Breakcold, folk, Salesflare and HubSpot Sales Hub work like this, and none of their pricing pages lists automated invitation sequences.
  • Outreach synced into the CRM you keep. The tool sends on LinkedIn and pushes contacts and messages into HubSpot, Salesforce or Pipedrive. La Growth Machine, lemlist, HeyReach and Closely take this route.

Which kind you need depends on where your deals close. When most deals close in LinkedIn messages and one call, a light CRM can be enough. When buyers move on to email and meetings, you'll want the first kind, or a full CRM with an outreach tool synced into it.

How we chose these LinkedIn outreach tools

A LinkedIn outreach tool sends invitations and messages from your account and follows up when people accept. For this list we kept tools that also store contacts, notes or deals inside the product, plus the outreach tools that sync every message into the CRM you already run. Then we read each one's homepage, pricing page and help pages.

We judged the CRM part on the questions we had to answer when we joined our own sending engine to our CRM:

  • Does a LinkedIn lead become one record, or a second copy of someone you already know?
  • Does a reply stop that person's outreach in every campaign?
  • Do email and meetings land on the same record as the LinkedIn thread?
  • Is there a pipeline with deals and stages?
  • How is sending paced, and who approves what goes out?

Honestly, every tool here that sends for you carries some risk on LinkedIn, ours included. Section 8.2 of the LinkedIn User Agreement bans "bots or other unauthorized automated methods," and LinkedIn's prohibited software page says members who use them "risk having their accounts restricted or shut down."

The 15 best LinkedIn outreach tools with a CRM in 2026

01Omniscient

Omniscient is a CRM and LinkedIn outreach software that writes a message for each lead and sends only text your team has approved. The CRM fills itself in from Gmail or Outlook and your calendar, and once a lead's email address is on the record, the LinkedIn thread sits next to their emails and meetings.

We built both halves around one record, so when you save a Scout lead to a list or add it to a campaign, Omniscient looks for that person first. It creates a new record only when there's no match.

The outreach side works like this:

  • Scout builds a lead list from a sentence you type and scores each lead out of 100. Up to 60 points come from fit and 40 from signals that it's a good time to reach out.
  • Otto, our AI agent, drafts each person's messages from your template, and you approve them person by person or all at once when you launch.
  • A reply stops that person in every campaign, and an opt out keeps them out of later imports too.
  • The Inbox shows each thread with the person's company and campaign, and turns a reply into an opportunity at the stage you pick.

Sending follows the pacing we describe in how to automate LinkedIn outreach without getting restricted:

  • A new account starts at 5 invitations a day and adds 2 a day until it reaches your limit.
  • Each day's total lands somewhere between 80% and 100% of that limit.
  • If fewer than 1 in 5 of a week's invitations get accepted, counted once at least 20 have gone out, or LinkedIn rate limits the account, it drops to half pace for 3 days, then eases back to full speed over the next 3.

LinkedIn doesn't publish its invitation limit, and its help page only says that reaching it may restrict your invitations, typically for one week. That's why new senders start at 15 invitations a day and 80 a week, and the settings stop at 40 a day and 200 a week.

Omniscient is LinkedIn only, with no cold email steps, so a team that wants email and LinkedIn in one sequence will do better with NetHunt CRM, Meet Alfred or lemlist. If you rotate dozens of accounts for clients, an agency tool such as HeyReach will suit you better.

Paid seats start at $19 per seat a month, billed yearly, and the pipeline board and forecast come with Pro at $49. Teammates who only view or log data are free, and the trial lasts 14 days with no credit card. The CRM page and the LinkedIn Outbound page show both halves.

02NetHunt CRM

NetHunt CRM is a sales CRM software that works inside Gmail and Google Workspace and runs sequences that mix email with LinkedIn steps. Its sequences page lists connecting with prospects, withdrawing connection requests and sending messages on the "Professional Network," with delays you set between actions.

  • Basic costs $24 per user a month, billed yearly, or $30 billed monthly, and covers leads, deals and multiple pipelines.
  • Multichannel sequences and the Professional Network integration come with Business, at $60 per user a month billed monthly, with yearly pricing on request.
  • The free trial lasts 14 days with every feature except custom SMTP.

Interestingly, NetHunt's pages say "Professional Network" where you'd expect the word LinkedIn, and they let you choose which actions run automatically. Before you plan a campaign around it, confirm in the trial exactly which LinkedIn steps send on their own. NetHunt fits a team that already sells from Gmail and wants a full pipeline next to its outreach.

03Kanbox

Kanbox is a LinkedIn automation software that combines lead imports, automated sequences, a smart inbox and Kanban pipelines. Its pricing FAQ says leads move between pipeline columns on their own as they progress through a campaign.

  • Starter costs $12 a month, billed yearly, with one campaign running at a time and 300 lead imports a month.
  • Pro costs $50 per LinkedIn account a month billed yearly, with 5 campaigns at once, several accounts in one team, and webhooks and an API.
  • The free trial lasts 15 days, needs no credit card and includes Starter's features.

Its pricing page says campaigns run in the cloud around the clock without your computer. We didn't find named CRM integrations on its site, so Kanbox fits best when it's the only pipeline you plan to keep.

04Meet Alfred

Meet Alfred is a LinkedIn automation software that runs sequences across LinkedIn, email and X from the cloud. Its pricing page lists an Advanced CRM with "tags, notes and pipeline stages on every connection, synced with your inbox."

  • Individual covers LinkedIn only and costs $29 per user a month, billed yearly, and Business adds email and X at $49.
  • Teams costs $39 per user a month billed yearly and adds native Salesforce and HubSpot, which Individual and Business leave out.
  • Its homepage offers a 7 day free trial with no credit card.

Thankfully, the FAQ on Meet Alfred's homepage says outreach runs from its cloud with no browser extension, and you can adjust daily limits and activity windows. It suits a small team that wants a pipeline view of its LinkedIn contacts before paying for a separate CRM.

05Linked Helper

Linked Helper is a LinkedIn automation software that runs as a desktop app on your own computer or a VPS. Its Standard plan includes the Linked Helper CRM, with custom notes and tagging, plus an inbox "for managing chats without switching to LinkedIn."

  • Standard costs $15 per LinkedIn account a month on a monthly term, and the pricing page shows the local version from $8.25 a month on longer terms.
  • Native integrations include HubSpot, Salesforce and Pipedrive, though Standard caps sending contacts to your CRM at 20 a day and sending message history needs Pro.
  • The trial gives 14 days of full Pro access with no card, and a license simply runs out unless you turn on auto renewal yourself.

Luckily for anyone on a tight budget, it has the lowest price per account on this list. Its pricing page doesn't mention a deal pipeline, so you'd likely pair it with a CRM through those integrations.

06Dripify

Dripify is a LinkedIn automation software that runs drip campaigns of LinkedIn and email steps from the cloud. Its pricing page gives every plan a small CRM section: a central dashboard, notes on each lead, imports and exports, and the option to resume a lead in a sequence.

  • Pro costs $79 per user a month billed monthly, or $59 billed yearly, with email steps in the same sequence and a dedicated inbox.
  • Advanced costs $99 per user a month billed monthly, or $79 yearly, and adds lead tagging, team management and A/B tests.
  • A HubSpot integration comes with Pro, next to webhooks, Zapier and an open API.
  • The trial lasts 7 days with Advanced features and no credit card.

Its pricing page lists up to 75 connection requests a day on Pro and Advanced. Our own settings stop at 40 a day, so we'd start a new account far below that and raise it slowly. Dripify fits a small team that wants notes on each lead today and a HubSpot sync once deals pile up.

07Waalaxy

Waalaxy is a LinkedIn prospecting software that runs invitation and message sequences from the cloud with monthly invitation quotas. Pro includes CRM synchronization, and the plan comparison lists CRM sync with "2000+ tools," duplicate prevention across your team and smart reply detection.

  • Pro costs €19 per user a month with 300 invitations a month, and the page offers monthly, quarterly and yearly terms without saying which one that price uses.
  • Cold email sequences start on Business, at €69 per user a month.
  • The LinkedIn Inbox costs €20 a month extra and adds tags, templates, scheduled follow ups and CRM export, with your first 500 conversations free.
  • Each plan comes with a 14 day free trial.

The pricing page doesn't describe a CRM of Waalaxy's own beyond those Inbox tags. It suits a founder who already keeps a CRM and wants simple LinkedIn outreach next to it. Because the quota counts per month, spread your campaigns across all four weeks.

08Breakcold

Breakcold is a CRM software that syncs LinkedIn, email, WhatsApp and Telegram conversations into one inbox and pipeline. Its pricing page says every plan has the full CRM and its AI assistant, Chief of Staff, and plans differ only by how much AI work is included.

  • X1 costs $99 a month, billed monthly, for 1 seat and 2 connected accounts, and each extra seat or account costs $10 a month.
  • Every plan lists reply drafts on every channel and a tasks queue where "you approve every send."
  • The trial lasts 14 days, and Breakcold asks for a card up front.

Its pricing page doesn't list automated LinkedIn invitation sequences. Breakcold fits a founder or small team who writes LinkedIn messages by hand and wants the pipeline to update itself afterwards.

09folk

folk is a CRM software for small teams that captures contacts from LinkedIn and Sales Navigator with its folkX Chrome extension. Standard costs $24 per member a month, billed yearly, or $30 billed monthly, and includes LinkedIn and Gmail templates.

  • Standard also syncs email, calendar and WhatsApp, with an allowance of 2,000 messages per member a month.
  • Email sequences start on Premium, at $48 per member a month billed yearly.
  • The free trial lasts 2 weeks with no credit card.

Frankly, folk is the gentlest step up from a spreadsheet on this list. Its pricing page doesn't list automated LinkedIn invitations, so your team sends those by hand and folk keeps the record.

10Salesflare

Salesflare is a CRM software for B2B teams that captures data from email and calendar and opens a sidebar inside LinkedIn. Its help article says the sidebar shows on profiles, company pages and messages, creates the account and contact for you, and lets you copy LinkedIn messages onto the timeline as notes.

  • Growth costs $29 per user a month, billed yearly, or $39 billed monthly, with the LinkedIn sidebar and email campaigns.
  • Workflows of several emails start on Pro, at $49 billed yearly.
  • The trial lasts 30 days with no credit card.

Its pricing page doesn't mention automated LinkedIn invitations, so those stay manual. Helpfully, the follow up can run as an email workflow from the same record once you've found the person's address. Salesflare suits a small B2B team that meets people on LinkedIn and closes over email.

11HubSpot Sales Hub

HubSpot Sales Hub is a CRM software whose sequences can include LinkedIn steps through the Sales Navigator integration. HubSpot's knowledge base describes them as tasks that remind you "to manually send a connection request" or an InMail, and Sales Navigator opens on the contact when you work the task.

  • Sequences need a Sales or Service seat on Professional or Enterprise.
  • Sales Hub Professional costs $90 per seat a month paid yearly, or $100 paid monthly, both on an annual commitment, plus a required $1,500 onboarding fee paid once.
  • LinkedIn actions need your own Sales Navigator subscription on top.
  • HubSpot's free CRM covers up to 2 users, without sequences.

Refreshingly, nothing here sends on LinkedIn by itself, so HubSpot runs no automation on your LinkedIn account. The catch is that a rep still clicks every invitation. HubSpot Sales Hub fits a company that already runs HubSpot and wants LinkedIn steps tracked on the same contact.

12La Growth Machine

La Growth Machine is a sales automation software that runs multichannel sequences on LinkedIn, email, calls and X, priced per sender identity. Its Ultimate plan syncs natively with HubSpot and Pipedrive, and the pricing FAQ says every action and message syncs to the contact's timeline.

  • Basic costs €60 per identity a month for LinkedIn and email.
  • Pro adds calls and a unified LinkedIn and email inbox at €120 per identity a month.
  • The trial lasts 14 days with no card, one identity and no custom sequences.

Oddly, the Ultimate card showed the same €120 as Pro when we checked, so confirm the Ultimate price before you budget for the CRM sync. Other CRMs connect through Zapier. La Growth Machine fits a team on HubSpot or Pipedrive whose reps call and email as often as they message.

13lemlist

lemlist is a sales engagement software that runs sequences across email, LinkedIn and calls. Its pricing page lists contact management and task management on every plan, plus CRM integrations with HubSpot, Salesforce and Pipedrive.

  • Multichannel, the plan with LinkedIn steps and calls, costs $109 per user a month billed monthly, or $87 billed yearly.
  • The Email plan costs $69 a month and leaves LinkedIn steps out.
  • Working in lemlist from inside your CRM is an Enterprise feature.
  • The FAQ mentions a 14 day free trial while a banner on the same page offers 30 days, so check which one you get.

lemlist keeps the deal in your CRM and the sequence in lemlist. We'd point a team to it when cold email leads and LinkedIn follows, with the pipeline already in HubSpot, Salesforce or Pipedrive.

14HeyReach

HeyReach is a LinkedIn automation software that runs outreach from many LinkedIn accounts at once, with one unified inbox for every conversation. HubSpot is the only CRM its pricing page names, next to Clay, Make and Zapier.

  • Growth costs $79 per sender a month billed monthly, or $64 billed yearly.
  • Agency costs $999 a month with white label included, and Unlimited costs $2,999 a month.
  • Email comes through native Instantly and Smartlead integrations.
  • The FAQ describes a 14 day trial with 3 LinkedIn accounts and no card, for a plan the page doesn't list, so check the terms.

HeyReach's pricing page doesn't describe a CRM of its own, so you'd pair it with HubSpot or another CRM. It fits agencies and teams that rotate many senders. With one or two senders, a tool with its own CRM, such as NetHunt CRM or Omniscient, saves you a second subscription.

15Closely

Closely is a LinkedIn and email outreach software that puts replies from both channels into one Smart Inbox. Its pricing page lists CRM integrations on every plan and links pages for Salesforce, Pipedrive, HubSpot and GoHighLevel.

  • Starter costs $65 a month billed monthly for 1 LinkedIn account, or $39 billed yearly, with unlimited email accounts.
  • Growth covers 3 LinkedIn accounts at $159 a month billed monthly and adds AI personalization, an email finder and lead scoring.
  • White label comes with every plan, and the page doesn't state the trial length, so check it at signup.

The pricing page doesn't describe a CRM or deal pipeline of Closely's own. Closely fits a small agency that wants its own brand on outreach and keeps each client's deals in that client's CRM.

How a LinkedIn lead becomes one CRM record

Surprisingly, much of our work on joining outreach to the CRM went into deciding when two records are the same person. A campaign reaches people who may already be in your CRM from a demo request or last year's import.

If the tool makes a fresh contact for every lead, your reps end up with two records and half the history on each, and our matching rules exist to prevent that.

These are the rules we settled on, and the same questions are worth asking any vendor:

  • Search results stay search results until you keep one, so a Scout list of 500 leads adds only the people you choose to keep.
  • A person is matched first by the ID our data source gives their profile and then by LinkedIn address, because two people can share a name.
  • A company is created only when its web domain is known, so firms with similar names stay apart.
  • New data fills empty fields and leaves anything a teammate typed exactly as it was.

When to keep your CRM and add a LinkedIn outreach tool

If your team already runs HubSpot, Salesforce or Pipedrive well, you may not need a new CRM at all. An outreach tool that syncs into it keeps each deal where your reps already work. La Growth Machine, lemlist, HeyReach and Closely are built that way, and Linked Helper, Waalaxy, Dripify and Meet Alfred's Teams plan also pass contacts on to a CRM.

Admittedly, a sync is the faster route when your team already lives in its CRM. Before you launch, check that a reply in the outreach tool stops the sequence and lands on the right existing contact. How to choose a LinkedIn outreach tool with a CRM

Pick the kind first, then use the free trial to test the places where sending meets the record.

  1. 01
    Add a lead you already have

    Put someone from your CRM in a test campaign and check that the tool finds their existing record.

  2. 02
    Reply from a second account

    Answer the first message and confirm the sequence stops for that person in every campaign.

  3. 03
    Look for email and meetings

    Send the same person an email and book a call, then see whether both appear next to the LinkedIn thread.

  4. 04
    Set your pacing

    Use a daily range, working hours in your time zone and a weekly cap, and confirm nothing goes out overnight.

  5. 05
    Decide who approves messages

    Choose whether someone checks each message or approves the whole campaign at launch.

  6. 06
    Turn a reply into a deal

    Create an opportunity from a reply and check that its stage and amount land in your pipeline.

FAQs

What is the best LinkedIn outreach tool with a CRM?

Omniscient fits teams that want approved LinkedIn outreach and a full CRM that fills itself in from email and calendar. NetHunt CRM suits Gmail teams that want email and LinkedIn steps together, Kanbox or Meet Alfred suit smaller teams that want a simple pipeline, and La Growth Machine or lemlist suit teams that keep HubSpot or Pipedrive.

Do LinkedIn automation tools include a CRM?

Some do, at different depths. Kanbox has Kanban pipelines, Meet Alfred has tags, notes and pipeline stages, Linked Helper has tags and notes, and Dripify has notes on each lead. Omniscient and NetHunt CRM go further and keep deals and email history on the same record.

Can I use a LinkedIn outreach tool with HubSpot or Salesforce instead?

Yes. La Growth Machine syncs natively with HubSpot and Pipedrive on Ultimate, and lemlist connects to HubSpot, Salesforce and Pipedrive. Test that replies land on the right existing contact before you launch.

Can HubSpot send LinkedIn messages automatically?

No. HubSpot sequences add LinkedIn connection requests and InMails as tasks that remind a rep to send them by hand through Sales Navigator. Sequences need a Sales Hub Professional or Enterprise seat, and LinkedIn actions need a Sales Navigator subscription.

Is LinkedIn automation allowed?

Section 8.2 of LinkedIn's User Agreement bans bots and other unauthorized automated methods, and LinkedIn says members who use them risk having their accounts restricted. Careful pacing lowers that risk, and no tool can remove it.

How much does a LinkedIn outreach tool with a CRM cost?

On the vendors' own pages today, entry prices in this guide run from $8.25 a month for Linked Helper's local version on longer terms to $109 per user a month for lemlist's Multichannel plan billed monthly, and HubSpot's sequences need Sales Hub Professional at $90 per seat a month with an annual commitment. Omniscient starts at $19 per seat a month, billed yearly.
