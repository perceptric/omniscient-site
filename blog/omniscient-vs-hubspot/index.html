Where HubSpot shines

HubSpot is a CRM platform that puts sales, marketing, service and website tools on one shared customer database, and Sales Hub is the part built for sales teams.

A few things stand out for a small team:

  • The free CRM covers up to two users and 1,000 contacts, free with no end date.
  • Breeze Assistant, HubSpot's AI helper, comes with the free CRM and can research companies and sum up records.
  • The mobile app is free on iOS and Android.

If your team runs newsletters and landing pages next to sales, HubSpot keeps all of that and your pipeline on one database.

Where Omniscient shines

Omniscient is an agentic CRM that runs a B2B team's whole sales motion, from finding the next customer to closing the deal and keeping the account, with the CRM's AI in the seat price. Otto, its built-in agent, does the busy work at every step. All of it runs on Omniscience, a shared memory of every customer conversation, and Otto works through skills, fixed playbooks for each job, so answers stay accurate and consistent.

Here's what that looks like from the first lead to the closed deal:

  • Find customers: Describe your buyer and Otto builds a lead list with work emails, finds the right people at a company or finds companies like your best customers. Website forms bring inbound leads straight in.
  • Capture everything: Every seat, free ones included, captures email and meetings from Gmail or Outlook onto the right person, company and deal.
  • Engage and meet: Replies waiting on you rise to the top, and Otto drafts follow-ups you send from your own inbox. Each meeting gets a prep brief before it, and notes, action items and suggested deal updates from Google Meet, Zoom and Teams after it.
  • Manage the pipeline: A pipeline board and forecast, custom objects and fields, and fields Otto fills with proof for you to accept.
  • Run the business: Ask Otto anything in the app, in Slack or by voice, and get an answer with the quote and date behind each fact. Manager mode adds deal reviews, rep scorecards and call coaching.
  • Automate and extend: Workflows run follow-ups and Slack alerts, and Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor read the same memory through MCP.

HubSpot covers ground we don't. Omniscient sends no marketing email and has no dialer or mobile app, and we're a newer product, with a shorter public track record than HubSpot's.

How each CRM fills in your records

HubSpot's data agent answers custom questions about a company or contact from your transcripts, emails, meetings and the web, and writes each answer to the record. Its smart properties cost $0.10 per answer, from Starter up.

In Omniscient, every email, meeting and call lands on the right company, person and deal first. Then Otto follows these rules:

  • After a call, he suggests stage, amount and next step changes, each with the quote it came from.
  • You accept or skip each suggestion, and nothing changes until you do.
  • Otto never overwrites a value on his own, including one your team typed.

We picked these rules because a wrong amount in a forecast does more harm than a blank one. With clean data, duplicates merge with their full history, missing work emails and company facts fill in, and you can undo any of it. Asking questions in HubSpot and Omniscient

HubSpot has Breeze Assistant, the data agent and agents that run on HubSpot Credits. Omniscient has one agent, Otto, who works at every step of the sales motion on top of Omniscience, the shared memory of every email, call, meeting and note on the account. Ask what a buyer said about pricing and each fact in the answer shows its quote and date, so you can check it before it reaches a forecast.

Otto answers through skills, each a set of fixed steps for one job, written in the Agent Skills format. You pick one with / in Ask, or Otto picks one from its description. These come ready:

  • Find leads: Describe your buyer and Otto builds a lead list with work emails, or finds people at a company and companies like your best customers.
  • Research an account: What your own emails, calls and notes say about a company, with the source of each fact.
  • Prep a meeting: A brief on who's coming and what's still open from the last talk.
  • Wrap up a call: Notes, action items and suggested deal updates, each with its quote.
  • Review the pipeline: Deals that need attention and why.

System skills are ours, Workspace skills are your team's, edited by owners and admins, and Personal skills are your own, written in plain words. Because every rep runs the same steps, the same question gets the same kind of answer each time.

Otto also uses your Knowledge, such as pricing and your playbook. Claude, ChatGPT and Cursor can read the same memory through our MCP server, and the Ask Otto page shows the rest.

AI and credits in HubSpot and Omniscient

HubSpot runs its AI agents on HubSpot Credits. Here is how HubSpot Credits work:

  • Credits cost $0.010 each, and capacity packs come as 1,000 credits for $10.
  • Paid plans include 500 credits a month on Starter, 3,000 on Professional and 5,000 on Enterprise.
  • The prospecting agent uses 100 credits a lead, and unused credits expire at the end of each month.

Omniscient includes the CRM's AI in the seat price. Ask Otto and his skills run on each paid seat, with a daily limit on questions for each person.

Moving from HubSpot to Omniscient

Omniscient's HubSpot importer reads your data and never writes back, so your HubSpot account stays exactly as it was. It brings over these records:

  • Companies become accounts, matched by domain, and contacts become people, matched by email.
  • Contacts still at the subscriber, lead or marketing qualified lead stage arrive as leads.
  • Pipelines keep their stages, and deals become opportunities with their amount, close date and owner.
  • Notes arrive as plain text on the deal, company or contact they belong to.

Plan around a few details before you run it:

  • Contacts without an email address are skipped unless they're still at a lead stage, so export any you want to keep first.
  • Owners are matched to teammates by email, so invite your team with the addresses they use in HubSpot.
  • Custom properties, workflows and sequences stay in HubSpot, and running the import again updates what's there without making doubles.

Which one should you choose

If you're still torn, ask how much of your pipeline comes from marketing email and how much of the sales motion you want one CRM and its agent to run.

Choose HubSpot if:

  • You want marketing email, landing pages and your CRM on one database.
  • Your reps book meetings through cold email in volume or calls and need a dialer.
  • Your team works from phones a lot and wants mobile apps.
  • You're two people on the free CRM, and it covers what you need.

Choose Omniscient if:

  • You want one CRM for the whole sales motion, from finding leads to closing deals, with Otto doing the busy work at each step.
  • Your deals move through emails and meetings, and you want prep briefs, call recaps, follow-up drafts and pipeline reviews in the same place as your records.
  • You want every step to draw on one shared memory and follow the same skills, so answers stay accurate and consistent.
  • You want AI in the seat price, with one number to plan around.

FAQs

Is Omniscient cheaper than HubSpot?

Omniscient's paid seats start at $19 a month billed yearly, and HubSpot Starter costs $7 a seat for new customers and $20 at list price. For a forecast, Omniscient Pro is $49 a seat billed yearly, and HubSpot Professional starts at $90 a seat plus a $1,500 onboarding fee paid once.

How is Ask Otto different from HubSpot's AI?

HubSpot's agents run on HubSpot Credits. Otto comes with each paid seat and works at every step, from lead lists to meeting recaps and pipeline reviews, on every email, call, meeting and note in Omniscience. His answers show the quote and date behind each fact, and skills keep them in the same shape every time.

Can I use Omniscient and HubSpot together?

Yes, while you compare them, since Omniscient reads your HubSpot data and never writes back to it. Plan to pick one as your main CRM when the trial ends.

Can I move my data from HubSpot to Omniscient?

Yes, Omniscient's importer brings over companies, contacts, pipelines, deals and notes, or Omniscient's team migrates them for you. Contacts without an email address are skipped unless they're still at a lead stage, and custom properties and workflows stay in HubSpot.

Does Omniscient send email like HubSpot?

Partly. Otto drafts single emails and replies that you send from your own Gmail or Outlook, and workflows send follow-ups from your team's own templates. Omniscient has no marketing email, which HubSpot covers in Marketing Hub.
