If you're weighing Attio vs HubSpot, you're choosing between a CRM you shape yourself and a platform that already has a hub for each team. Both start free, and both put email sequences on their second paid plan.

Prices are from each vendor's site in November 2026.

Here's our short answer:

  • Pick HubSpot if marketing brings in most of your leads and you want forms, ads, email campaigns and the sales pipeline on one shared record.
  • Pick Attio if your process doesn't fit a standard pipeline and you want to design your own objects, or if you sell mostly by email to people you know.
  • Look at Omniscient if you want one agentic CRM for your whole sales motion, from finding leads to closing deals, with Otto doing the busy work.

Where Attio shines

Attio is a flexible CRM that builds records from your email and calendar and lets you shape objects and fields around how your business works.

Here is how Attio's plans work:

  • Email and calendar sync come on every plan, and even Free fills in first interaction, last interaction and connection strength for each contact.
  • Objects grow with the plan, from 3 on Free to 5 on Plus and 12 on Pro, and custom objects and formula attributes start on Pro.
  • Workflows, AI agents, the API, webhooks, an App SDK and an MCP server come on every plan, Free included.

Attio is where we'd point a team whose process doesn't look like a standard sales pipeline, such as an investor tracking deal flow. It shines brightest when someone on your team enjoys designing objects and has time set aside for it.

Where HubSpot shines

HubSpot is a customer platform that runs marketing, sales and service tools on one shared CRM. It connects with over 2,000 apps.

Here is what each Sales Hub plan adds:

  • Starter adds 500 calling minutes, HubSpot's Notetaker for meetings and up to 50 workflows with a starter set of triggers and actions.
  • Professional adds sequences, forecasting, conversation intelligence, lead scoring and up to 300 workflows.
  • The mobile app comes on every plan, and teammates who only need to look can have view only seats at no extra cost.

We think HubSpot's biggest strength is that marketing and sales share one record. Each contact carries an Original Traffic Source, the first known web source that brought them in, and our HubSpot importer keeps it as the lead's source.

Teams that move up to Professional also pay a required $1,500 onboarding fee once, on top of the seats.

Data model: custom objects in Attio and HubSpot

Attio treats objects as something you design, starting from people, companies and deals. You then add your own, such as investors or partners, and link them the way your business works.

HubSpot starts from fixed objects that most sales teams share, like contacts, companies and deals. That shape fits a standard sales process from the first day.

The difference shows up clearly when you move data out of each tool:

  • HubSpot's deal stages come with a win probability for each stage, which a forecast can use right away.
  • Attio's deal stages arrive as a list of statuses with no win probability, so an importer reads the won stage from Attio's celebration setting and the lost one from its name.
  • HubSpot keeps leads and customers in one contact list sorted by lifecycle stage, so an importer brings contacts at a lead stage over as leads.

If your reps live on the phone, HubSpot is the better pick here, since calling minutes start on Starter.

LinkedIn outreach in Attio and HubSpot

HubSpot puts its LinkedIn steps inside sequences on Professional and Enterprise, and they need a Sales Navigator subscription of their own. They work as task reminders: completing one opens Sales Navigator on the contact's record so you can send the InMail or connection request yourself.

LinkedIn Sales Navigator is a prospecting tool from LinkedIn that adds advanced search filters and InMail to your account. Core and Advanced both offer a free trial to members without a paid LinkedIn plan who haven't had a free trial in the past year.

Attio teams that prospect on LinkedIn connect a separate tool through Attio's app marketplace instead.

lemlist is a sales engagement tool that runs email and LinkedIn steps in one campaign. Attio built the lemlist app, which adds Attio people to a campaign, and LinkedIn steps start on lemlist's Multichannel plan.

HeyReach is a LinkedIn outreach tool that sends from many accounts at once and charges per sender. Its Attio app, built by Neon Deer Data Labs, pushes Attio people into HeyReach campaigns. It can also log accepted invitations and replies on each person's timeline.

Whichever you pick, the LinkedIn tool is a second bill. LinkedIn can restrict accounts that use automation tools, so look at each tool's pacing settings before you connect an account.

On HubSpot, credits come with paid seats. Once a month's credits are used, features that use them pause until the next reset date, unless you add capacity.

Both let you set limits on credit use. HubSpot sets a limit per feature, and Attio's credits guide lets admins cap Ask Attio's share of the pool.

Attio vs HubSpot pricing

Both charge per seat, with lower prices for yearly billing. Here is how Attio's plans and HubSpot's Sales Hub plans compare:

What moves the bill more than the headline price:

  • HubSpot's $7 and $10 Starter prices are new customer discounts on a $20 list price, so ask what a seat costs at renewal.
  • HubSpot Professional asks for a yearly commitment even when you pay monthly, plus the $1,500 onboarding fee.
  • Attio's Plus plan covers up to 10 seats, so a team of 11 or more starts on Pro.

Moving between Attio and HubSpot

Attio's migration guide moves HubSpot data into any paid Attio plan through Import2, free for Attio users. It covers companies, people, deals, custom objects, notes, tasks and files.

Going the other way, HubSpot's free CRM includes CRM import. Two tips carry over to either move:

  • Most importers match deal owners by email address, so invite your team with the same emails before any import.
  • If your Attio workspace relies on custom objects, check your HubSpot plan first, since HubSpot puts them on Enterprise.

Attio vs HubSpot: which one to choose

Start with how your team usually sells:

Choose HubSpot if:

  • Your leads come from forms, ads and email campaigns, and marketing and sales should share one record.
  • Your reps make calls and want calling minutes and transcripts in the CRM.

Choose Attio if:

  • Your process needs objects of your own, like investors or partners.
  • You sell mostly by email to people you know and want sequences on Pro.

FAQs

Is Attio cheaper than HubSpot?

At the first paid plan, HubSpot costs less for new customers: Sales Hub Starter starts at $7 a seat billed yearly, with a $20 list price, while Attio Plus costs $35 billed yearly. At the plans with sequences, Attio Pro costs $79 billed yearly and HubSpot Professional costs $90, plus a $1,500 onboarding fee paid once.

Does Attio have a free trial?

Yes. Attio offers a 14 day trial of its Pro plan, and its Free plan covers up to 3 seats.

Which is better for a startup, Attio or HubSpot?

Attio fits a startup that wants to shape its own data model, and its startup program offers up to 80% off for eligible teams. HubSpot fits a startup whose leads come from marketing, and it runs its own HubSpot for Startups program.
