Many sales teams sell by phone, but their CRM was built for email. Reps jump between a dialer, an inbox and the CRM, and call notes end up in three places or nowhere.

Close is a sales CRM with calling, email and SMS built in, plus an AI assistant and agents that help keep the pipeline current. SDRs, account executives, founders and inside sales teams use it to work every lead from one screen. This review covers what Close is best at, its key features, its plans and usage fees, and who it fits.

By the end, you'll know whether Close fits your team and which plan to price. 📞

Is Close a good CRM?

Close is a good CRM for sales teams that sell by phone, email and SMS and want all three built into one fast tool. It fits inside sales teams, SDR teams and small businesses that make a lot of calls.

If you want a CRM that runs the whole sales motion for you on day one, from lead lists to closed deals, look at an agentic CRM like Omniscient too.

What Close is best at

Close is best at outbound and inside sales. Calling, email and SMS live inside the CRM, so reps work a list of leads without opening another tool.

Here is what stands out when you use it:

  • Calling built in. Reps call from the CRM, with call recording on every plan and dialers on higher plans.
  • One screen for every channel. Calls, emails and texts land on the same lead record.
  • A fast, focused interface. Smart Views turn your pipeline into lists of leads to work next.
  • AI that helps between calls. Chloe, Close's AI assistant, answers pipeline questions, preps calls and writes summaries.
  • Open to other tools. Close connects to more than 100 tools and offers an MCP server for AI assistants.

Close key features

Here are the parts of Close that sales teams use most, with links to Close's own pages.

Calling and dialers

Close has built-in calling on every plan, with call recording kept for 30 days on Solo and Essentials, 90 days on Growth and with no time limit on Scale. Essentials adds Local Presence, which shows a local number to the person you call.

The dialers grow with the plan:

  1. Growth: the Power Dialer calls through a list one lead after another, with voicemail drop.
  2. Scale: the Predictive Dialer, plus live call coaching.

Email and SMS

Every plan sends email and SMS from the CRM. Each user connects 3 email accounts on Solo and Essentials, and 10 on Growth and Scale. Growth adds bulk email, email scheduling, Send As and text scheduling.

Chloe and AI agents

Chloe, Close's AI assistant, answers questions from your own data, preps calls and creates tasks. The AI Notetaker records and summarizes calls and meetings on every plan.

Close also offers AI Agents, and its Voice Agent is live. Growth adds AI Playbooks, Auto Updates, AI email rewrite and Lead Summaries. Each plan includes AI credits: 500 a month on Solo, then 1,000, 1,500 and 2,000 per user a month on Essentials, Growth and Scale.

Workflows and automation

Growth adds Workflows, which send automated emails, texts and call tasks, including after hours. It also adds automated lead assignment and call and meeting outcomes.

Reporting and team controls

Every plan has reporting and dashboards. Essentials adds team performance reports, and Growth adds a leaderboard. Scale adds custom reports, custom objects, required fields, user groups and advanced roles and permissions.

Close pricing

Here is how Close's plans work:

Prices are from Close's site in November 2026.

  • Solo: $9 a month billed yearly, or $19 billed monthly, for 1 user and up to 10,000 leads.
  • Essentials: $35 per user a month billed yearly, or $49 billed monthly, with unlimited users and leads.
  • Growth: $99 per user a month billed yearly, or $109 billed monthly. Close marks it as the recommended plan.
  • Scale: $139 per user a month billed yearly, or $149 billed monthly.
  • Enterprise: talk to Close's sales team.

Close has no free plan. Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial with no credit card, and Close offers a 30-day money-back guarantee.

Usage fees and add-ons

Calling and SMS usage are billed on top of the plan price. Here is what to budget for:

  • Calls: most outbound destinations cost around $0.02 a minute, and rates vary by country.
  • Phone numbers: from about $1 a month per number.
  • SMS: charged per message sent and received, by country.
  • Call Assistant: $50 a month per account, plus $0.02 per recorded minute.
  • Extra organizations: $50 a month each on Solo and Essentials, with 1 included on Growth and Scale.

What a team of 5 pays

A team of 5 on yearly billing pays $175 a month on Essentials, $495 on Growth and $695 on Scale, before calling and SMS usage.

Most phone-first teams price Close at Growth, since the Power Dialer and Workflows start there.

Who Close is for

Close fits teams that sell by talking to people. Here is who gets the most from it:

  • Inside sales and SDR teams. Dialers, Local Presence and call recording keep reps on the phone.
  • Small businesses that make a lot of calls. One tool covers calling, email and texting.
  • Founders doing their own sales. Solo covers 1 user for $9 a month on yearly billing.
  • Sales managers who coach. Scale adds live call coaching and custom reports.

When teams look beyond Close

Teams usually look beyond Close when they want something new from their CRM. Here are the most common goals:

  • One CRM that runs the whole sales motion. An agent that finds leads, preps meetings, recaps calls and keeps deals current from day one.
  • AI in the seat price. One number to plan around, with no credit pools or usage fees to watch.
  • A deeper account memory. Every email, call and meeting on one shared record that agents can read.
  • Meetings more than calls. A CRM built around video meetings and email for teams that rarely dial.

Here are the tools we'd look at for each goal:

  • Pick Omniscient if you want one agentic CRM for your whole sales motion, from finding leads to closing deals, with Otto doing the busy work.
  • Pick HubSpot if you want sales and marketing on one database.
  • Pick Attio if you want to design your own objects and agents.
  • Pick Salesflare if you want a simple sales CRM that fills itself in.

FAQs

How much does Close CRM cost?

Close CRM costs $9 a month for 1 user on Solo, billed yearly, or $19 billed monthly. Essentials is $35 per user a month billed yearly, Growth $99 and Scale $139. Calling and SMS usage are billed on top, with most calls around $0.02 a minute and phone numbers from about $1 a month.

Does Close CRM have a free plan?

No, Close CRM has no free plan. Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial with no credit card required, and Close offers a 30-day money-back guarantee. Solo, at $9 a month billed yearly, is the lowest-cost way to use it after the trial.

Does Close CRM have a power dialer?

Yes, Close CRM has a Power Dialer on the Growth plan, which calls through a list one lead after another, with voicemail drop. Scale adds a Predictive Dialer and live call coaching. Every plan includes built-in calling and call recording.

Does Close CRM have AI?

Yes, Close CRM has Chloe, an AI assistant that answers pipeline questions, preps calls and writes summaries, plus an AI Notetaker on every plan. Growth adds AI Playbooks, Auto Updates and Lead Summaries. Each plan includes monthly AI credits, from 500 on Solo to 2,000 per user on Scale.

Which CRM is recommended for teams that need more than Close's calling tools?

For teams that need more than Close's calling tools, we'd recommend Omniscient. It is one agentic CRM for the whole sales motion, with Otto finding leads, prepping meetings and keeping deals current, and AI in every paid seat from $19 a month billed yearly. Teams that mainly want sales and marketing together may prefer HubSpot.
