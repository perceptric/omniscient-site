If you run an agency, your LinkedIn outreach happens in two places: inside your clients' accounts, and inside your own, where you win the next client. Twelve of the 13 LinkedIn automation tools for agencies below are built for the first job, with client workspaces, an agency plan or an agency program. Omniscient, the one we make, fits the second.

We built the LinkedIn sending engine inside Omniscient, so we judged each tool by the settings we had to get right ourselves, such as each account's own working hours and warmup.

Prices and plans come from each vendor's own site, read in November 2026. We haven't run client campaigns in every tool, so we compare their stated settings with the engine we built.

Here's the short version, sorted by the agency work you do:

  • Your agency's own new business, with the CRM and proposals in one place: Omniscient.
  • Many client senders with a flat agency price and white label: HeyReach.
  • Client campaigns that mix LinkedIn with cold email: Skylead or GetSales.io.
  • Clients who want their own branded logins and reports: Expandi, Closely or Meet Alfred.
  • A separate team per client and a revenue share on each one: Dripify.
  • A client list that changes every month: Dux-Soup, which bills only the accounts you run.
  • The lowest starting price per client account: Linked Helper, or Waalaxy if you'd rather stay in the cloud.

What agencies need from LinkedIn automation tools

A sales team usually runs a handful of LinkedIn accounts. An agency runs at least one for every client, so a setting you get wrong is wrong on every account at once.

Before you compare prices, check that a tool covers these:

  • A separate space for each client, so leads, campaigns and replies never mix.
  • Pacing that belongs to each LinkedIn account, with that account's own time zone and working hours.
  • One inbox where you can filter replies by client and by sender.
  • A way for clients to connect their own accounts without handing you a password.
  • White label and client reports, if clients log in or you resell the tool.
  • A pricing model that still makes sense at 10, 25 or 50 senders.

Frankly, white label is the easiest item to spot on agency pricing pages, but it only changes what clients see, while pacing and client separation protect each account.

Whose LinkedIn account is it when you run outreach for clients

We'd settle this before any trial, since it shapes your client contract. LinkedIn's User Agreement says in section 2.2 that "You will not share your account with anyone else."

Section 8.2 adds that members agree not to "use or attempt to use another's account (such as sharing log-in credentials or copying cookies)." The same section bans "bots or other unauthorized automated methods" for adding contacts and sending messages.

Every tool on this list automates the actions that rule covers, ours included, and the account at risk belongs to your client. Honestly, no setting removes the risk, so the best you can do is keep it small and agreed in writing:

  • Ask each client to connect their own account through the tool's sign in link, so the password never passes through your team.
  • Get a written OK that names the account, the daily limits and who answers replies.
  • Agree on what happens when the contract ends, including who keeps the conversations.
  • Keep each client's sender in that client's own space, so their leads never land in another client's lists.

In Omniscient, the connect link works once, expires after 15 minutes and is tied to one workspace and one person. Disconnecting a sender cancels its queued invitations and messages, and its conversations stay in the Inbox.

The 13 best LinkedIn automation tools for agencies

01Omniscient

Omniscient is a CRM and LinkedIn outreach software that writes a message for each lead and sends only text your team has approved. For an agency, it fits the outreach that wins your own clients.

Each connected LinkedIn account, which we call a sender, keeps its own settings:

  • Its own time zone and working hours, with a lunch break and start and end times that move by up to 20 minutes a day.
  • A pace of Careful, Normal, Fast or Custom, capped at 40 invitations a day and 200 a week.
  • A warmup that starts a new or reconnected account at 5 invitations a day and adds 2 a day.
  • A manual mode that turns its queue into a list you send by hand.

Replies from every sender land in one Inbox that you can filter by sender and by campaign, with the person's CRM record beside the thread. A reply stops that person's outreach in every campaign. When a reply turns into a deal, Otto, our AI agent, can write the proposal in Deal Room.

Omniscient sends on LinkedIn only and has no agency dashboard, white label or client reports. You can belong to several workspaces, but a client's workspace comes from that client inviting you, so for outreach on behalf of 20 clients, HeyReach, Skylead or GetSales.io will suit you better.

Paid seats start at $19 per seat a month, billed yearly, and the free trial lasts 14 days with no credit card. The LinkedIn Outbound page has the full tour.

02HeyReach

HeyReach is a LinkedIn automation software that runs outreach from many LinkedIn accounts at once and rotates sending between them. Its pricing FAQ says you pay for senders, while teammates, VAs and clients aren't charged.

  • Growth costs $79 per sender a month and includes workspaces and permissions, so each client's data stays separate under one login.
  • Agency costs $999 a month for 25 senders and adds white label with your own domain and branding. A 50 sender version is listed too, so ask HeyReach for its price.
  • Unlimited costs $2,999 a month for up to 300 senders.
  • Growth includes a residential proxy per sender, while Agency and Unlimited ask you to bring your own.
  • Email runs through native integrations with Instantly and Smartlead.

Plan for the proxies before you move up, because on the agency bundles every client sender needs a proxy you source and look after yourself.

Thankfully, you can test it on real accounts first. The Growth trial covers up to 3 LinkedIn accounts for 14 days with no card, and the FAQ describes a trial of 30 days for Agency and Unlimited on request. HeyReach fits an agency that already runs many client senders and wants them all under one login. Our HeyReach alternatives guide compares it with the rest.

03Expandi

Expandi is a LinkedIn automation software that runs outreach sequences from the cloud, with a dedicated, country based IP address for each account. Business costs $99 a month, or $79 a month billed yearly, and the free trial lasts 7 days.

  • New profiles warm up automatically at a pace you can set.
  • Smart sequences mix LinkedIn actions with email steps that branch on what each person does.
  • The Agency plan starts at 10 seats and is priced on request.
  • The pricing page lists "Use Expandi under your own brand" and "Transparent reports for clients."
  • Its agency page adds custom roles and permissions "for co-workers and clients."

Two things slow an agency down here. With no public agency price, you can't work out your margin until after a sales call. And 7 days is short for a warmup: ours takes 5 days just to climb from 5 to 15 invitations a day.

Expandi fits an agency with 10 or more seats whose clients log in and expect branded reports.

04Skylead

Skylead is a LinkedIn automation and cold email software that builds sequences across both channels, with unlimited email accounts on every seat. Its Agency plan costs $999 a month for 25 seats or $1,999 for unlimited seats, listed as a promotion for the first year.

  • Agency adds one dashboard for every client and seat, plus a Unibox for all client conversations.
  • It also lists client roles and permissions, risk alerts, unlimited agency teammates and a white label option.
  • A single seat costs $100 a month and includes up to 100,000 emails a month, with a free trial of 7 days.

Unlimited email accounts on each seat help when every client brings their own sending domains. Before you price a year of client work on the Agency plan, though, ask Skylead what it costs once the first year ends. Skylead fits an agency that sells LinkedIn and cold email as one service.

05GetSales.io

GetSales.io is a LinkedIn and email outreach software that charges per LinkedIn account and per inbox, built for agencies and GTM teams. LinkedIn costs $69 a month for 1 account or $195 a month for 5, and the page says the price falls to "$39 to $10 per account at scale."

  • Its homepage describes each client getting "Its own workspace, its own LinkedIn and email accounts, and its own inbox."
  • Each LinkedIn account runs on a managed ISP proxy with a gradual ramp, and the email module gives each client a dedicated server and IP.
  • White label is included in the Business setup, which the page prices from $500 a month in resources, and the trial lasts 7 days.

Interestingly, the pricing page lists no fee per user, only per LinkedIn account and per email inbox. That suits an agency where several of your people touch each client.

The managed proxy also saves you a job that HeyReach's agency bundles leave to you. GetSales.io fits an agency that wants every client fully walled off, down to the email server.

06Closely

Closely is a LinkedIn and email outreach software that writes AI personalized messages and sends them in one sequence across both channels. Starter costs $65 a month, or $39 a month billed yearly, for one LinkedIn account.

  • Growth costs $159 a month for 3 LinkedIn accounts and Essential costs $259 for 5, both with monthly credits for email finding and AI personalization.
  • Every plan lists unlimited email accounts and white label with your own domain, colors and logo, and the FAQ says setup is free.
  • Its homepage says clients "see your logo, your domain (app.youragency.com), your branding everywhere," and lists client portal access and branded reporting.
  • LinkedIn and email replies land in one inbox.

That makes it the cheapest way into white label on this list, since the other tools that list it keep it for agency plans, larger setups or partner programs.

A few details are missing from the site, though. It mentions client portal access without describing a separate workspace per client, and the FAQ names a fixed price of $1,499 a month however many seats you manage without saying how it relates to the plans. The trial length isn't stated either, so ask about all three before you sign. Closely fits a small agency whose clients log in and should only ever see your brand.

07Meet Alfred

Meet Alfred is a LinkedIn automation software that runs sequences on LinkedIn, email and X from the cloud. Its Agency plan (Teams in one comparison table) starts at 3 seats and gives you "Separate workspaces per client or squad, with their own leads and campaigns."

  • It costs $39 per user a month, billed yearly, and includes email steps.
  • A shared inbox lets your team assign threads and leave internal notes.
  • White label lets you "Run several client brands from one account, each with its own look and domain."
  • The homepage advertises a free trial of 7 days with no credit card to sign up.

A minimum of 3 seats is low for an agency plan with white label, so you can start with your first few clients. The X steps are a bonus if a client's buyers are active there. Meet Alfred fits a small agency that wants client workspaces and its own brand without a big monthly bundle.

08Dripify

Dripify is a LinkedIn automation software that runs drip campaigns of LinkedIn and email steps from its own servers. Pro costs $79 per user a month, or $59 billed yearly, and each seat connects one LinkedIn account.

  • Advanced costs $99, or $79 billed yearly, and adds team management, so "one administrator can oversee the whole team from a single login."
  • Advanced also lets you assign roles, track each member's results and prevent duplicate outreach.
  • Its agency partner program offers "One Dripify account, a separate team per client," each with its own members, roles, templates and metrics.
  • Partners earn up to 35% recurring revenue share on the clients they bring.
  • The 7 day trial opens the Advanced plan and needs no credit card.

The pages don't mention white label, so your clients will see Dripify. Duplicate prevention is worth a test in your trial: check whether it works across client teams or only inside one, since two clients can sell to the same buyers.

Dripify fits an agency that's happy to run clients on Dripify's brand and earn a share for it. Our Dripify alternatives guide covers its limits in more depth.

09SalesRobot

SalesRobot is a LinkedIn and cold email outreach software that runs in the cloud with a dedicated IP for each LinkedIn account, and it charges per account:

  • Basic costs $59 a month, or $39 billed yearly, with one active campaign.
  • Advanced costs $79, or $59 billed yearly, and adds unlimited campaigns, A/B testing and an inbox.
  • Professional costs $99, or $79 billed yearly, and adds team management.
  • Its agency page describes one dashboard where you run every client's campaigns and inboxes.
  • White label runs through its partner program, and the free trial lasts 14 days with no credit card.

Its homepage says "Our AI replies on your behalf instantly." If you switch that on for a client, agree first on which replies a person reads before anything goes out, since every answer carries your client's name.

SalesRobot fits an agency that wants to resell outreach under its own brand and keep billing per client account.

10Dux-Soup

Dux-Soup is a LinkedIn automation software that runs drip campaigns either as a desktop app or on its own servers. STACKED local costs $55 per seat a month, STACKED cloud costs $99, and yearly billing takes 25% off both.

  • Its agency page says you can "Add new client accounts in minutes and remove them just as easily," with one agency dashboard and a shared inbox.
  • The same page says "Metered billing means you only pay for the accounts you're actively running."
  • Campaigns can be shared across a team on multi-account plans of 2 or more seats.
  • Native integrations include Salesforce, Slack, Zapier and Make.

Fortunately for agencies with churn, metered billing means a client who leaves mid-month stops costing you. For client work, plan on the cloud plan, because the local one runs only while that machine is on.

The pricing page doesn't list email steps or white label, and its agency page lists a free trial of 14 days. Dux-Soup fits an agency whose client list changes from month to month.

11La Growth Machine

La Growth Machine is a sales automation software that runs sequences on LinkedIn, email, calls and X, billed per identity. On monthly billing, Basic costs €60 per identity a month for up to 3 identities on LinkedIn and email, and Pro costs €120 with no limit on identities and adds calls.

  • Each client sender counts as one identity, so the bill follows your number of senders.
  • The Agency plan needs at least 6 identities, is priced through sales and adds onboarding sessions and discounted plans.
  • The free trial covers one identity for 14 days with no card.

Calls set it apart here: if your service includes phone follow ups, Pro puts them in the same sequence as the LinkedIn and email steps.

Client separation is harder to judge, because the pricing page doesn't mention white label or separate client workspaces. Ask how clients stay apart before you add a second one. La Growth Machine fits an agency that sells multichannel campaigns and runs the client view itself.

12Waalaxy

Waalaxy is a LinkedIn outreach software that sends invitations and follow ups automatically, with a monthly invitation quota on every plan. Pro costs €19 per user a month for 300 invitations a month, Advanced costs €49 for 800, and Business costs €69 and adds cold email.

  • Its agencies page says "each client gets their own dedicated account within your workspace, so data stays fully separated."
  • You switch between your team's Waalaxy accounts in one click, without logging in and out of their LinkedIn profiles.
  • The Team section tracks activity across all accounts and exports results for the whole team.
  • Discounts start at 5 licenses, and the Team plan comes with a free trial of 14 days.

On white label, the page is direct: Waalaxy doesn't offer it. Its LinkedIn Inbox is also an extra €20 a month, and 300 invitations a month on Pro is about 15 a working day per client.

Waalaxy fits a small agency starting with a few clients on a tight budget, where clients only ever see your reports. Our Waalaxy alternatives guide lists what to move to later.

13Linked Helper

Linked Helper is a LinkedIn automation software that runs as a desktop app on your own computer or on a server you control. Standard costs $15 per LinkedIn account a month, less on 3, 6 or 12 month terms, and volume discounts start at 10 licenses.

  • Each client can have a separate workspace with its own data, accounts and permissions, and clients can join as view only guests.
  • One license runs one LinkedIn account, and every extra workspace needs at least one license.
  • Automation runs only while that computer or rented server is on.
  • The trial gives 14 days of Pro with no card.

Luckily for agencies watching their margins, it has the lowest starting price per account on this list. The trade is upkeep: someone on your team keeps that server running for every client.

Linked Helper fits an agency with a technical person who'd rather manage a server than pay cloud prices.

Running many client accounts with LinkedIn automation

LinkedIn doesn't publish its invitation limit, and its help page says only that an account that reaches it "may be temporarily restricted from sending invitations." That restriction "typically lasts one week," which for a client means a week of paused outreach you'll need to explain.

Across 20 client accounts, small settings add up, so we built these lessons into Omniscient's engine and would look for each one in any trial:

  • Warm up every new or reconnected account. Ours starts at 5 invitations a day and adds 2 a day until it reaches that account's limit.
  • Let each day's cap move. Ours draws it fresh every day between 80% and 100% of the limit, so no two days look alike.
  • Send in the client's hours and time zone, even when your team works from another one.
  • Slow down when the signals turn. Ours runs at half pace for 3 days, then eases back over 3 more, when LinkedIn rate limits the account or fewer than 20% of last week's invitations were accepted. It judges acceptance only after 20 invitations, so a few ignored invites on day one never slow a new account.
  • View the profile 1 to 4 hours before inviting, as a person would.
  • Never retry a send that may have gone through. A missing message is easy to fix by hand, and a doubled one can't be taken back.

Understandably, the warmup matters more in agency work, because you rarely know how a client's account was used before it reached you.

If two clients sell to the same buyers, watch for overlap between them. Two of your senders can invite the same person in the same week, since each client's workspace keeps its own lists.

How to choose a LinkedIn automation tool for your agency

First decide whether clients will log in or only see your reports. If they log in, white label and client roles move up your list, and if not, spend your trial on pacing and the inbox.

Then run these checks in the first week of any trial:

  1. 01
    Count your senders

    Estimate how many client accounts you'll run in six months, and price each tool at that number.

  2. 02
    Connect a real client account

    Go through the flow from the client's side and confirm no password changes hands.

  3. 03
    Set two time zones

    Give two senders different working hours and confirm each one sends only in its own.

  4. 04
    Reply from a test account

    Check that a reply stops the sequence in every campaign and shows up under the right client.

  5. 05
    Log in as a client

    See what a client user or guest can view and what stays hidden.

  6. 06
    Disconnect a sender

    Check what happens to its queued messages and to its conversations.

Happily, none of these checks needs your full lead list, so you can finish them before a client campaign goes live. If a client would rather run outreach on their own, our list of the best LinkedIn automation tools covers sales teams and solo sellers.

FAQs

What is the best LinkedIn automation tool for agencies?

HeyReach, Skylead and GetSales.io suit many client senders, and Expandi, Closely and Meet Alfred add branded client access. Omniscient fits your agency's own new business, with the CRM and proposals in one place.

Can an agency run LinkedIn outreach from a client's account?

LinkedIn's User Agreement says members shouldn't share their account or use someone else's, so the risk is real. Have the client connect their own account through the tool, and agree on limits in writing.

Which LinkedIn automation tools have white label for agencies?

Closely lists it on every plan. HeyReach, Expandi, Skylead and Meet Alfred list it on their agency plans, GetSales.io lists it for larger setups, and SalesRobot sells it through its partner program. Waalaxy says it doesn't offer it, and Omniscient, Dripify, Dux-Soup, La Growth Machine and Linked Helper don't list it.

How many LinkedIn accounts can an agency run at once?

HeyReach's Unlimited plan covers up to 300 senders, but LinkedIn's limits apply to each account, so pacing every sender on its own matters more than the total.

How do LinkedIn automation tools for agencies charge?

Linked Helper, SalesRobot and GetSales.io charge per LinkedIn account, with Linked Helper from $15 a month, and Dux-Soup bills agencies only for the accounts they run. HeyReach and Skylead sell agency plans from $999 a month, and Omniscient charges per seat from $19 a month, billed yearly.
