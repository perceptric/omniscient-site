13 Best Attio Alternatives in 2026

Attio is a CRM software that builds records from your email and calendar and lets you shape the data model around how your business works. If you're comparing Attio alternatives, you've probably run into one of its plan limits, or you need something it leaves to another tool, like LinkedIn outreach.

We build Omniscient, a CRM that also runs LinkedIn outreach, and we built our own Attio importer, so we've worked with Attio's records and objects up close. This list starts from the limits on Attio's own pricing page and gives you the 13 alternatives we'd shortlist in 2026, sorted by the reason you're leaving.

Prices come from each vendor's own pricing page, read on November 3, 2026. We haven't run every CRM here with a live sales team, so where we judge one, we compare what it publishes with what we learned building ours.

Our short answer, by the reason you're leaving:

  • Pick Omniscient if you prospect on LinkedIn and want each reply on a CRM record that fills itself in.
  • Pick HubSpot if you want a free CRM now and marketing tools on the same platform later.
  • Pick folk if you're a founder or an agency and your deals start in your own network.
  • Pick Pipedrive if your team plans its week around pipeline stages.
  • Pick Twenty if you like Attio's flexible objects and want open source software you can host yourself.
  • Pick Day AI if you want AI agents working from a shared memory of your team's email and meetings.
  • Pick Lightfield if you want call recording and sequences inside the CRM, under one contract.
  • Pick Clarify if you want the whole team on board without paying per seat.
  • Pick Salesflare if you want a CRM that fills itself in and sends email campaigns on its first plan.
  • Pick Copper if your team spends the whole day in Gmail.
  • Pick monday CRM if your projects already run on monday.com boards.
  • Pick Affinity if you set up Attio to track deal flow at a fund.
  • Pick Close if your team sells by phone.

What Attio does well

Attio still fits plenty of teams, so it helps to know what you'd give up. Its homepage now opens with "Welcome to agentic revenue," and it describes agents that route new leads and catch renewals before they slip. Its pricing page lists these today:

  • Email and calendar sync and automatic enrichment come with every plan, Free included.
  • Objects grow with the plan: 3 on Free, 5 on Plus, 12 on Pro and unlimited on Enterprise.
  • Free sends up to 200 emails a month and Plus up to 1,000, and Pro removes the cap.
  • Pro adds sequences, Call Intelligence, permission controls and advanced reporting.
  • Eligible startups can get up to 80% off, and the trial gives you 14 days of Pro.

Honestly, if your process doesn't fit a standard CRM and someone on your team enjoys designing objects, Attio is hard to replace. Our Omniscient vs Attio comparison goes through the two feature by feature.

Why teams look for an Attio alternative

Every reason below comes from Attio's own pricing page, help center and app directory:

  • Sequences and Call Intelligence start on Pro, at $79 per user a month billed yearly, or $99 monthly.
  • Sequences send email only, and Attio's help center lists "cold or high volume sales outreach" among the uses they aren't meant for.
  • Attio's sequences don't send on LinkedIn, so teams that prospect there add an app from its directory, such as HeyReach, which logs LinkedIn invitations and messages in Attio.
  • AI runs on credits: seat credits go from 100 per user a month on Free to 2,500 on Enterprise, and workspace credits from 250 on Free to 10,000 on Pro.
  • Another 5,000 workspace credits cost $70 or $85 a month, depending on billing.
  • Free stops at 3 seats and Plus at 10, so an eleventh seat means moving to Pro.

These matter most for a small sales team, and for anyone who opens most conversations on LinkedIn.

The 13 best Attio alternatives in 2026

01Omniscient

Omniscient is a CRM and LinkedIn outreach software that fills in your records from email, calendar and call transcripts, and sends only the LinkedIn messages your team approves. Otto, our AI agent, drafts those messages and the emails you send after a call, and he proposes field values with the quote behind each one.

The hardest call we made was deciding when the AI may write to a record without asking you. You set each field to off, suggest or auto, and then:

  • A value with no quote behind it, or one Otto is less than 60% sure of, is dropped.
  • An auto field fills itself only when it's empty and Otto is at least 85% sure, and everything else waits in Suggestions for you to accept or skip.
  • A value someone on your team typed is never changed, and a private inbox never fills a field on its own.

We made suggestions wait on purpose, because one wrong amount in a forecast costs more than an empty field. Fields filled with proof start on Pro, at $49 per seat a month billed yearly.

On the LinkedIn side, Scout finds leads from a sentence that describes who you want and scores each one out of 100. LinkedIn's invitation limit page gives no number, so each sender paces itself. At the Normal pace:

  • A new sender starts at 5 invitations a day and adds 2 a day, and each day's total lands between 80% and 100% of your limit.
  • It works inside the sender's hours, with a lunch break and random gaps of 3 to 12 minutes, and by default views the profile 1 to 4 hours before inviting.
  • It drops to half pace for 3 days when LinkedIn rate limits the account, or when a week brings 20 or more invitations and fewer than 1 in 5 are accepted.

Replies land in one Inbox with the person's CRM record beside each thread, and a reply stops that person's outreach in every campaign.

Admittedly, Omniscient is narrower than Attio in a few places. We have no mobile app and no email sequences, so if your team sells mostly by email, Attio Pro or folk Premium will suit you better. Teammates who only view or log data are free, paid seats start at $19 per seat a month billed yearly, and the trial lasts 14 days with no credit card. The CRM page and the Outbound LinkedIn page show the rest.

02HubSpot

HubSpot is a customer platform software that puts marketing, sales and service tools on one CRM. Its free CRM is "100% free with no expiration date" for up to 2 users and 1,000 contacts. It includes Breeze Assistant, which researches companies and preps you for sales calls.

  • Sales Hub Starter shows a "starts at" price of $7 per seat a month for new customers, next to $20.
  • Sequences start on Professional, according to HubSpot's own Sales Hub pricing guide.
  • Professional starts at $90 per seat a month on a yearly commitment, plus a $1,500 onboarding fee you pay once.
  • Paid plans include HubSpot Credits for AI, 500 a month on Starter and 3,000 on Professional, and extra credits cost $0.01 each.
  • Included credits reset each month and don't roll over.

HubSpot suits a team that wants a free CRM today and expects to add marketing tools later. If sequences are why you're leaving Attio, plan on Professional. Our guide to the best CRM for startups compares HubSpot with other early stage picks.

03folk

folk is an AI CRM software that tracks relationships and deals for sales and agency teams. Its browser extension, folkX, imports single people or whole lists from LinkedIn and Sales Navigator, and it also works on Gmail, WhatsApp, Instagram and X.

  • Standard costs $24 per member a month billed yearly, or $30 monthly, and Premium costs $48, or $60 monthly.
  • Email sequences, custom objects and dashboards start on Premium.
  • Standard includes 2,000 magic fields a month, which are folk's AI fields, and a Workflow Assistant limit of 1,500 a month.
  • Every plan has unlimited members, and the trial lasts 2 weeks with no credit card.

Interestingly, folk's Premium plan gets you email sequences for less than Attio's Pro seat. It suits founders and agencies whose deals start from LinkedIn conversations and their own network.

04Pipedrive

Pipedrive is a sales pipeline CRM software that helps small businesses manage leads and close deals. Its AI Sales Assistant predicts which deals you're likely to win or lose and suggests which leads to pursue.

  • Nova, its meeting intelligence, writes a brief before each call, records and transcribes it, and drafts CRM updates for you to approve.
  • The homepage says Nova comes with every plan at no extra charge.
  • The free trial lasts 14 days with no credit card.
  • Its pricing page didn't load for us on November 3, so check today's plans there.

Pipedrive suits a team that wants a clear pipeline more than a custom data model. Thankfully, its CRM updates from calls wait for your approval, which is the same choice we made for suggested values in Omniscient.

05Twenty

Twenty is an open source CRM software that gives you building blocks for your own data model and automations, in its cloud or on servers you run. Its site calls it "the #1 Open Source CRM on GitHub."

  • Pro costs $9 per user a month billed yearly, with email and calendar sync, a custom data model and about 100 AI tasks a week per member.
  • Organization costs $39 and adds custom roles with field and row permissions, plus SSO.
  • You can host the open source core yourself for free, and the cloud trial lasts 30 days.
  • Cloud workspaces include an MCP server for connecting AI assistants.

Twenty is the closest match here if you love Attio's flexible objects and want to own the code and the data. In return, expect to build more of the CRM yourself, and to look after the servers if you host it.

06Day AI

Day AI is an AI CRM software that builds a shared memory from your team's email, calendar, meetings and Slack, then runs agents on top of it. Each agent has a job and a person it reports to, like Quinn for meeting prep or Ren for renewals, and the homepage says nothing goes out without your approval.

  • Turbo is listed at $24 per agent a month, Professional at $60, Executive at $200 and Super at $800.
  • Paid agents get automated skill slots, from 2 on Turbo up to 10 on Executive and Super.
  • The Free plan lets teammates search, view and share, and a workspace with a Professional agent or higher adds colleagues at no cost.
  • Day says it charges no per seat fees and no usage based fees, and we didn't see a free trial on its pricing page.

Refreshingly, Day charges for the AI work a person gets, so colleagues who only look things up cost nothing. We made the same call in Omniscient, where teammates who only view or log data are free. Day suits a team that liked Attio's inbox sync and wants agents to take on more of the follow up. Its homepage describes no LinkedIn features, so outreach there still needs a separate tool.

07Lightfield

Lightfield is an AI CRM software that updates itself from every customer interaction, with agents that run outbound and flag deals at risk. It connects email, calendar, Slack and LinkedIn, records and transcribes your meetings, and lets you build custom objects and relationships, much like Attio.

  • Starter costs $89 per user a month, billed monthly, for up to 3 seats and 50,000 records, with a 14 day free trial.
  • Pro costs $999 a month per workspace, billed yearly, with 5 capture seats, unlimited viewer seats, workflows and sequences across channels.
  • From Pro up, agent work draws on a yearly pool of workspace credits, and unused credits don't roll over.
  • Each extra capture seat costs $200 a month, billed yearly.

Lightfield's agent moves your records, history and pipelines over for you, which helps if your Attio workspace holds years of custom data. Watch the step from Starter to Pro, though, because a fourth seat or sequences puts you on a yearly workspace contract. It fits a funded team that wants meeting recording and outbound sequences inside the CRM.

08Clarify

Clarify is an AI CRM software that calls itself "the autonomous CRM," with an agent named Rep that drafts campaigns and enrolls leads in them. It syncs with your email and calendar and records every call.

  • The Free plan costs $0 with unlimited seats, 1,000 credits a month, 20,000 records and one active campaign.
  • Starter costs $50 a month for the whole team, with 5,000 credits a month, 250,000 records and five active campaigns.
  • Clarify's own pricing example puts a meeting summary at 30 credits, and Starter credits don't roll over.

Luckily for small teams, adding a teammate never changes the bill. Watch the credits instead, since every summarized meeting draws from the same pool.

09Salesflare

Salesflare is a B2B CRM software that fills itself in from your inbox, email signatures, websites and calendar. Its homepage headline reads "The CRM that keeps itself up to date," which is about as close to our own pitch as any CRM on this list.

  • Growth costs $29 per user a month billed yearly, or $39 monthly, with LinkedIn and email sidebars, a mobile app and personalized email campaigns.
  • Pro costs $49 billed yearly, or $64 monthly, and adds workflows of several emails in a row, user permissions and custom dashboards.
  • Enterprise costs $99 billed yearly, needs at least 5 users and includes data migration done for you.
  • The free trial lasts 30 days with no credit card.

Fortunately for teams leaving Attio over sequences, Salesflare's Pro plan sends email workflows at a lower seat price than Attio Pro. Its homepage mentions AI suggestions, but the pricing page names no AI features or custom objects, so test both in the trial if your Attio setup depends on them.

10Copper

Copper is a CRM software that works inside Gmail and Google Calendar, built for service businesses that sell through relationships. A Chrome extension puts it next to your mail, so you add leads, track conversations and find files without leaving Google Workspace.

  • Basic costs $23 per seat a month billed yearly, or $29 monthly, with pipelines, task automation, contact enrichment and up to 2,500 contacts.
  • Professional costs $59 billed yearly, or $69 monthly, and adds workflow automation, bulk email and reporting for up to 15,000 contacts.
  • Business costs $99 billed yearly, or $134 monthly, and adds email series, custom reports and unlimited contacts.
  • You can start free with no credit card, though the pricing page doesn't say how long the trial lasts.

Copper's AI is light: the site names AI flags and alerts on pipelines, plus AI help writing email templates. It lists agencies, consulting firms, construction companies and financial services firms among the businesses it's built for. A team on Microsoft 365 should confirm support before a trial, since the homepage centers on Google.

11monday CRM

monday CRM is a sales CRM software built on monday.com's boards, where you shape the columns, pipelines and automations around your process. If you liked bending Attio to fit how you sell, boards give you a similar freedom, and the AI versions of its plans add agents such as an Outreach caller agent, a Lead sourcer agent or one you build yourself.

  • Basic costs $12 per seat a month billed yearly, or $18 monthly, Standard costs $17 or $25, and Pro costs $28 or $41.
  • Plans start at 3 seats, and Basic stops at 1,000 contacts and deals and 5 columns per board.
  • Mass emails and email sequences start on Pro.
  • The AI plans include AI credits without saying how many, and the free trial lasts 14 days with no credit card.

Conveniently, a team that already plans projects in monday.com can run sales on the same boards. Basic's limits are tight for anyone moving an Attio workspace, so expect to start on Standard, and a solo founder still pays for 3 seats from day one.

12Affinity

Affinity is an AI CRM software that helps private capital firms, such as venture capital and private equity funds, find deals through their network. It captures your firm's emails and meetings automatically and scores how strong each relationship is.

  • Essential costs $2,000 per user a year, billed annually, with relationship scoring and pipeline tracking.
  • Scale costs $2,300 and adds meeting notes, chat with your pipeline, the Ascend agent platform and a hosted MCP server.
  • Advanced costs $2,700 and adds enrichment from PitchBook, Dealroom and Crunchbase.
  • We didn't see a free trial on its pricing page.

If you set up Attio to track deal flow at a fund, Affinity is built for that job and surfaces the warm path to a decision maker. For a startup sales team, it's more CRM than you need.

13Close

Close is a sales CRM software that helps teams call and text their leads, with a dialer and SMS built in. Its AI assistant, Chloe, answers questions about your pipeline and your calls on every plan.

  • Solo shows $9 or $19 a month for one user, depending on billing, and Essentials shows $35 or $49 per user.
  • Growth adds workflows and the Power Dialer, and Scale adds the Predictive Dialer and live call coaching.
  • Call Assistant costs $50 a month plus $0.02 a minute.
  • The trial lasts 14 days with no credit card and includes $5 of calling and enrichment credits.

Frankly, Close is the one we'd pick for a team on the phone all day, since Omniscient has no dialer. Once you hit dial on a saved list, Close works through it on its own.

How to move from Attio to another CRM

Personally, we'd run the new CRM next to Attio for a couple of weeks before moving the team over, in this order:

  1. 01
    Connect your inbox first

    Add Gmail or Outlook and your calendar to the new CRM while Attio keeps running, so its timeline starts filling.

  2. 02
    Export what an importer might skip

    Save custom objects and lists as spreadsheets, since an importer may only read the standard records.

  3. 03
    Import the core records

    Move companies, people, deals and notes, then check that each deal kept its stage and owner.

  4. 04
    Rebuild the custom parts

    Recreate custom objects and fields in the new data model, then load the spreadsheets from step 2.

  5. 05
    Move automations last

    Rebuild workflows and sequences once the data settles, so nothing fires on a half imported record.

  6. 06
    Give LinkedIn a slow start

    A new Omniscient sender begins at 5 invitations a day and adds 2 a day, so allow a couple of weeks before you judge your first campaign.

In Omniscient, the importer reads companies, people, deals with their stages, and notes from Attio. People already in your workspace are matched by email and filled in, so a contact that came in through your inbox doesn't turn into a duplicate. People without an email address are skipped, so keep them in the spreadsheets from step 2.

Attio's custom objects stay behind, so you rebuild them in our data model. If you'd rather not run the import yourself, we migrate your companies, contacts, deals and notes for you.

FAQs

What is the best Attio alternative?

That depends on why you're leaving Attio. Omniscient fits small teams that prospect on LinkedIn and want replies on the CRM record. HubSpot fits teams that want a free CRM with room for marketing, and Twenty fits teams that want a flexible data model in open source.

Is there a cheaper alternative to Attio?

Yes, several cost less than Attio's Plus plan, which is $35 per user a month billed yearly. Twenty starts at $9 per user, monday CRM at $12 per seat with a 3 seat minimum, Omniscient at $19 per seat, Copper at $23 per seat and folk at $24 per member, all billed yearly, and HubSpot and Clarify have free plans.

Is there a free alternative to Attio?

HubSpot's free CRM covers up to 2 users and 1,000 contacts, and Clarify's free plan has unlimited seats with 1,000 credits a month. You can also host Twenty's open source core yourself for free.

Which Attio alternatives are built around AI agents?

Day AI runs agents on a shared memory of your team's email, meetings and Slack, and prices each agent instead of each seat. Lightfield updates its records from every interaction and runs outbound sequences from Pro, and Clarify's agent, Rep, drafts campaigns and enrolls leads. Omniscient's Otto fills in fields with the quote behind each value and drafts the LinkedIn messages you approve.

Does Attio have LinkedIn automation?

No, Attio's own sequences send email only, through your synced Gmail or Microsoft account. Teams that prospect on LinkedIn add an app from Attio's directory, or move to a CRM that sends on LinkedIn, like Omniscient.

Can I move my data from Attio to Omniscient?

Yes, our importer reads companies, people, deals with their stages, and notes from Attio, or we migrate them for you. Custom objects stay behind, so you recreate them in Omniscient's data model.
