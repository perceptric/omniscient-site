Questions4.0

Questions, answered

Still curious? Book a demo and we'll walk you through it.

Can I add my own record types?

Yes. Owners and admins can add custom objects, like partners or stores, give them fields and link them to companies, people or deals.

What kinds of fields can I add?

Text, number, money, dropdown, multi-select, date, person, link to another record, email, phone, website and checkbox.

Will Otto fill in my own fields?

Yes. Name the field clearly and Otto suggests values from your emails and calls, each with the quote it came from.

Can I change things later?

Yes. Rename, reorder, edit or delete any field, stage or record type you made, whenever you like.
