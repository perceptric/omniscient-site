Questions4.0

Questions, answered

Still curious? Book a demo and we'll walk you through it.

Can I add my own objects?

Yes. Owners and admins can add custom objects in Settings, give them fields, and link them to companies, people or opportunities.

Which field types are there?

Text, number, currency, select, multi-select, date, person, relation, email, phone, URL and checkbox.

Will Otto fill in my custom fields?

Yes. Give a field a clear name and Otto suggests values from your emails and calls, each with the quote it came from.

Can I change or delete fields later?

Yes. Rename, reorder, change options or delete any field you made.
