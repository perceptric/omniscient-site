Questions

Questions

Something else? hello@tryomniscient.com

Do I need a separate e-signature tool?

No. Buyers sign on the contract page. Each signer gets their own link, and you see who has signed.

Can I use my own contract templates?

Yes. Add your templates to the Library. Omniscient fills them from the deal and the approved proposal, and you can edit any part before you send it.

What happens when the buyer asks for changes?

They suggest changes on the page. You accept or skip each one, and every version is saved with who changed what.

How do renewals work?

Omniscient reads the end date, notice period and renewal terms from the signed contract and makes a renewal opportunity 90 days before the end date.

Can I bring in contracts I already signed?

Yes. Upload the PDFs. Omniscient reads the terms and dates and adds each one to the right company.
