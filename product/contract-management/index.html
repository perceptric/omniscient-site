Questions

Questions

Something else? hello@tryomniscient.com

Can I bring my PandaDoc documents?

Yes. Connect PandaDoc from New contract and every document is listed with its status and date, up to 500 at a time. Pick the ones you want. Each comes in with its status and dates, completed ones bring their signed PDF, and one that's already in Omniscient is skipped.

Which files can I upload?

PDF or Word (.docx) files up to 25 MB each, one at a time or many at once, even as a .zip. Mark each one Draft or Signed, with its signed date if you know it.

Does Omniscient write or draft contracts?

No. Otto never writes, drafts or suggests contract text, and there are no ready-made templates. Your contracts are the files you upload or import, a blank document you write, or your team's own templates. Otto only reads a contract to find where people sign and to suggest its dates and terms, each with its quote, for a person to confirm.

Can I edit the contract in Omniscient?

Yes, while it's a draft. A Word file opens in an editor with undo, styles, bold, italic, underline, lists, alignment and links, and the + adds text, a table, an image, a page break or a signature block. A PDF takes text boxes. Your edits become the next version, and your upload stays version 1.

How does Omniscient find where people sign?

It reads the tags other tools put in contracts: DocuSign-style tags like \s1\, Dropbox Sign and Adobe Sign tags, and {{sig:1}}. Without tags, it reads the signature blocks, such as "By:", "Name:" and "Date:" lines, in one column or several. You accept each field before you send.

How does sending work?

Three steps: Recipients, Edit document, then Review and send. Send to up to ten signers, in order or all at once, and add CC for anyone who only needs the signed copy. Reminders go out on days 2 and 5 and links expire after 30 days unless you change it.

Do signing emails come from my address?

Yes, when your Gmail, Outlook or other mailbox is connected and allowed to send. The email sits in your Sent folder and a reply lands in the same thread. Without one, it goes as "Your name via Omniscient" with replies coming to you.

What happens to the deal?

Sending moves the opportunity to your Contract sent stage, and signing moves it to Won with the contract's value and the signed date. Each step shows on the deal's timeline. 90 days before the renewal notice date, a renewal opportunity is created. Each of these can be turned off.

What does Otto read?

Parties, effective date, term, end date, auto-renewal, notice period, renewal notice date, value, payment terms, termination rights and key obligations, each with a quote and a page from the file. A suggestion whose quote isn't in the file is dropped, and nothing is saved until a person confirms it.
