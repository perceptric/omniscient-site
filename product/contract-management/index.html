Questions

Questions

Something else? hello@tryomniscient.com

Which files can I upload?

PDF or Word (.docx) files up to 25 MB each. Upload one, or drop in many at once, even as a .zip. Each upload to a contract becomes a new version, and earlier versions stay as they were. Contracts you signed before come in as signed, and Otto reads each one.

Does Omniscient write or draft contracts?

No. Omniscient has no contract templates and never writes, drafts or suggests contract text. Your contracts are the files you upload. Omniscient reads them only to find where people sign, and Otto reads them to suggest dates and terms, each with its quote, for a person to confirm.

How does Omniscient find where people sign?

It reads the tags other tools put in contracts: DocuSign-style tags like \s1\, Dropbox Sign and Adobe Sign tags, and {{sig:1}}. Without tags, it reads the signature blocks, such as "By:", "Name:" and "Date:" lines, in one column or several. Scanned PDFs are read too. Signers are filled in from the opportunity's people and you, and you review each field before you send.

How does signing work?

Send the approved version to up to ten signers, in order or all at once. Each signer opens a private link, confirms their name and email, agrees to sign electronically and types or draws a signature. Reminders go out on day 2 and day 5, and links expire after 30 days unless you change that. When everyone has signed, you get a signed PDF with a certificate page and an audit trail.

What if the other side wants changes?

They can comment on their link or send back a changed file. A Word file's tracked changes show with who made each one and when. The new file becomes the next version and goes back to review. When you send a version back, they see which sections were added, taken out or changed.

Who can see a contract?

Everyone in the workspace, unless you change it. A contract can be private to its owner and the people you add, or shared with a group like Legal or Finance. Workspace owners and admins see every contract. Signing and review links are stored only as hashes, and you can ask signers for a one-time code by email first.

What does Otto read?

Parties, effective date, term, end date, auto-renewal, notice period, renewal notice date, value, payment terms, termination rights and key obligations, each with a quote and a page from the file. A suggestion whose quote isn't in the file is dropped, and nothing is saved until a person confirms it.
