Questions

Questions

Something else? hello@tryomniscient.com

Does anything send before we approve it?

No. Every email in a workflow starts unapproved, and Publish waits until someone on your team approves each one. Changing an approved email's words asks for approval again.

Which mailbox sends the emails?

The record owner's own mailbox: the opportunity's owner, or else the workflow's owner. Gmail, Microsoft 365 and any mailbox connected over IMAP and SMTP can send.

What stops a run?

A reply from the person stops their runs in every workflow. A bounce stops the run, and an unsubscribe stops every run for that address. Workflows skip a bounced or unsubscribed address from then on, and you can stop a run or take a person out by hand.

How fast does it send?

Only in the owner's working hours and days, with a random 2 to 6 minute gap between emails. Each mailbox has a daily cap, 100 by default and 50 for a free Gmail address, and a newly connected mailbox starts at 20 a day and adds 10 a day.

Can Otto write the emails?

When you ask. Otto drafts or rewrites an email step and it's marked as his. It still needs a person's approval before the workflow can go live.

Can someone go through a workflow twice?

You choose: once, or again after a gap once their last run has ended. A person is never in the same workflow twice at once.

Can a workflow ask for approval on a contract?

Yes. Start from Contract version uploaded or Contract sent back with changes, and add the Ask for approval step. Pick the approvers, in order or all at once, and a reminder after a set number of days. Only if and Branch can test the contract's value, status, end date, version number, whether it's on the client's paper and whether changes were sent back. A contract over $25,000 can ask Finance, for example.
