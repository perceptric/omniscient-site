Questions

Questions

Something else? hello@tryomniscient.com

Does Omniscient send LinkedIn messages on its own?

No. Omniscient writes each message, and nothing is sent until someone on your team approves it.

Is it safe for my LinkedIn account?

Sending stays within daily limits for invites and messages, only during your working hours. You can lower the limits in the campaign's settings.

Where do the leads come from?

Scout searches people and companies by role, company, size and location, and finds people who engaged with posts on your topic.

What happens when a lead replies?

The sequence stops for that lead, and the reply goes to your inbox with a drafted answer.

Can I use my own lists?

Yes. Add people from your CRM or from a saved Scout list to any campaign.
