Questions

Questions

Something else? hello@tryomniscient.com

Do I have to leave my current CRM?

No. Connect your email and calendar and run both side by side. When you are ready, we migrate your companies, contacts, deals and notes for you.

How does migration work?

Send us an export from your CRM or a spreadsheet. We map your fields to Omniscient, run a test import and check it with you, then move everything over. We support HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive and Attio.

Does a bot join my calls?

Only if you want one. Omniscient reads the transcripts Google Meet, Zoom and Teams already create. The call recording add-on sends a bot when you would rather Omniscient record the call.

Who can see my emails?

You choose per inbox: share everything, share only that a conversation happened, or keep it private.

What if Omniscient gets a fact wrong?

Every fact shows the quote it came from. Deal updates wait for you to accept or skip them, and values your team typed are never changed.

Do you train AI models on my data?

No. Your emails and calls are never used to train models.
