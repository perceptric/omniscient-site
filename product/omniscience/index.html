Questions4.0

Questions, answered

Still curious? Book a demo and we'll walk you through it.

What does Omniscience do?

It saves every email, meeting, call and note on the right customer record, so you and Otto can look anything up later.

What does it connect to?

Gmail, Outlook, Google Calendar, Microsoft calendar, Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, Slack, and imports from HubSpot, Salesforce, Attio or Pipedrive.

Can I keep some emails private?

Yes. You choose which emails your team can see. Private ones stay visible only to you.

What if Otto does not know the answer?

Otto only answers from what is on the record, and he shows the quote. If the record does not say, he tells you.
