Questions4.0

Questions, answered

Still curious? Book a demo and we'll walk you through it.

Where do the leads come from?

From a data provider with millions of people and companies. Each result shows why it matches what you asked for.

Does Otto add leads by himself?

No. Otto shows you the list, and you pick who to save. Nothing goes into your CRM without your click.

Does it use LinkedIn?

No. Otto never reads LinkedIn in the background. To save someone from LinkedIn, use the Chrome extension while you view their profile.

How many leads can I find?

Lead searches use credits from your plan. Pro and Business plans include more each month.
