Questions4.0

Questions, answered

Still curious? Book a demo and we'll walk you through it.

Does it send messages for me?

No. It puts your team's template in the message box. You read it, change it and send it yourself.

Does it browse LinkedIn for me?

Never. It only works on the page you have open, when you click. It never scrolls, visits profiles or clicks for you.

How many people can I save at once?

Up to 10 per click from search results, a company's people, a post's comments or a reactions list.

Which browsers does it work in?

Chrome and other Chromium browsers, like Edge, Arc and Brave.
