Questions4.0

Questions, answered

Still curious? Book a demo and we'll walk you through it.

Which meeting apps does it work with?

Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams. Connect them once, and calls on your calendar get notes from then on.

Do I have to approve every update?

Yes. Otto shows each change with the quote behind it, and nothing changes until you accept.

Who can see the notes?

Notes live on the customer record, so teammates who can see that customer can see its notes.

Does the brief work for people I haven't met?

Yes. For new contacts, the brief draws on their company, past emails and anything you asked Otto to research.
