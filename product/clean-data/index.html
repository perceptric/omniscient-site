Questions4.0

Questions, answered

Still curious? Book a demo and we'll walk you through it.

How does merging work?

Omniscient matches records by website, email and name. It keeps everything from both records, and you can undo the merge.

Where do the found emails come from?

From our lead data provider. Each found email is tagged, and you choose which people to save.

Does it read LinkedIn in the background?

No, never. The Chrome extension only saves a profile when you click.

Does it clean data I import?

Yes. Imports from HubSpot, Salesforce, Attio, Pipedrive or a CSV file go through the same merge and fill.
