Questions

Questions

Something else? hello@tryomniscient.com

What can Otto build?

A proposal or a small website for one deal, with its own pages, header, phone menu and footer. You say what you need in one line, like "Proposal for Acme: SEO and LinkedIn, 12 months", and Otto plans the pages before he writes them.

Where do the facts come from?

Facts about the buyer come from the opportunity, its company, your calls and the buyer's website. Your services, team, prices and results come only from your Library or your answers. Anything Otto doesn't know is left as a highlighted [placeholder] and listed for you to fill. He never invents prices, customers or results.

Are there templates?

No. There are no templates or ready-made sections. Otto writes every page for the deal in front of him, and you can also start from an empty page in your brand.

How does it match our brand?

Otto reads your website's home page, a few more pages and its stylesheets, and copies your fonts, colors, buttons and spacing. A brand guide in your Library wins over the scan. Each site keeps its look even after you redesign your website.

Can I edit what Otto wrote?

Yes. Every page is made of real elements you can pick, move, type into and style for desktop, tablet and phone. Ask Otto, docked in the builder, to change the element you picked or the whole page.

How do buyers respond?

They can approve the proposal, fill in a form or send you a message on the page, and it reaches you by email or Slack. Then send the contract from Contracts, with the signers filled in from the opportunity.
