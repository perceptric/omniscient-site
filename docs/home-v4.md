# Home page v4

Built Nov 2026. Replaces home v3 (folk's layout). The first plan copied Chatwoot's sections; the page as built takes
the hero and the four-tools scene from Arrowsterr, the part blocks from folk and the context layer idea from Attio.
Only layouts are borrowed. Words, screens and drawings are Omniscient's own.

## Order
1. **Hero.** "The Agentic CRM for ___ Brands", with the word turning every 2.2s (Ambitious, Modern, Fast-Growing,
   Founder-Led, B2B). One line, Start free trial and Book a demo, "14 days free. No credit card required." Under it a
   live Scout search types a sentence and the leads pop in with their photos and scores. Then the tools it works
   with, as a scrolling row.
2. **The four tools.** "Instead of four tools that never talk to each other..." over a drawing of four islands
   (Prospecting, Legacy CRM, Call & meeting intelligence, Contract management) with broken links. Then "...run your
   entire GTM motion on one connected platform" over the same four on one base (Scout, CRM, Call notes, Contracts),
   wired to Omniscient in the middle. Both are SVG drawn by `assets/home4.js`.
3. **Platform.** "Agents do the busywork. You close the deal." A stage menu (Find customers, Unify conversations,
   Close deals, Sign and renew) sticks under the header and follows the part on screen.
4. **Four parts**, after folk: a square tag with a line across, a big heading, one line, then one wide screen on a
   soft textured panel with step cards beside it that play from Working to Done in view.
   - Prospect: "Find your next customers by typing". The lead list uses famous founders with real photos.
   - Unify: "Unify all conversations". One person's record fed by Gmail, Meet and LinkedIn.
   - Close: "Close deals with every field filled in". The board and a field update with its quote.
   - Sign and renew: "Contracts signed, then renewed on time". Signers, terms with quote and page, the three alerts.
5. **Omniscience**, the context layer: a dark full-width band with a starfield, the word Omniscience in big type, a
   glowing core with eight sources on two orbits sending sparks into it, the three readers (Agents, Your team, Ask
   Omniscient) and five short points.
6. **Closing**: "Your entire GTM motion. One connected platform." with the two buttons and three security points.

Pricing and questions moved to `/pricing/`, security to `/security/`. Every link to `/#pricing` and `/#security`
now points there.

## Motion
Scroll progress line, hero glow and sheen, the typing search, the logo row, scores counting up, step cards, panels
lifting on hover, the orbit and the sky. The sky and orbit only run while the band is on screen. With reduced
motion, nothing moves and every part shows its last frame.

## Photos
The founders' photos (Steve Jobs, Walt Disney, Henry Ford, Coco Chanel, Thomas Edison, Andrew Carnegie) come from
Wikimedia Commons, credited under the list: Steve Jobs by Matthew Yohe, CC BY-SA 3.0; the others public domain
(check each file's page before launch). Using famous people in an ad can raise publicity-rights questions, which
the estates of some of them still hold. Have someone check before paid campaigns use this page.

## Running cost
$0 a month at Beta (50 reps) and Growth (500 reps): static pages on the same Fly hosting, with Google Fonts.
