Questions, answered

Still curious? Book a demo and we'll walk you through it.

Do I have to leave my current CRM?

No. Connect your inbox and calendar and run Omniscient next to it. When you are ready, we move your companies, people, deals and notes over from HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive or Attio for you.

Who gets a Free seat?

Teammates who only view records or log their own email and meetings. Free seats come with a workspace that has at least one Pro or Business seat.

Is the AI really included?

Yes. Every paid seat includes Otto. Fast answers have no limit, and Pro and Business add a monthly pool of smart and "Think harder" answers shared across the workspace. When a pool runs out, fast answers keep working until the next month.

Does a bot join my calls?

Only if you want one. Omniscient reads the transcripts Google Meet, Zoom and Teams already create. The call recording add-on sends a notetaker when you would rather Omniscient record the call.

Can Omniscient send follow-up emails for me?

Otto writes drafts that you send from your own inbox. Workflows can also send follow-ups from your team's own templates, from each owner's mailbox, and a reply stops the run.

What if Otto gets something wrong?

Every value Otto suggests shows the quote it came from, and it waits for you to accept or skip it. Values a person typed are never changed, and you can undo any change.

Do you offer discounts for bigger teams?

Yes. Teams of 21 to 50 seats get 10% off, and 51 to 100 seats get 20% off. For more than 100 seats, talk to us.
