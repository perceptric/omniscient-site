Questions

Questions

Something else? hello@tryomniscient.com

Do I have to leave my current CRM?

No. Connect your email and calendar and run both side by side. When you are ready, we migrate your companies, contacts, deals and notes from HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive or Attio for you.

Do I need a separate e-signature tool?

No. Bring your PandaDoc documents with their status and signed copies, or upload your own. Omniscient finds where each person signs and sends each signer a private link, with reminders on day 2 and day 5. You see who has signed and who is next, and the signed PDF ends with a certificate and the audit trail.

Can Omniscient send follow-up emails for me?

Yes, with Automation. Build a workflow, approve each email, then Publish. Emails go from the owner's own mailbox in working hours, and a reply stops the run.

Does a bot join my calls?

Only if you want one. Omniscient reads the transcripts Google Meet, Zoom and Teams already create. The call recording add-on sends a bot when you would rather Omniscient record the call.

What happens when the AI gets something wrong?

Every value Omniscient writes shows its quote and its source. Fields update on their own only when the field's rule allows it. Everything else waits for you to accept or skip, and values a person typed are never changed.

Who can see my emails?

You choose per inbox: share everything, share only that a conversation happened, or keep it private. The rule is enforced in the database, so a teammate cannot work around it.
