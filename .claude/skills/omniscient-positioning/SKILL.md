# Omniscient positioning and facts

## The one-paragraph story

Omniscient is an agentic CRM for B2B teams. Like other AI CRMs, it syncs leads, email, meetings and call intelligence. What makes it different is Omniscience, the brain underneath: it joins every email, call, meeting, note and record into one context layer that AI agents can query. Ask it anything and it answers, with the quote and date behind each fact. Otto, the agent built into Omniscient, answers through skills: fixed steps for a job, so answers are accurate and consistent, built from your own records.

Short tagline options: "This is The Agentic CRM." "Ask your CRM anything." "Every customer conversation, remembered."

## Facts you may state

- **Omniscience:** every email, meeting, call and note lands on the right company, person and deal. Your team and your agents read from one shared memory.
- **Ask Otto:** plain-word questions, answers with quotes and dates. Also in Slack and by voice.
- **Skills:** ready skills (find leads, research an account, prep a meeting, wrap up a call, review the pipeline), Workspace skills that owners and admins write, Personal skills. Pick one with /, or Otto picks one. Agent Skills (SKILL.md) format.
- **Call intelligence:** notes, action items and transcripts from Zoom, Google Meet and Teams. Otto suggests stage, amount and next step changes with the quote behind each. Nothing changes until you accept.
- **Find leads:** describe the buyer and Otto builds a lead list with work emails, finds people at a company, or finds lookalike companies. Job changes are tracked. Leads save only on your click.
- **Clean data:** duplicates merge with full history, emails and company details fill in, undo any of it.
- **Email sync:** Gmail and Outlook. Replies waiting on you rise to the top. Otto drafts, you send from your own inbox.
- **Workflows:** triggers (stage change, new record, meeting booked, email, schedule, webhook) and steps (email from the team's own templates, wait, branch, field update, Slack alert).
- **Knowledge:** pricing, playbook, product facts and tone that Otto uses in answers and drafts.
- **Data model:** custom objects and fields (text, number, currency, select, multi-select, date, person, relation, email, phone, URL, checkbox).
- **Ecosystem:** one-click connections, imports from HubSpot, Salesforce, Attio and Pipedrive, MCP server for Claude, ChatGPT and Cursor, REST API with webhooks, Chrome extension that saves the profile you are on, on your click only.

## Pricing (state exactly)

14-day free trial of Pro, no credit card. Billed yearly (monthly costs 20% more): Free $0 for teammates who only view or log data; Starter $19 per seat a month; Pro $49; Business $99. Free viewer seats on Pro and Business. Paid seats include the CRM's AI. Call recording with a bot is a $25 per seat add-on. 10% off from 21 seats.

## Honest trade-offs you may use

No phone dialer (Close fits phone teams). No bulk marketing email (HubSpot fits). The best parts start on Pro. Newer product with a shorter track record.

## Never say

Deal Room, proposals, the Library, Experiences, Contract Management, contracts, e-signatures, renewal-notice alerts, Scout, tasks. Otto never writes contract text. Omniscient never automates LinkedIn. Never claim Omniscient lacks lead finding.
