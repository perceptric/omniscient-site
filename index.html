FAQ

Questions, answered

Still curious? Book a demo and we'll walk you through it.

What makes Omniscient an agentic CRM?

Otto, the built-in agent, does the busy work. He finds leads, writes call notes, drafts follow-ups and keeps fields up to date. You stay in charge of what gets saved and sent.

Does Otto change my records on his own?

No. Otto suggests each change with the quote it came from, and you accept or skip it. Every field shows whether you, Otto or a sync set it.

How long does setup take?

Most teams are running the same afternoon. Connecting Gmail, Outlook, your calendar, Slack or HubSpot is one click each. No API keys.

Can I bring my data from HubSpot or Salesforce?

Yes. Import from your old CRM or a CSV file. Omniscient finds duplicates and merges them, and you can undo any merge.

Does Omniscient send messages on LinkedIn?

Never. The Chrome extension only saves the profile you are looking at when you click. It does not message, scroll or browse for you.

How does billing work?

You pay per seat. Yearly billing costs about 20% less than monthly. Teammates who only need email and meetings captured can use Free seats once you have one Pro or Business seat.

Can I delete or export my data?

Yes. Everything you create can be deleted, and any list exports to CSV.
