How we protect your data

1. It reads. You decide what goes out.

Omniscient reads your email and calendar to keep records current. It sends an email only when you click Send, or when you publish a workflow whose emails you approved. It never deletes or edits anything in your inbox.

  • Drafts wait for you to send them
  • Workflow emails need your approval first
  • Your inbox is never changed

2. Every inbox gets its own rule

Each person chooses what the team sees from their inbox: everything, only that a conversation happened, or nothing at all. The rule is enforced in the database, so it holds for everyone on the team. A do-not-track list keeps people and domains out entirely.

Questions, answered

Still curious? Book a demo and we'll walk you through it.

Does Omniscient send emails on its own?

No. It sends an email only when you click Send, or when you publish a workflow whose emails you approved. It never deletes or edits anything in your inbox.

Is my data used to train AI models?

No. Your emails and calls are never used to train AI models, and Google's Limited Use rules apply to data from Google accounts.

Who on my team can see my emails?

You decide for your own inbox: share everything, share only that a conversation happened, or keep it private. The rule is enforced in the database.

What happens to my data if I leave?

Disconnect your accounts and the emails and meetings we stored are deleted. You can export any list to CSV first, and delete anything you created at any time.
