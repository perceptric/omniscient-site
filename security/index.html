How we handle your data.

Omniscient reads your email and calendar to keep your records up to date, and it holds your contracts. Here is what it does and does not do.

Omniscient reads your email and calendar. It sends an email only when you click Send or publish a workflow whose emails you approved, and never deletes or changes anything in them.
